## Design choices and why
- **Which cells to keep.** A cell is kept when its closed cube meets $K_t$, with no test on its label. This makes the discarded cells a subcomplex, and the cofaces of a retained cell retained, both exactly. So $\hat d^2=0$ and $\hat\varphi$ is an exact chain map, and every defect is a two-letter word through $Q$.
- **Labels of cells that leave the test region.** They never get one. Retained cells lie in $\mathcal B_i(t')$, and witness cells are discarded cells in $\mathcal B_i(u)$. Both have $\tilde x\in U^+\subseteq Z^+$ by buffered restriction (1), so $\pi$ applies. The full $D_i$ is never an object over $T\times S^n$. Witness cells satisfy $|s|>t>R$, so they lie in $Z$ exactly, with no $\delta$-thickening.
- **Early levels are zero.** The estimates hold only eventually. Setting $C_i=0$ below $i_0$ makes every bound in the corner's definition hold at every level, and the scalar comparison absorbs the change through an eventual isomorphism.
- **No length-$L$ band.** Every residual has the form $u\,Q\,v$ with $u,v$ single primitives. So the two radii $t'<u$ and one estimate pair $(u,u+1)$ suffice, and buffered-restriction (3) is not needed.
- **Dropped.** The orientation residual $\hat A_g\mathrm{pr}\,z-\mathrm{pr}\,z$ (HS paper lines 1092-1095) is left out, because the corner does not consume it. The isometry homotopy $U_g$ enters only through $V_g$.
- **Symmetric over $\mathbb Q$**, following l-theory A4, not $\varphi/2$ quadratic.
