## Design choices and why
- **Which cells to keep.** A cell is kept when its closed cube meets $K_t$, with no test on its label. This makes the discarded cells a subcomplex exactly, so $\hat d^2=0$, $\hat\varphi$ is an exact chain map, and every defect is a two-letter word through $Q$.
- **Labels only where needed.** Retained cells lie in $\mathcal B_i(t')$, and witness cells are discarded cells in $\mathcal B_i(u)$; both have $\tilde x\in U^+\subseteq Z^+$ by buffered restriction (1). The full $D_i$ is never an object over $T\times S^n$. Witness cells satisfy $|s|>t>R$, so they lie in $Z$ exactly.
- **Empty control space (decision 6).** The statement has no nonemptiness hypothesis; clause (2) is conditional on $T\ne\varnothing$ because standing data and the graph-type action require it. The uniform proof needs control labels defined without a base point of $T$ (see the report's NEEDS for chain-models/grid/control-labels.md).
- **Early levels are zero.** The estimates hold only eventually; $C_i=0$ below $i_0$ makes every bound in the corner's definition hold at every level, and the scalar comparison absorbs the change through an eventual isomorphism.
- **No length-$L$ band.** Every residual is $\alpha\,Q\,\beta$ with $\alpha,\beta$ single elements of $\mathcal P_i$, so two radii $t'<u$ suffice.
- **Dropped.** The orientation residual $\hat A_g\mathrm{pr}\,z-\mathrm{pr}\,z$ (HS paper lines 1092-1095), because the corner does not consume it; admissibility of $f$ (decision 3: evaluation proves $f_T\simeq\kappa$); the old clause "$C_i=0$ for $i<i_0$", not used by Theorem A.
- **Symmetric over $\mathbb Q$**, not $\varphi/2$ quadratic.
- **Open statements (decision 8).** None. The link to corner/theorem/scalar-image.md is for the name $U'$ only; if the corner splits $U'$ into its own def article, relink there.
