Lean: `HSP.Compression.pr_d_Q` (stated, proof open).

Proof plan. The missing ingredient is a face lemma for the chain model: if the entry of
`toMatrix ((chainModel A i).d r (r - 1))` at `(a', a)` is nonzero, then
`(chainModelCube A i (r - 1) a').realization ⊆ (chainModelCube A i r a).realization`.
Prove it first for the grid complex by induction on `n` (`gridComplex k (n + 1)` is a based tensor
of `gridComplex k n` and the circle complex; read the blocks with `basedComplexTensor_d_eq`; in the
circle complex the entries of `d` from `e_w` hit only `[w]` and `[w + 1]`, whose realizations lie
in the arc), then for `D_i = C(k_i) ⊗ P` using that `P` has zero differential (cells of CP^2), so
only the grid factor moves. Then `(pr d Q)_{b,a} = d_{cell b, a} · [a discarded]`: a nonzero entry
would put the retained cube of `b` inside the discarded cube of `a`, so `a` meets `K_t`. The second
identity is the transpose of the first (`Matrix.transpose_mul`).
