`HSP.forgetControl T X := HSP.push Prod.snd ⋙ HSP.forget` (diagram order: push first, as in the article's $U\circ\mathrm{pr}_*$). The enumeration of $G_i\times\{1..n_i\}$ is `HSP.groupEnum` of forget-group.md. Equip `X` with the trivial `C_p`-action (a type synonym or an explicit `MulAction` from `MulAction.trivial`-style instance; check the name in Mathlib, unverified). The max metric on `T × X` is Mathlib`s `Prod.pseudoMetricSpaceMax` default; `IsIsometricSMul` on the product with the action on the first factor needs a short instance. Corner and compression use the induced functor on exterior quotients, `HSP.extFunctor` (categories/supports/exterior-functor.md).
