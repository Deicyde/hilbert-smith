# Note: Averaged degree-one test

`HilbertSmith.Interface.averagedTest` (module
`HilbertSmith.Interface.Averaging`) proves
`HilbertSmith.Interface.AveragedTest d` with no hypotheses: for a local
action on a nonempty $W$ there are $j$, a map $a : Y \to S^d$ for the
action of the open subgroup $p^j\Zp$ (`LocalAction.restrict j`,
identified with $\Zp$ by $g \mapsto p^j g$), and a closed ball
$\overline B(z, r) \subset W$ such that $aq$ is homotopic on $W^+$ to the
pinch `LocalAction.pinch z r`. The proof is the text's averaging
argument: Mathlib's Haar probability measure on $\Zp$, the average of the
pinch over the subgroup, and normalized straight segments. It uses the
bound $1/4$ where the text has $1/2$; any bound below $1$ works.

Departure: no degree is defined, and the pinch is chosen so that degree
one becomes a homotopy. In `HilbertSmith.Interface.Pinch`, $P$ is the
radial blow-up $u \mapsto u/(1 - \lVert u\rVert)$ of the open unit ball
followed by `LocalAction.sphereEquiv`, the identification of $S^d$ with
the one-point compactification of the ambient space, so $\xi$ is the
point at infinity. `LocalAction.pinch_comp_c_homotopic` proves
$P_{z,r}c \simeq \mathrm{id}$ by moving the blow-up parameter, the centre
and the radius. In place of $\deg(aqc) = 1$, equation (5.1) is the
separate theorem `HilbertSmith.Interface.aqc_homotopic_id`,
$aqc \simeq \mathrm{id}$, the form the contradiction consumes
(`HilbertSmith.Interface.OrbitCharacterTheory.false_of_test`). The Lean
statement is therefore not the article's statement word for word.

Departure: the homotopy is free (`ContinuousMap.Homotopic`). That it
fixes $\infty$ and is constant outside $H\overline B(z, r)$ (preprint
`05-local-tests.tex`, lines 178-179) is not stated, and no later step
uses it. The ball is a closed ball of the ambient norm: after
stabilization the ambient space is $\R^n \times \R^{d-n}$ with the sup
norm, so the text's round ball becomes a product of round balls; the
proof uses no roundness.

Persistence under the passage to the open subgroup is
`HilbertSmith.Interface.LocalAction.restrict_faithful` for faithfulness
and `HilbertSmith.Interface.LocalAction.orientationPreserving_restrict`
for the degree-free predicate `LocalAction.OrientationPreserving` (see
the note on the invariant chart subset). The pinch does not change under
restriction (`LocalAction.restrict_pinch`). Nothing remains open for this
step.
