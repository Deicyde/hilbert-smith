`HSP.exteriorCutIdent Z : ExteriorQuotient X Z ⥤ SupportQuotient ⊤ Z` (one-line def: objects `fun M => ⟨M, supported_univ M⟩`, maps `Quotient.map id`). It is not definitionally the identity, because the support subcategory `𝒜_X` is a full subcategory (a subtype of objects) of `𝒜_G(X)`, so it gets its own declaration. The commutation $\bar F\circ j_Z=j_{Z'}\circ\bar F_{\mathcal B}$ is not `rfl` (both composites reduce only after `Quot.ind`), so it is its own lemma, categories/cuts/exterior-cut-naturality.md. corner/theorem/iterated-cut.md and scalar-iterated-cut.md cite this def for their $j$.
