# Note: Invariant chart subset

`HilbertSmith.Interface.chartReductionNoSC` (module
`HilbertSmith.Interface.ChartReduction`) proves `ChartReductionNoSC` from
Newman's theorem, taken as the hypothesis `HilbertSmith.NewmanInput`
(module `HilbertSmith.Inputs`): a faithful jointly continuous action of
$\Zp$ on a connected Hausdorff $n$-manifold gives a faithful
`LocalAction p n` on a connected open subset of $\R^n$. The proof follows
the text: `isClosed_interior_fixSet` (each $F_j$ is closed, by Newman's
theorem on the saturation $H_jB$), `exists_forall_fixSet_notMem_nhds`
(the point $x$), `exists_invariant_chart_subset` (the connected invariant
$O = H_jB$ inside the chart at $x$), and faithfulness on $O$ from the
classification of closed subgroups of $\Zp$
(`HilbertSmith.closed_eq_dvd_pow`). `LocalAction.ofChart` carries the
action into $\R^n$.

Departure: no second countability. The text assumes it for $M$
(preprint `05-local-tests.tex`, lines 4-6), but the proof never uses it.
The second-countable form `ChartReduction` follows by
`chartReduction_of_noSC`.

Departure: the statement records less than the article. It drops the
open subgroup $G$ and the chart, which no later step uses, and
orientation preservation, which is proved separately.

The orientation clause is proved for the degree-free predicate
`HilbertSmith.Interface.LocalAction.OrientationPreserving` (module
`HilbertSmith.Interface.Orientation`): for every $g$ and every ball
$B(x, \varepsilon) \subset W$, the local map
$v \mapsto g(x + \varepsilon v) - g(x)$ on the punctured unit ball is
homotopic in $E \setminus \{0\}$ to the local map of $0$, which is
$v \mapsto \varepsilon v$. The module docstring explains why this implies
the local-homology notion. `LocalAction.exists_orientationPreserving_restrict`
(module `HilbertSmith.Interface.OrientationFacts`) finds an open subgroup
preserving orientation on a connected $W$ from continuity of the action
at $0$ (`exists_padicSubgroup_localMap`) and the independence of the
homotopy class of a local map from the ball
(`localMap_homotopic_of_admissible`), not from the text's
preserve-or-reverse dichotomy (lines 68-72), which needs degree theory.
`HilbertSmith.Interface.orientation` bundles it with persistence under
restriction and stabilization (`orientationPreserving_restrict`,
`orientationPreserving_stabilize`).

What remains is Newman's theorem, a hypothesis of the route's final
theorem `HilbertSmith.Interface.padicExclusion_of_oaiInterface_noSC`.
`HSBridge.newmanInput` discharges `HilbertSmith.NewmanInput` from
HS_proof's Lean proof of Newman's theorem for prime order
(`HSFormal.Newman.newmanPrimeOrder`).
