HSP.DualCat.karEmb : StrictDualFunctor A (kar A), on Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Idempotents.toKaroubi` (objects ⟨M, 𝟙 M, _⟩). Strictness: `(toKaroubi M)^* = toKaroubi (M^*)` holds by `Karoubi.ext` from `(𝟙 M)^* = 𝟙 (M^*)`, which the duality category provides. Full faithfulness is Mathlib's `fullyFaithfulToKaroubi` (Remark only). Complexes in A are read in Kar A through the general functor action (l-theory/base/pairs/functor-poincare.md); cls(C,φ) for a complex in A is notation in the ultimate L-group, not a declaration here.
