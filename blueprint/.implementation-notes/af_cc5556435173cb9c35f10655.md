The enumeration $e^G_n$ is a separate one-line def, `HSP.groupEnum G n : G × Fin n ≃ Fin (Fintype.card G * n) := (Fintype.equivFin G).prodCongr (Equiv.refl _) |>.trans finProdFinEquiv`, so $\varepsilon_G$ is `Fintype.equivFin G` (noncomputable, but one fixed term). `HSP.forgetFree` (signatures/point-functors/forget-functor.md) must use the same `HSP.groupEnum`; then `Phi₁ ∘ forget = Ubar ∘ Phi` holds by `rfl` on objects and entrywise on matrices, which corner Theorem A and evaluation/component-projection-natural.md need. Earlier drafts enumerated the product directly with `Fintype.equivFin (G i × Fin n)`; either works as long as both functors share the term, but the named form is the one the paper states.
