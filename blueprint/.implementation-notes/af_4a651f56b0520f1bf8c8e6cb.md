# Note: Moving a test

`HilbertSmith.Interface.moveTest` (module
`HilbertSmith.Interface.MoveTest`) proves Proposition 5.8 along the
text's proof (preprint `05-local-tests.tex`, lines 491-517) from three
hypotheses: `OrbitNull d δD` (Lemmas 5.4 and 5.5 combined),
`CollapseInput` (Lemma 5.2, proved as
`HilbertSmith.Interface.collapseInput`), and `hpinch`: for every
nonempty open $V \subset Y \setminus \{y_\infty\}$, $aq$ is homotopic on
$W^+$ to a map constant on $q^{-1}(Y \setminus V)$. Lemma 5.7 is
`HilbertSmith.homotopyExtension`. The conclusion is $h \simeq D_s a$ with
$h(y) = h(y_\infty)$ for $y \notin V$, so $b = h(y_\infty)$.

The first paragraph of the proof supplies `hpinch`.
`HilbertSmith.Interface.pinchTransport` proves `PinchTransport d`: on a
connected $W$, any two pinches with closed balls in $W$ are homotopic on
$W^+$, along the ball paths of `HilbertSmith.Interface.exists_ballPath`
(module `HilbertSmith.Interface.Pinch`), whose closed balls stay in one
compact $K \subset W$, so the family of pinches is continuous
(`LocalAction.continuous_pinchFun`). `HilbertSmith.Interface.hpinch_of`
combines it with Lemma 5.3's pinch form (`averagedTest`) into `hpinch`:
the moved pinch has its ball in $q^{-1}(V)$ and is constant off it.

Departure: the maps $D_s$ carry no degree.
`HilbertSmith.Interface.DegreeMaps d` is a sequence of self-maps $D_k$
of $S^d$, meant to be the telescope's degree-$k$ maps, and the $s$ of
`moveTest` indexes the composite `DegreeMaps.comp s`
$= D_{s+1} \circ \cdots \circ D_2$, of intended degree $(s+1)!$;
$s = 0$, the identity, is allowed. Degrees enter only through the
interface's invariant: `OAIInvariant.mapD` (Proposition 4.8 for each
$D_k$) and `OAIInvariant.mapD_comp`.

Departure: Lemmas 5.4 and 5.5 are about rational Čech cohomology, which
neither Mathlib nor TauCeti has, so `HilbertSmith.Interface.OrbitNull`
states their composite, which needs no cohomology: for a compact
metrizable space $X$ with a continuous $\Zp$-action, orbit map
$q : X \to Y$ and closed $F \subset Y$, if $v : F \to S^d$ becomes
nullhomotopic when pulled back to $q^{-1}(F)$, then some $D_s v$ is
nullhomotopic. It holds for odd $d \geq 3$ when each $D_k$ has degree
$k$, and it splits into the two lemmas once a Čech cohomology of compact
metrizable spaces exists.

What remains: `OrbitNull` is assumed. It is the only field of
`HilbertSmith.Interface.LocalInputs` (`orbitNull`, in the odd dimensions
$2j + 3$ with the interface's degree maps), and Lemmas 5.4 (rational
orbit pullback) and 5.5 (odd-sphere nullhomotopy) are not formalized.
