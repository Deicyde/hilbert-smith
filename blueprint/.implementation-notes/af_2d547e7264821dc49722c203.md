`𝒜_Z` and vanish in `Ext` (support ideal; below `i₀` everything is zero, and morphisms are
taken modulo eventual equality). So `b̂` is a chain map `C → C^{N-*}` in `Ext`, and `Ĥ`, `Ĥ'`
are homotopies `φ̂_♮ b̂ ≃ 1`, `b̂ φ̂_♮ ≃ 1`; assemble the `HomotopyEquiv`. Zero outside
`[0, N]`: all ranks vanish there.
