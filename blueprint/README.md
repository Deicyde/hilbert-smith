# HilbertSmith

A Lean 4 formalization of the proof of the Hilbert–Smith conjecture in every
finite dimension, following the OpenAI preprint of September 23, 2026
(oai-math #304), which has not been formalized before.

- [Roadmap](roadmap/README.md) — the book: chapters, statements, and their
  dependencies.
- [Coverage](coverage/README.md) — what counts as done, and what is out of
  scope.
