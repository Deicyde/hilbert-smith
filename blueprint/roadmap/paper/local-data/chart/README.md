# The local datum and the test map

## The local datum

A local datum packages an action on an open subset of $\mathbb R^n$ with explicit constants: the ball of radius $2$ lies in the domain, every point moves by at most $D$, and points near the origin are not fixed by the whole group. A chart at the good point, rescaled and recentred, and the action of a small $H_j$ renamed $\mathbb Z_p$, give one. Faithfulness is not carried on, only the absence of fixed points near the origin.

## Averaging and the test region

Averaging the chart over the Haar measure gives an invariant map $F_0$ within $D$ of the identity.

The test region is the set where $F_0$ is small. It is compact, invariant, lies well inside the ball of radius $2$, and contains no point fixed by the whole group, so all its stabilizers lie in $H_1$.

## The pinch and the test map

A radial pinch opens a small ball onto $\mathbb R^n$ and sends the rest of the sphere to $\infty$; it is homotopic to the identity relative to $\infty$. "Degree one" is carried in this form throughout.

- [The pinch map](pinch.md)
- [The pinch is continuous](pinch-continuous.md)
- [The pinch opens the ball onto $\mathbb R^n$](pinch-homeomorph.md)
- [The pinch is homotopic to the identity](pinch-homotopy.md)

Composing the pinch with $F_0$ gives the test map. It is invariant, the preimage of every compact subset of $\mathbb R^n$ is a compact subset of the open test region, and it is homotopic to the identity relative to $\infty$.
