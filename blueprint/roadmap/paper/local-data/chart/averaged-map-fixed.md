# The averaged map at a fixed point

For a local datum, if $x\in\Omega$ and $a\cdot x=x$ for all $a\in\mathbb Z_p$, then $F_0(x)=x$.

*Proof sketch.* The integral of a constant against a probability measure is that constant.
