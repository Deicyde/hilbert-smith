# The pinch opens the ball onto $\mathbb R^n$

For $\rho>0$, $\sigma_\rho$ restricts to a homeomorphism from $B(0,\rho)$ onto $\mathbb R^n$, with inverse $w\mapsto\rho w/(1+|w|)$.

*Proof sketch.* For $w\in\mathbb R^n$, $x=\rho w/(1+|w|)$ has $|x|<\rho$ and $\rho-|x|=\rho/(1+|w|)$, so $\sigma_\rho(x)=w$. Conversely, for $|x|<\rho$, $w=x/(\rho-|x|)$ has $1+|w|=\rho/(\rho-|x|)$, so $\rho w/(1+|w|)=x$. Both maps are continuous.
