# Local data from a faithful action

This chapter turns a faithful action of $\mathbb Z_p$ on a connected $n$-manifold into the fixed data that the construction chapters consume: a compact test region in $\mathbb R^n$ on which no point is fixed by the whole group, a test map from the sphere that is homotopic to the identity, and a compact metric control space with a free isometric action of the cyclic group $C_p$. As in the introduction, an $n$-manifold is a Hausdorff space with an atlas onto open subsets of $\mathbb R^n$, with no countability assumption, and connected spaces are nonempty. Throughout, $p$ is a prime.
