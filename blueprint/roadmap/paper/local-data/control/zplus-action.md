# The action on the test region

For a local datum, the action of $\mathbb Z_p$ on $\Omega$ restricts to an action of $\mathbb Z_p$ on the test region $Z^+$: $a$ sends $z\in Z^+$ to $a\cdot z$ computed in $\Omega$.
