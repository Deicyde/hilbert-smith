# The quotient topology on the control space

The [control space](control-space.md) $T=Z^+/H_1$ carries the quotient topology of $Z^+$: a set $U\subseteq T$ is open if and only if its preimage under the quotient map $Z^+\to T$ is open in $Z^+$.
