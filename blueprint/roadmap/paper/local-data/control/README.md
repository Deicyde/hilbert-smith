# The control space and the fixed local data

## The control space

The control space is the orbit space of the test region under $H_1$, with the orbit metric: first the maximum distance along $\mathbb Z_p$-orbits, then the minimum over $H_1$. The quotient $C_p=\mathbb Z_p/H_1$, realised as $\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z$ through reduction modulo $p$, acts on it freely and isometrically, the orbit map is uniformly continuous, and distinct translates of a point stay a fixed distance apart.

## Finite quotients

For each level $\ell\ge1$ write $G_\ell=\mathbb Z/p^\ell\mathbb Z$, $q_\ell:\mathbb Z_p\to G_\ell$ for reduction modulo $p^\ell$, and $\chi_\ell:G_\ell\to C_p$ for reduction modulo $p$. Representatives of $G_\ell$ in $\{0,\dots,p^\ell-1\}$ act on the local datum almost as a group action: the defect is an element of $H_\ell$, which moves points of a compact invariant set less than any prescribed amount once $\ell$ is large, uniformly over the level's representatives.

## Fixed local data

The fixed local data add a pinch radius and buffer radii to a local datum. They are chosen once, before any level $\ell$, and every later chapter quantifies over levels only after them.
