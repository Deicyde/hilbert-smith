# The control space and the fixed local data

## The control space

The control space is the orbit space of the test region under $H_1$, with the orbit metric: first the maximum distance along $\mathbb Z_p$-orbits, then the minimum over $H_1$. The quotient $C_p=\mathbb Z_p/H_1$, realised as $\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z$ through reduction modulo $p$, acts on it freely and isometrically, the orbit map is uniformly continuous, and distinct translates of a point stay a fixed distance apart.

## Finite quotients

For each level $i\ge1$ write $G_i=\mathbb Z/p^i\mathbb Z$, $q_i:\mathbb Z_p\to G_i$ for reduction modulo $p^i$, and $\chi_i:G_i\to C_p$ for reduction modulo $p$. Representatives of $G_i$ in $\{0,\dots,p^i-1\}$ act on the local datum almost as a group action: the defect is an element of $H_i$, which moves points of a compact invariant set less than any prescribed amount once $i$ is large, uniformly over the level's representatives.

## Fixed local data

The fixed local data add a pinch radius and buffer radii to a local datum. They are chosen once, before any level $i$, and every later chapter quantifies over levels only after them.
