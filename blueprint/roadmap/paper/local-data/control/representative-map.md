# Representatives of the finite quotients

For $i\ge1$, define $r_i:\mathbb Z/p^i\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z_p$ by sending a residue class to its representative in $\{0,\dots,p^i-1\}$, viewed as a $p$-adic integer.
