# The Haar probability measure on $\mathbb Z_p$

Give $\mathbb Z_p$ the Borel $\sigma$-algebra of its topology. The *Haar probability measure* $\mu$ on $\mathbb Z_p$ is the Haar measure of the compact group $\mathbb Z_p$ normalized so that $\mu(\mathbb Z_p)=1$; it is a probability measure.
