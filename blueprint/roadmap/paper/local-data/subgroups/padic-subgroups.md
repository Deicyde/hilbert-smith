# The subgroups $H_j$

Fix a prime $p$. For $j\in\mathbb N$, let $H_j=p^j\mathbb Z_p=\{a\in\mathbb Z_p : p^j\mid a\}$, an additive subgroup of $\mathbb Z_p$.
