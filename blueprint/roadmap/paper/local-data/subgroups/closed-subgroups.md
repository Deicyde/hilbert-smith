# Closed subgroups of $\mathbb Z_p$

Every closed additive subgroup $K\neq 0$ of $\mathbb Z_p$ equals $H_j$ for some $j\in\mathbb N$.

*Proof sketch.* Integers are dense in $\mathbb Z_p$, so the closed subgroup $K$ is closed under multiplication by $\mathbb Z_p$, that is, $K$ is a nonzero ideal. Every nonzero ideal of $\mathbb Z_p$ is generated by some power $p^j$, because $\mathbb Z_p$ is a discrete valuation ring with uniformizer $p$; so $K=p^j\mathbb Z_p=H_j$.
