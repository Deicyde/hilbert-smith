# Subgroups of $\mathbb Z_p$ and a good point

## The subgroups $p^j\mathbb Z_p$

The open subgroups of $\mathbb Z_p$ are the subgroups $H_j=p^j\mathbb Z_p$, and the whole chapter is phrased through them and their fixed sets.

- [The subgroups $H_j$](padic-subgroups.md)
- [Fixed sets of the subgroups $H_j$](fixed-sets.md)

They are compact and open and shrink to $0$, and fixed sets are closed in a Hausdorff space.

- [The subgroups $H_j$ form a basis at $0$](padic-subgroups-basis.md)
- [Fixed sets are closed](fixed-sets-closed.md)

Every nonzero closed subgroup is one of them, so a proper closed subgroup lies in $H_1$; this is what later makes the cyclic action free.

- [Closed subgroups of $\mathbb Z_p$](closed-subgroups.md)
- [Proper closed subgroups lie in $p\mathbb Z_p$](proper-closed-subgroups.md)

Joint continuity and compactness give the one estimate used again and again: elements near the identity move a compact set little, measured in a chart. It is stated for any topological group, because the group step of the main theorems uses it too.

- [Uniformly small displacement near the identity](small-displacement.md)

## A good point

Newman's theorem on fixing an open set, an input stated in the appendix on [Newman's theorem](../../inputs/newman/README.md), shows that the interiors of the fixed sets are closed when every point has a fixed neighbourhood. Connectedness and faithfulness then rule that out: some point has no neighbourhood fixed by any $H_j$. Fixed sets of the $H_j$ may still have interior elsewhere; only this one point is needed.

- [Interiors of fixed sets are closed](interior-fixed-set-closed.md)
- [A point with no fixed neighbourhood](good-point.md)
