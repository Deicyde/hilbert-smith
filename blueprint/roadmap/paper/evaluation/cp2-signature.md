# Signature of CP^2

$\sigma_{\{1\}}[P,\varphi_P]=1$ in $\mathbb Z$. Here the [cells of CP^2](../chain-models/grid/cp2-cells.md) $(P,\varphi_P)$ are read in $\mathrm{Free}(\mathbb Q[\{1\}])$ through [based vector spaces over the trivial group](based-to-free.md), and in its Karoubi envelope through the [Karoubi embedding](../l-theory/base/categories/karoubi-embedding.md), so that the class lies in $L^p_4(\mathrm{Free}(\mathbb Q[\{1\}]))$, where the [integer signature](../signatures/l-four/integer-signature.md) $\sigma_{\{1\}}$ is defined.

*Proof sketch.* $H^2(P;\mathbb R)=\mathbb Re_2^*$, and the middle form is $e_2^*(\varphi_Pe_2^*)=e_2^*(e_2)=1$, i.e. $\langle1\rangle$.
