# Signature of CP^2

$\sigma_{\{1\}}[P,\varphi_P]=1$ in $\mathbb Z$.

*Proof sketch.* $H^2(P;\mathbb R)=\mathbb Re_2^*$, and the middle form is $e_2^*(\varphi_Pe_2^*)=e_2^*(e_2)=1$, i.e. $\langle1\rangle$.
