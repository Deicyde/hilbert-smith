# Theorem B

There is $\varepsilon\in\{\pm1\}$, depending only on $n$, such that for every continuous $c:T^n\to S^n$ homotopic to $\kappa$:
$$\sigma_{\rm tail}(\Delta_{\mathbf 1}\mathfrak m_c)=[(\varepsilon,\varepsilon,\dots)]\in\mathbb Z^{\mathbb N}/\mathbb Z^{(\mathbb N)}.$$

*Proof sketch.* $\mathfrak m_c=\mathfrak m_\kappa$ ([germ homotopy](germ-homotopy.md)). Then use [pinch evaluation](pinch-evaluation.md), additivity of $\sigma_{\rm tail}$ ([tail signature](../signatures/tail-signature.md)), and the [constant tail](constant-tail.md).
