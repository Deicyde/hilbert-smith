# Pinch faces

Let $Q^{(1)}_i=\mathrm{Box}_{k_i}(K)$. For $1\le m<n$, $Q^{(m+1)}_i$ is the face of $Q^{(m)}_i$ whose coordinate $n-m+1$ is the top endpoint of its arc. Each $Q^{(m)}_i\otimes P$ is a block with $n-m+1$ arc factors, labelled by torus position.
