# Constant tail

For the constant sequence $\mathbf P$, $\sigma_{\rm tail}(q_*\mathrm{cls}[\mathbf P])=[(1,1,\dots)]$.

*Proof sketch.* [Tail-signature lift](../signatures/tail/tail-signature-lift.md): $(\pi_i)_*\mathrm{cls}[\mathbf P]=\mathrm{cls}[P,\varphi_P]$ for every $i$, $\mathrm{cls}^{-1}$ exists by the [point comparison](../l-theory/comparison/free-comparison.md), and [CP^2 signature](cp2-signature.md) gives $1$.
