# The constant sequence of CP^2

Let $\mathbf 1$ be the [trivial group sequence](../categories/asymptotic/trivial-group-sequence.md). The constant functor $\mathrm{Free}(\mathbb Q[\{1\}])\to\mathcal P_{\mathbf 1}$ into the [sequence category](../categories/asymptotic/point-sequence-category.md), $M\mapsto(M,M,\dots)$, $u\mapsto(u,u,\dots)$, is a strict [duality functor](../l-theory/base/categories/duality-functor.md) with $\xi=\mathrm{id}$. The *constant sequence* $\mathbf P=(P,\varphi_P)$ is the image of the [cells of CP^2](../chain-models/grid/cp2/cp2-cells.md) under the composite of [based vector spaces over the trivial group](based-to-free.md) and the constant functor ([duality functors carry Poincaré complexes](../l-theory/base/pairs/functor-poincare.md)): the strict $4$-dimensional symmetric Poincaré complex in $\mathcal P_{\mathbf 1}$ with every coordinate $(P,\varphi_P)$.
