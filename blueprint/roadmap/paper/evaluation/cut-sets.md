# Cut sets

In $S^n=\mathbb R^n\cup\{\infty\}$, for $1\le m\le n$ put $B_m=[-s,s]^{n-m+1}\times\{s\}^{m-1}$ and let $Y_m$ be its boundary in the affine plane it spans. Put $Y_0=S^n$ and $A_m=\overline{Y_{m-1}\setminus B_m}$. Then $A_m\cup B_m=Y_{m-1}$, $A_m\cap B_m=Y_m$, $Z_0\subseteq A_1$, and $Y_n=\{y_-,y_+\}$ with $y_\pm=(\pm s,s,\dots,s)$. Over $T\times S^n$ use $T\times A_m$, $T\times B_m$, $T\times Y_m$, $T\times\{y_\pm\}$.
