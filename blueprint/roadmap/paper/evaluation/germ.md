# Germ of a labelling

For continuous $c:T^n\to S^n$, the *germ* $\mathfrak m_c\in\mathbb L_N(\mathcal B_{\mathbf 1,Z_0}(S^n))$ is the image, under the quotient functor $\mathcal A_{\mathbf 1}(S^n)\to\mathcal B_{\mathbf 1,Z_0}(S^n)$, of $\mathrm{cls}(D_i,\varphi_i)$ with labels $c$.
