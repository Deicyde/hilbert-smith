# The labelled-model class

Let $n\ge1$ and $R>0$, and let $Z_0=\{\infty\}\cup\{z\in\mathbb R^n:|z|\ge R\}$ be the exterior of the [standing data](../corner/standing-data.md). For continuous $c:\mathbb T^n\to S^n$, the *labelled-model class* (or *germ*) $\mathfrak m_c\in\mathbb L_N(\mathcal B_{\mathbf 1,Z_0}(S^n))$ is the image, under the map induced by the quotient functor $\mathcal A_{\mathbf 1}(S^n)\to\mathcal B_{\mathbf 1,Z_0}(S^n)$, of $\mathrm{cls}[D_i,\varphi_i]$ for the [labelled model](labelled-model.md) with labels $c$.
