# Cubical pinch

Put $a=1/4$, $b=1/2$, $s=R/(2\sqrt n)$, and $K=[-a,a]^n$. For $x\in T^n$ with lift $\tilde x\in[-2,2)^n$, let $\kappa(x)=\tfrac sa\,\tilde x\,\min\bigl(1,\tfrac{b-a}{b-|\tilde x|_\infty}\bigr)$ if $|\tilde x|_\infty<b$, and $\kappa(x)=\infty$ otherwise. It is continuous, maps $K$ linearly onto $[-s,s]^n$ with faces to faces, and maps $\{a\le|\tilde x|_\infty\}$ into $\{|z|_\infty\ge s\}\cup\{\infty\}$.
