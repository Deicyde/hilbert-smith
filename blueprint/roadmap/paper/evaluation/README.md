# Evaluation of the iterated cut

A continuous *labelling* $c:T^n\to S^n$ turns the [chain model](../chain-models/grid/chain-model.md) $(D_i,\varphi_i)$ into a strict Poincaré complex over $S^n$ ([labelled model](labelled-model.md)). Its class in the exterior quotient is the [germ](germ.md) $\mathfrak m_c$, and homotopic labellings have equal germs ([germ homotopy](germ-homotopy.md)). The [iterated cut](../corner/theorem/iterated-cut.md) applies $n$ [inner cuts](inner-cut.md) along the faces of a fixed target cube ([cut sets](cut-sets.md)), then a [component projection](component-projection.md). Each step is natural ([inner-cut naturality](inner-cut-natural.md), [iterated-cut naturality](iterated-cut-natural.md)). For the [cubical pinch](cubical-pinch.md) $\kappa$, the source pieces are grid boxes and their faces ([pinch faces](pinch-faces.md), [pinch labels](pinch-labels.md)). The torus structure restricts exactly to the box structures ([box restriction](box-restriction.md), [face restriction](face-restriction.md)), so each step is an [inner cut formula](inner-cut-formula.md) computation. What survives is $\pm[\mathbb{CP}^2]$ ([pinch evaluation](pinch-evaluation.md)). [Its signature is 1](cp2-signature.md), and so is the [constant tail](constant-tail.md). This gives [Theorem B](theorem-b.md) and its [test-map form](theorem-b-test-map.md), using the homotopy $f_T\simeq\kappa$ ([test map to pinch](test-map-to-pinch.md)).
