# The tail signature

Classes over the [point category](../../categories/asymptotic/point-quotient.md) $\mathcal V_{\mathbf G}$ lift to the [sequence category](../../categories/asymptotic/point-sequence-category.md) $\mathcal P_{\mathbf G}$, and the lifts agree eventually. The tail signature is the resulting sequence of integer signatures modulo finitely supported sequences. All of this rests on the open statements [Lemma U](../../l-theory/comparison/lemma-u.md), through the point comparisons, and [lifting](../../l-theory/localization/boundary/lift-to-pair.md), through the localization boundary of the point filtration.
