# The tail category

$\mathcal V=\mathcal P/\mathcal S$ is the quotient by [the finite-support filtration](finite-support-filtration.md), and $q:\mathcal P\to\mathcal V$ is the quotient duality functor.

**Remark (not formalized).** A map factors through $\mathcal S$ iff it is eventually zero, so morphisms of $\mathcal V$ are sequences modulo eventual equality.
