# Tensoring with a permutation module

Let $X$ be a finite $G$-set. $\tau_X:\mathrm{Free}(\mathbb Q[G])\to\mathrm{Free}(\mathbb Q[G])$ sends $B$ to $X\times B$ with the diagonal action, which is free because $B$ is free. It sends $u$ to $1_X\otimes u$. It commutes strictly with transposition.

## Sources

- HS paper, Section 3
- New: the orthonormal form on $\mathbb Q[X]$ is the identity matrix, so $(1\otimes u)^T=1\otimes u^T$.
