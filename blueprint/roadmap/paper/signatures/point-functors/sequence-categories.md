# The sequence category

Fix finite groups $G_i$, $i\in\mathbb N$. Let $\mathcal P=\prod_i\mathrm{Free}(\mathbb Q[G_i])$ with coordinatewise composition and duality, and let $\pi_i:\mathcal P\to\mathrm{Free}(\mathbb Q[G_i])$ be the coordinate functors. $\mathcal S\subset\mathcal P$ is the full subcategory of objects $(B_i)$ with $B_i=\varnothing$ for almost all $i$.
