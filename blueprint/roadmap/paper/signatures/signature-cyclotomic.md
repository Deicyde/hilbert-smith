# Cyclotomic lemma on $L^p_4$

Let $G$ be cyclic of order $p^a$ with $a\ge1$, generated by $g$. If $\mathrm{Sign}_G(x)(g)=0$, then $p\mid\sigma_G(x)$.

*Proof sketch.* Choose a representative and apply [form cyclotomic](form-cyclotomic.md) to $b_C$.
