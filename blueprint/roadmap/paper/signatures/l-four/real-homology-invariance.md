# Real homology is homotopy invariant

A chain homotopy equivalence $f$ of complexes in $\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathrm{Free}(\mathbb Q[G])$ induces isomorphisms $f_*$ on $H_r(-;\mathbb R)$ for every $r$.

*Proof sketch.* Base change to $\mathbb R$ is additive, so it carries the homotopy inverse and the homotopies to real ones. Homotopic maps induce the same map on homology.
