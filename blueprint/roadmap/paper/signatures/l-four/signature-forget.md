# Forgetting keeps the integer signature

$\sigma_1((U_G)_*x)=\sigma_G(x)$.

*Proof sketch.* $U_G$ does not change the cochains, $\varphi_2$ or the pairing. So $b_{U_GC}=b_C$, and the result follows from [the identity value](../forms/form-signature-identity.md).
