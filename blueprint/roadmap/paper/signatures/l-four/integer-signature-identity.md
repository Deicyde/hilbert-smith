# The integer signature is the value at the identity

For $x\in L^p_4(\mathrm{Free}(\mathbb Q[G]))$, $\sigma_G(x)=\mathrm{Sign}_G(x)(1)$ in $\mathbb R$, where $\sigma_G$ is the [integer signature](integer-signature.md) and $\mathrm{Sign}_G$ is [the equivariant signature on $L^p_4$](signature-hom.md).

*Proof sketch.* Choose a representative $x=[C,\varphi]$. Then $\mathrm{Sign}_G(x)(1)=\mathrm{sign}_{b_C}(1)$ ([signature of a complex](complex-signature.md)), which is $\dim V^+-\dim V^-=\sigma_G(x)$ by [the identity value](../forms/form-signature-identity.md).
