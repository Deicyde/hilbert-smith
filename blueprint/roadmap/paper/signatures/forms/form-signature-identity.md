# Value at the identity

$\mathrm{sign}_b(1)=\dim V^+-\dim V^-$. This integer does not depend on $G$ or $\rho$.

*Proof sketch.* The trace of the identity is the dimension. Then apply [independence](../l-four/signature-splitting-independence.md).
