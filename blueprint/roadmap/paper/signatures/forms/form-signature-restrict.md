# Restriction of the form signature

For $H\le G$ and $h\in H$, the signature of $(W,b,\rho|_H)$ at $h$ equals $\mathrm{sign}_b(h)$ computed for $G$.

*Proof sketch.* A $G$-invariant splitting is $H$-invariant. Apply [independence](../l-four/signature-splitting-independence.md) for $H$.
