# Restriction of the form signature

Let $(W,b,\rho)$ be as in [the existence lemma](../l-four/signature-splitting-exists.md): $W$ finite-dimensional, $b$ nondegenerate symmetric and $\rho$ an action of a finite group $G$ by $b$-isometries. For $H\le G$ and $h\in H$, the signature of $(W,b,\rho|_H)$ at $h$ equals $\mathrm{sign}_b(h)$ computed for $G$.

*Proof sketch.* By the existence lemma $(W,b,\rho)$ has a $G$-invariant splitting. A $G$-invariant splitting is $H$-invariant. Apply [independence](../l-four/signature-splitting-independence.md) for $H$.
