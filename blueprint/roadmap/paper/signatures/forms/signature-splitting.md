# Signature splittings

Let $W$ be a real vector space, $b$ a bilinear form on $W$, and $\rho$ an action of a monoid $G$ on $W$ by linear maps. A *signature splitting* of $(W,b,\rho)$ is a pair of subspaces $V^+,V^-$ of $W$ such that both are $\rho$-invariant, $W=V^+\oplus V^-$, $b(x,x)>0$ for every nonzero $x\in V^+$, and $b(x,x)<0$ for every nonzero $x\in V^-$.
