# Additivity

$\mathrm{sign}_{b\perp b'}=\mathrm{sign}_b+\mathrm{sign}_{b'}$ on $W\oplus W'$ with the diagonal action.

*Proof sketch.* The sum of the two splittings is a splitting. Then use [independence](../l-four/signature-splitting-independence.md) and the trace of a product map.
