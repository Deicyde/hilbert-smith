# Integer signature

$\sigma_G:L^p_4(\mathrm{Free}(\mathbb Q[G]))\to\mathbb Z$ is the additive map with $\sigma_G[C]=\dim V^+-\dim V^-$ for $b_C$. It satisfies $\sigma_G(x)=\mathrm{Sign}_G(x)(1)$.

*Proof sketch.* It is [the hom](signature-hom.md) evaluated at $1$, which is integer-valued by [the identity value](form-signature-identity.md).
