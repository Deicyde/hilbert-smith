# Lifts agree eventually

If $q_*y=q_*y'$ in $\mathbb L_4(\mathcal V)$, then $(\pi_i)_*y=(\pi_i)_*y'$ in $\mathbb L_4(\mathrm{Free}(\mathbb Q[G_i]))$ for all but finitely many $i$.

*Proof sketch.* $q_*(y-y')=0$, so $y-y'=i_*z$ with $z\in\mathbb L_4(\mathcal S)$ by [D6](../l-theory/D6.md). The coordinates of $z$ are finitely supported by [E6](../l-theory/E6.md).
