# Permutation tensor rule

Let $X$ be a finite $G$-set and $s_X$ the standard form on $\mathbb R[X]$. Then $\mathrm{sign}_{s_X\otimes b}(g)=|X^g|\,\mathrm{sign}_b(g)$ on $\mathbb R[X]\otimes W$ with the diagonal action.

*Proof sketch.* $(\mathbb R[X]\otimes V^+,\mathbb R[X]\otimes V^-)$ is a splitting, since a positive definite form tensored with a definite form is definite of the same sign (use orthonormal bases). The trace of a tensor product is the product of the traces, and the trace of a permutation matrix is its number of fixed points.
