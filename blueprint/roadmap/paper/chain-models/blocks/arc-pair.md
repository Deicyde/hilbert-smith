# Arc pair

Let $I_m$ be the arc complex: vertices $[0],\dots,[m]$, edges $e_0,\dots,e_{m-1}$, $de_w=[w+1]-[w]$, $m\ge1$. Its boundary is $C=\mathbb Q[0]\oplus\mathbb Q[m]$, with inclusion $j$. The arc data are as follows, where terms with a missing edge are omitted:
- $\varphi_I(e_w^*)=\tfrac12([w]+[w+1])$ and $\varphi_I([w]^*)=\tfrac12(e_{w-1}+e_w)$;
- $\varphi_C([0]^*)=-[0]$ and $\varphi_C([m]^*)=[m]$;
- on the relative cochains $R=(I_m/C)^{1-*}$: $b([w])=e_w^*$ for $w<m$, $b([m])=e_{m-1}^*$, $b(e_w)=[w+1]^*$ for $w\le m-2$, $b(e_{m-1})=0$;
- $K_1=K+\tfrac12Hb$ and $K_2=\tfrac12bH$, where $K([m])=-e_{m-1}$ (zero elsewhere) and $H(e_w^*)=e_w$, $H([w]^*)=0$.
