# Block cut

Let $F_j=I_m$ be an arc factor of a block $Q$ and $0<w<m$. Let $Q',Q''$ be the blocks with $F_j$ replaced by the subarcs $[0,w]$ and $[w,m]$, with inclusions $\iota',\iota''$. Then:
1. $\varphi_Q=\iota'\varphi_{Q'}\iota'^*+\iota''\varphi_{Q''}\iota''^*$;
2. on the common face ($j$-th factor $[w]$), the summands of $\varphi_{\partial Q'}$ and $\varphi_{\partial Q''}$ are negatives of each other.

*Proof sketch.* It suffices to check one arc: $\varphi_I([w]^*)=\tfrac12e_{w-1}+\tfrac12e_w$ splits between the two subarcs, and every other entry lies in one of them. The point $[w]$ is the top end of $[0,w]$ and the bottom end of $[w,m]$, so it gets $\varphi_C=+1$ in one and $-1$ in the other.
