# Group approximations

For $g\in G_i$, put $a_g=a_{g,i}=1$ if $g=0$, and $a_{g,i}=a_i[\widehat{r_i(g)}]\otimes1_P$ otherwise. Every $a_g$ is carried by $\Gamma_{\widehat{r_i(g)}}$, because $\widehat{r_i(0)}=\mathrm{id}$.
