# The track map on Euclidean space

Fix a [local datum](../../../local-data/chart/local-datum.md). For $a,b,c\in\mathbb Z_p$ and $t\in[0,1]$, the *track map* on $\mathbb R^n$ is $L_{a,b,c,t}=(1-t)\,\widehat a\widehat b+t\,\widehat c$, with $\widehat a$ the [completed map on Euclidean space](completed-map-euclid.md).
