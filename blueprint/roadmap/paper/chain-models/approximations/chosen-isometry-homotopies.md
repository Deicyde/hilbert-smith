# Chosen isometry homotopies

Fix $U^T_g\in M(C(k_i))_{n+1}$ for all $i\in\mathbb N$ and $g\in G_i$ as in [isometry homotopies](isometry-homotopies.md), and put $U_g=U^T_g\otimes\varphi_P\in M(D_i)_{N+1}$, the external product of structures in the explicit bases, as in [chain-model duality](../grid/chain-model-duality.md).
