# Chosen product homotopies

Fix $0<\rho<\rho_+$ and a family of maps $B^T_{g,h}$ of degree $+1$ of $C(k_i)$, for $i\in\mathbb N$ and $g,h\in G_i$, as in [product homotopies](product-homotopies.md) for the radius $\rho$, and put $B_{g,h}=B^T_{g,h}\otimes1_P$ ([tensoring with the identity of the cells of CP^2](tensor-one-p.md)).
