# Carriers

Let $E$ be a finite based chain complex in degrees $\ge0$, and $F$ a rational chain complex with augmentation $\varepsilon$. A *carrier* $\Gamma$ assigns to each basis element $e$ of $E$ an augmented acyclic subcomplex $\Gamma(e)\subseteq F$, with $\Gamma(e')\subseteq\Gamma(e)$ whenever $e'$ occurs in $de$. A linear map $u$ of any degree from $E$ to $F$ is *carried* if $u(e)\in\Gamma(e)$ for every $e$.
