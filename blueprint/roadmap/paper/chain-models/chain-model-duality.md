# Chain-model duality

The duality data of $D_i$ are the pair $\varphi_i=(\varphi^s)^{\otimes n}\otimes\varphi_P$ and $b_i=b^{\otimes n}\otimes b_P$, with $\varphi^s,b$ from [circle duality](circle-duality.md). Both use the identification $(A\otimes B)^{m+m'-*}\cong A^{m-*}\otimes B^{m'-*}$ given by the Koszul rule $(\alpha\otimes\beta)(x\otimes y)=(-1)^{|\beta||x|}\alpha(x)\beta(y)$, in cochain degrees.
