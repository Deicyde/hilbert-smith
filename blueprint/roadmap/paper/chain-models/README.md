# Chain models

For each index $i$ this chapter builds a finite based rational complex $D_i=C(k_i)\otimes P$ of dimension $N=n+4$, where $C(k_i)$ is a cubical grid on the torus $\mathbb T^n=(\mathbb R/4\mathbb Z)^n$ (mesh $h_i=4/k_i$) and $P$ is the cellular chains of $\mathbb{CP}^2$. Its strict symmetric Poincaré structure $\varphi_i$ is a product of circle structures, with the block signs of the [external product](../l-theory/localization/products/external-product.md). The chapter also builds chain approximations $a_g$ of the completed representatives $r_i(g)$, $g\in G_i$, and the homotopies $B_{g,h},U_g,V_g$. Their estimates are stated through sphere labels $f_T(x_b)$, buffered cells $\mathcal B_i(v)$, and [graph types](../corner/actions/graph-type.md) of the restrictions to buffered cells for the control labels. Blocks (products of circles, arcs and points) are strict Poincaré pairs with Poincaré boundary, which the evaluation chapter uses.
