# Chain models

For each index $i$ this chapter builds a finite based rational complex $D_i=C(k_i)\otimes P$ of dimension $N=n+4$, where $C(k_i)$ is a cubical grid on the torus $T^n=(\mathbb R/4\mathbb Z)^n$ (mesh $h_i=4/k_i$) and $P$ is the cellular chains of $\mathbb{CP}^2$. It also builds a strict symmetric Poincaré structure on $D_i$, chain approximations $a_g$ of the completed representatives $r_i(g)$, $g\in G_i$, and the homotopies $B_{g,h},U_g,V_g$. Their estimates are stated as [graph-type matrices](../corner/graph-type.md) on buffered cells. Blocks (products of circles and arcs) carry relative duality, which the evaluation chapter uses.
