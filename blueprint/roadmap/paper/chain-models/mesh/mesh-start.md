# Mesh start

Fix a local datum. The *mesh start* $k_0$ is the least integer $k_0\ge8$ with $3\omega(2h_0)+8h_0\le1/16$, where $h_0=4/k_0$ and $\omega$ is the [modulus of the track family](../approximations/modulus.md). Such $k_0$ exists because $\omega(t)\to0$ as $t\to0$.
