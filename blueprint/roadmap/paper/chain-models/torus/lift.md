# The lift

For $x\in\mathbb T^n$, the *lift* $\tilde x\in\mathbb R^n$ is the representative of $x$ with all coordinates in $[-2,2)$: its $j$-th coordinate is the representative in $[-2,2)$ of $x_j\in\mathbb R/4\mathbb Z$.

## Sources

- New: the torus closing of the chart cube $[-2,2]^n$.
