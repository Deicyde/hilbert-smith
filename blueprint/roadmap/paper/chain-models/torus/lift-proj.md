# The lift inverts the quotient map near the origin

For $y\in\mathbb R^n$ with $|y|<2$, $\widetilde{q(y)}=y$.

*Proof sketch.* Every coordinate of $y$ lies in $(-2,2)$, so it is the representative in $[-2,2)$ of its class.

- New: the torus closing of the chart cube $[-2,2]^n$.
