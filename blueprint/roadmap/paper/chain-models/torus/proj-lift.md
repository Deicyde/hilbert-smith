# The quotient map inverts the lift

For $x\in\mathbb T^n$, $q(\tilde x)=x$.

*Proof sketch.* Each coordinate of $\tilde x$ is a representative of $x_j$.

## Sources

- New: the torus closing of the chart cube $[-2,2]^n$.
