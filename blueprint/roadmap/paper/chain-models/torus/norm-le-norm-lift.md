# The torus norm and the Euclidean norm of the lift

For $x\in\mathbb T^n$, $d_\infty(x,0)\le|\tilde x|$, with $|\cdot|$ the Euclidean norm.

*Proof sketch.* $d_\infty(x,0)=|\tilde x|_\infty\le|\tilde x|$.

## Sources
