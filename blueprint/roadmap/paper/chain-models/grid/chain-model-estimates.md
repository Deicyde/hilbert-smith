# Chain-model estimates

There is $\epsilon_i\to0$ such that, for all $i$ and $g,h\in G_i$:
1. $\varphi_i,b_i,H_i,H'_i,a_g,B_{g,h},U_g,V_g$ have sphere propagation at most $\epsilon_i$;
2. $a_0=1$, $a_gz_i=z_i$, $\varepsilon a_g=\varepsilon$;
3. $\kappa_i=\max_c\operatorname{diam}_Sf_T(|c|)\le\epsilon_i$.

Moreover, for $0<v<v'$ there are $i_0$ and $\epsilon_i(v,v')\to0$ such that for $i\ge i_0$ every $u$ above satisfies $u\iota_v=\iota_{v'}u^{[v,v']}$, and $u^{[v,v']}$ has graph type $\chi_i(g)$, $\chi_i(g+h)$, $\chi_i(g)$ for $u=a_g,B_{g,h},V_g$ and $\chi_i(0)$ otherwise, with bound $\epsilon_i(v,v')$. A set of cells whose closed cubes miss a fixed set spans a subcomplex.

*Proof sketch.* Collect the preceding articles, take maxima of the finitely many sequences, and include $\omega_f(h_i)$.
