# Buffered cells

For a real number $v$ (in use $v>0$), the *buffered cells* $\mathcal B_i(v)$ are the cells $b$ of $D_i$, and their duals in $D_i^{N-*}$, whose [sphere label](sphere-labels.md) satisfies $s_b\in B(0,v)$, the open Euclidean ball in $\mathbb R^n\subseteq S^n$. Membership depends on $s_b$ alone.
