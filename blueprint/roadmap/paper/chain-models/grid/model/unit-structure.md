# The structure of the unit complex

The structure $1\in M(\mathbb 1)_0$ of the [unit complex](../cubical/unit-complex.md) is the identity of $\mathbb Q$ in degree $0$, an element of the [structure complex](../../../l-theory/base/complexes/structure-complex.md).
