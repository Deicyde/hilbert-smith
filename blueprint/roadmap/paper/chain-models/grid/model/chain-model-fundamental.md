# Fundamental cycle of the chain model

The *fundamental cycle* of the [chain model](chain-model.md) is $z_i=z(k_i)\otimes e_4\in(D_i)_{n+4}$, in the summand $C(k_i)_n\boxtimes P_4$, from the [fundamental chain of the grid](../cubical/grid-fundamental.md) and the [fundamental cycle of CP^2](../cp2/cp2-fundamental.md).
