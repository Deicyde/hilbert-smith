# The structure of the grid complex

The structure of the [grid complex](../cubical/cubical-torus-grid.md) is $(\cdots(\varphi^s\otimes\varphi^s)\otimes\cdots)\otimes\varphi^s\in M(C(k))_n$, the iterated [product of structures in explicit bases](../tensor/structure-products/based-str-prod.md) of the [symmetric circle structure](../poincare/circle-data/circle-phi-sym.md) $\varphi^s$, starting from the [structure of the unit complex](unit-structure.md) for $n=0$.
