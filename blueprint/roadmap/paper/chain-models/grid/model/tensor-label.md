# Labels on a tensor product of based complexes

Given labels $l_C$ on the basis of $C$ and $l_E$ on the basis of $E$, and a map $c$, the basis vector $x\otimes y$ of the summand $C_p\boxtimes E_{r-p}$ of the [tensor product of based complexes](../based-complex-tensor.md) $(C\otimes E)_r$ is labelled $c(l_C(x),l_E(y))$.
