# The cube of a chain-model cell

The basis cell $c\otimes e_{r-p}$ of $(D_i)_r=\bigoplus_pC(k_i)_p\boxtimes P_{r-p}$ of the [chain model](chain-model.md) has the [cube](../cubical/grid-cube.md) $c$, the [cube of the basis vector](../cubical/grid-cell.md) of its first factor, read off the enumeration of the [tensor product of based complexes](../based-complex-tensor.md).
