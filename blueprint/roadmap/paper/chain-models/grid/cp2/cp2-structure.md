# The structure of CP^2

The structure of the [cellular chain complex](cp2-complex.md) $P$ of $\mathbb{CP}^2$ is $\varphi_P:P^{4-*}\to P$, $e_r^*\mapsto e_{4-r}$, an element of the [structure complex](../../../l-theory/base/complexes/structure-complex.md) $M(P)_4$.
