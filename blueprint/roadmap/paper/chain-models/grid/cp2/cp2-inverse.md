# The inverse of the structure of CP^2

The chain map $b_P:P\to P^{4-*}$, $e_{4-r}\mapsto e_r^*$, from the [cellular chain complex](cp2-complex.md) of $\mathbb{CP}^2$ to its [dual complex](../../../l-theory/base/complexes/dual-complex.md); it commutes with the zero differentials.
