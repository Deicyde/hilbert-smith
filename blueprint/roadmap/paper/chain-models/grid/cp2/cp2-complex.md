# The cellular chain complex of CP^2

$P$ is the chain complex of [based vector spaces](../../../l-theory/localization/products/based-vector-spaces.md) with $P_r=\mathbb Q e_r$ for $r\in\{0,2,4\}$, zero otherwise, and zero differential.
