# The cells of CP^2 vanish in negative degrees

The cellular chain complex $P$ of [the cells of CP^2](cp2-cells.md) has $P_q=0$ for $q<0$. So $P$ may be the second factor of a [tensor product of based complexes](based-complex-tensor.md).

*Proof.* $P_q$ has a basis vector only for $q\in\{0,2,4\}$.
