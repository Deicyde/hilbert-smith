# Cells of CP^2

$P$ is the chain complex with $P_r=\mathbb Q e_r$ for $r\in\{0,2,4\}$, zero otherwise, and zero differential. Its structure is $\varphi_P:P^{4-*}\to P$, $e_r^*\mapsto e_{4-r}$, with inverse $b_P=\varphi_P^{-1}$. Since $r(4-r)$ is even, $T\varphi_P=\varphi_P$. The induced form on $P_2$ is $\langle1\rangle$. The fundamental cycle is $e_4$, and $\varepsilon e_0=1$.
