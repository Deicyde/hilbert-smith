# Grid boxes

A *grid arc* of $S_k$ is the subcomplex spanned by $[w],\dots,[w+m]$ and $e_w,\dots,e_{w+m-1}$, where $0\le m\le k-2$. Its realization is the arc $|A|$ from $4w/k$ to $4(w+m)/k$. A *grid box* is a tensor product $B=A_1\otimes\dots\otimes A_n\subseteq C(k)$ of grid arcs. It is spanned by the cubes $c$ with $|c|\subseteq|B|=\prod_j|A_j|$.
