# Based inclusion

Let $B$ be a set of cells and $S\subseteq B$. The *based inclusion* $\iota_S:\mathbb Q^S\to\mathbb Q^B$ sends the basis vector of each $s\in S$ to the basis vector of $s$ in $\mathbb Q^B$; as a matrix in $\mathbb Q^{B\times S}$ its entry at $(b,s)$ is $1$ if $b=s$ and $0$ otherwise.
