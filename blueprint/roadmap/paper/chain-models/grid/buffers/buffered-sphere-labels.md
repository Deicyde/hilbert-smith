# Buffered sphere labels

A sphere label $s\in S^n$, a point of the [round sphere](../../../corner/round-sphere.md), is *buffered* at radius $v$ if $s\in B(0,v)$, the open Euclidean ball in $\mathbb R^n\subseteq S^n$.
