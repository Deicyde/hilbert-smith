# Sphere labels of dual cells

Fix a local datum and a radius $\rho$. With $N=n+4$, the dual cell $a^*$ of $(D_i^{N-*})_r$ carries the [torus label of dual cells](../model/chain-model-dual-label.md) $x_a$ and has the *sphere label* $f_T(x_a)\in S^n$, where $f_T$ is the [torus test map](../../approximations/torus-test-map.md) of radius $\rho$ into the [round sphere](../../../corner/round-sphere.md).
