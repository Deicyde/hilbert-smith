# Chain model

$D_i=C(k_i)\otimes P$, with the tensor differential $d\otimes1+(-1)^p1\otimes d$ on $C(k_i)_p\otimes P_q$ and basis cells $c\otimes e_r$. A cell and its dual both carry the torus label $x_c$. The fundamental cycle is the abbreviation $z_i=z(k_i)\otimes e_4$.
