# Chain model

$D_i=C(k_i)\otimes P$, with basis cells $c\otimes e_r$. A cell and its dual both carry the torus label $x_c$. The fundamental cycle is the abbreviation $z_i=(-1)^{n(n-1)/2}z(k_i)\otimes e_4$.
