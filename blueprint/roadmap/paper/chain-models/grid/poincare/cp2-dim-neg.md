# The cells of CP^2 vanish in negative degrees

The complex $P$ of the [cells of CP^2](../cp2-cells.md) vanishes in negative degrees: $P_q=0$ for $q<0$.

*Proof sketch.* $P_q=\mathbb Qe_q$ only for $q\in\{0,2,4\}$.
