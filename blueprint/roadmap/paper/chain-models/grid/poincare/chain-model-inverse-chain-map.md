# The chain-model inverse is a chain map

For every $i$, with $d$ the differential of $D_i$ and of $D_i^{N-*}$ (on the latter $d_r=(-1)^rd^*$), $N=n+4$, the map $b_i$ of [chain-model duality](../chain-model-duality.md) is a chain map $D_i\to D_i^{N-*}$ of the [chain model](../chain-model.md) to its [dual complex](../../../l-theory/base/complexes/dual-complex.md).
