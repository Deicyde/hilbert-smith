# The chain-model homotopy

For every $i$, with $d$ the differential of the [chain model](../chain-model.md) $D_i$, the structure $\varphi_i$ and the map $b_i$ of [chain-model duality](../chain-model-duality.md) and the [homotopy](chain-model-h.md) $H_i$ satisfy $\varphi_ib_i-1=dH_i+H_id$ on $D_i$.
