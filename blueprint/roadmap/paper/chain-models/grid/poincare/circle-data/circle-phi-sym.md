# The symmetric circle structure

For the [circle complex](../../cubical/cubical-torus-grid.md) $S=S_k$, the element $\varphi^s=\varphi+\tfrac12\partial H\in M(S)_1$ of the [structure complex](../../../../l-theory/base/complexes/structure-complex.md), where $\varphi$ is the [circle structure](circle-phi.md), $H$ the [circle homotopy element](circle-h.md) and $\partial$ the differential of $M(S)$.

## Sources

- New: $\varphi$ is slant with the Serre diagonal; see [circle duality](../../circle-duality.md).
