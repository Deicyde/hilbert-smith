# The dual grid homotopy

On the [dual complex](../../../../l-theory/base/complexes/dual-complex.md) $C(k)^{n-*}$, the map $H'^{(n)}$ of degree $+1$ is defined by $H'^{(0)}=0$ and $H'^{(n+1)}=\Xi^{-1}(H'^{(n)}\otimes A'+1\otimes K')\Xi$, [dual tensors of graded maps](../../tensor/based-dual-tensor-hom.md) of $H'^{(n)}$ with the [degree-zero map A'](circle-a-dual.md) and of the [identity of degree zero](id-dual-deg0.md) with the [dual circle homotopy](../circle-data/circle-k-dual.md) $K'$.
