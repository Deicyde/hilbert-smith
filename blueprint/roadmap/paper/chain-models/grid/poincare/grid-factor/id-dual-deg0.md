# The identity of a dual complex as a map of degree zero

For a complex $C$ of based vector spaces and $m\in\mathbb Z$, the identity of the [dual complex](../../../../l-theory/base/complexes/dual-complex.md) $C^{m-*}$, regarded as a map of degree $0$: in degree $r$ the identity of $C_{m-r}^*$.
