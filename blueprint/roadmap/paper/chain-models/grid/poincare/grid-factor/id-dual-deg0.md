# The identity of a dual complex as a map of degree zero

For a complex $C$ of based vector spaces and $m\in\mathbb Z$, the identity of the dual complex $C^{m-*}$, regarded as a map of degree $0$: in degree $r$ the identity of the [dual](../../../../l-theory/base/categories/duality-category.md) $C_{m-r}^*$ of the [based vector space](../../../../l-theory/localization/products/based-vector-spaces.md) $C_{m-r}$.
