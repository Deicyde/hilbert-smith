# The degree-zero map A of the circle

On the [circle complex](../../cubical/circle-complex.md) $S=S_k$, $A=\varphi^sb$ is the map of degree $0$ whose component in degree $r$ is the [circle inverse](../circle-data/circle-b.md) $b_r:S_r\to S_{1-r}^*$ followed by the component $\varphi^s_{r,1-r}:S_{1-r}^*\to S_r$ of the [symmetric circle structure](../circle-data/circle-phi-sym.md).
