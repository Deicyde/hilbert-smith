# The degree-zero map A' of the dual circle

On the [dual complex](../../../../l-theory/base/complexes/dual-complex.md) $S^{1-*}$ of the circle complex $S=S_k$, $A'=b\varphi^s$ is the map of degree $0$ whose component in degree $r$ is $\varphi^s_{r,1-r}:S_{1-r}^*\to S_r$ of the [symmetric circle structure](../circle-data/circle-phi-sym.md) followed by the [circle inverse](../circle-data/circle-b.md) $b_r:S_r\to S_{1-r}^*$.
