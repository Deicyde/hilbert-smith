# The augmentation of the chain model

The augmentation $\varepsilon=\varepsilon^{\otimes n}\boxtimes\varepsilon_P:(D_i)_0\to\mathbb Q$ of the [chain model](../model/chain-model.md) is the projection onto the only summand $C(k_i)_0\boxtimes P_0$ of $(D_i)_0$ followed by the Kronecker product (tensor product of based vector spaces) of the augmentation $\varepsilon^{\otimes n}$ of the grid complex and $\varepsilon_P$ of the cells of CP^2.

- New: the explicit chain model of the cubical grid.
