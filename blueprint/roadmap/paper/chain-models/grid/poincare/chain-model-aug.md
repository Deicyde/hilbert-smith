# The augmentation of the chain model

The augmentation $\varepsilon=\varepsilon^{\otimes n}\boxtimes\varepsilon_P:(D_i)_0\to\mathbb Q$ of the [chain model](../model/chain-model.md) is the projection onto the only summand $C(k_i)_0\boxtimes P_0$ of $(D_i)_0$ followed by the Kronecker product ([tensor product of based vector spaces](../../../l-theory/localization/products/based-tensor.md)) of the augmentation $\varepsilon^{\otimes n}$ of the [grid complex](../cubical/cubical-torus-grid.md) and $\varepsilon_P$ of the [cells of CP^2](../cp2/cp2-cells.md).
