# The dual homotopy of the chain model

On the dual $D_i^{N-*}$ of the [chain model](../chain-model.md), $N=n+4$, $H'_i=\Xi^{-1}(H'^{(n)}\otimes1_{P^{4-*}})\Xi$ is the map of degree $+1$ given by the [dual tensor of graded maps](../tensor/based-dual-tensor-hom.md) of the [dual grid homotopy](grid-h-dual.md) and the [identity of degree zero](id-dual-deg0.md) of $P^{4-*}$.
