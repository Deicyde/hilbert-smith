# The homotopy of the chain model

On the [chain model](../chain-model.md) $D_i=C(k_i)\otimes P$, $H_i=H^{(n)}\otimes1_P$ is the map of degree $+1$ given by the [tensor of graded maps](../tensor/based-tensor-hom.md) of the [grid homotopy](grid-h.md) $H^{(n)}$ and the [identity of degree zero](../tensor/id-deg0.md) of $P$.
