# Sphere labels

A cell $b$ of $D_i$, or its dual in $D_i^{N-*}$, with torus label $x_b$, has the *sphere label* $s_b=f_T(x_b)\in S^n$, where $f_T$ is the [torus test map](../approximations/torus-test-map.md). The *sphere propagation* of a matrix between spans of such cells is its [propagation](../../categories/asymptotic/propagation.md) for the labels $s_b$ and the round metric $d_S$ on $S^n$ of the [standing data](../../corner/standing-data.md).
