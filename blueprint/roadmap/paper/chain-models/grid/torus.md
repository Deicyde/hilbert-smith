# The torus

Let $n\in\mathbb N$. The torus is $\mathbb T^n=(\mathbb R/4\mathbb Z)^n$, with the distance $d_\infty(x,y)=\max_jd(x_j,y_j)$, the maximum of the circle distances $d(s,t)=\min_{m\in\mathbb Z}|s'-t'+4m|$ on $\mathbb R/4\mathbb Z$, for any representatives $s',t'\in\mathbb R$ of $s,t$. For $X\subseteq\mathbb T^n$ and $r\ge0$, $N_r(X)$ is the closed $d_\infty$-neighbourhood of $X$ of radius $r$: the points whose $d_\infty$-distance from $X$ is at most $r$. On $\mathbb R^n$, $|\cdot|$ is the Euclidean norm and $|\cdot|_\infty$ the maximum norm.
