# The dual circle homotopy

For the [circle complex](../cubical-torus-grid.md) $S=S_k$, the map $K'=\tfrac12bH$ of degree $+1$ on the [dual complex](../../../l-theory/base/complexes/dual-complex.md) $S^{1-*}$: in degree $r$ it is $\tfrac12$ times the component $(S_{1-r})^*\to S_{r+1}$ of the [circle homotopy element](circle-h.md) $H$ followed by the [circle inverse](circle-b.md) $b_{r+1}:S_{r+1}\to(S_{-r})^*$.
