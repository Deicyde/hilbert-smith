# Mesh sequence

Let $k_0$ be the least integer $k_0\ge8$ with $3\omega(2h_0)+8h_0\le1/16$, where $h_0=4/k_0$ and $\omega$ is the [modulus of the track family](../approximations/modulus.md). Set $k_i=k_0+i$ and $h_i=4/k_i$. Such $k_0$ exists because $\omega(t)\to0$ as $t\to0$, and the inequality then holds for every $h_i$ because $\omega$ is nondecreasing. In particular $h_i\le1/128$.
