# Kronecker product with a zero factor

For matrices $f:X\to X'$ and $g:Y\to Y'$ of [based vector spaces](../../../l-theory/localization/products/based-vector-spaces.md), the Kronecker products of the [tensor product of based vector spaces](../../../l-theory/localization/products/based-tensor.md) satisfy $f\boxtimes g=0$ whenever $f=0$ or $g=0$.

*Proof sketch.* A Kronecker product with a zero factor has all entries zero.
