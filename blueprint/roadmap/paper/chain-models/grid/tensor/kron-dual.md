# Transpose of a Kronecker product

For matrices $f:X\to X'$ and $g:Y\to Y'$ of [based vector spaces](../../../l-theory/localization/products/based-vector-spaces.md), whose duals are the transposes, the Kronecker product of the [tensor product of based vector spaces](../../../l-theory/localization/products/based-tensor.md) satisfies $(f\boxtimes g)^*=f^*\boxtimes g^*$.

*Proof sketch.* The transpose of a Kronecker product of matrices is the Kronecker product of the transposes, and the reindexing by Kronecker positions commutes with transposition.
