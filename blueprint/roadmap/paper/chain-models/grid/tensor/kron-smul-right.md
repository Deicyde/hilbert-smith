# Signs in the second factor of a Kronecker product

For matrices $f:X\to X'$, $g:Y\to Y'$ of [based vector spaces](../../../l-theory/localization/products/based-vector-spaces.md) and a sign $u=\pm1$, the Kronecker product of the [tensor product of based vector spaces](../../../l-theory/localization/products/based-tensor.md) satisfies $f\boxtimes(ug)=u\,(f\boxtimes g)$.

*Proof sketch.* A scalar factor of the second factor of a Kronecker product of matrices comes out.
