# Transport of summand projections

Let $C$ and $E$ be complexes of based vector spaces with $E$ vanishing in negative degrees, and $s=t$. The identification $(C\otimes E)_s=(C\otimes E)_t$ of the [tensor product of based complexes](../based-complex-tensor.md) followed by the projection onto the summand $C_p\boxtimes E_{t-p}$ is the projection onto the summand $C_p\boxtimes E_{s-p}$ followed by the identification of the summands.

*Proof sketch.* Substitute $t=s$.
