# Composites through a zero space vanish

For maps $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y\to Z$ of [based vector spaces](../../../../l-theory/localization/products/based-vector-spaces.md) with $\dim Y=0$, $gf=0$.

*Proof sketch.* The matrix product is an empty sum.
