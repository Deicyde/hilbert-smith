# Based subcomplexes

Let $E$ be a chain complex of rational vector spaces with a basis in each degree. A set $S_r$ of basis elements in each degree spans a *based subcomplex* if every basis element occurring with nonzero coefficient in $de$, for $e\in S_r$, lies in $S_{r-1}$.

**Remark (not formalized).** The span of the $S_r$ is then a subcomplex of $E$.
