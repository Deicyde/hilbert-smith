# Box hull

Let $k\ge8$. Let $X\subseteq\mathbb T^n$ be nonempty, and suppose each coordinate projection $X_j$ lies in some closed arc of length at most $1$. Let $J_j$ be the shortest closed arc containing $X_j$. It is unique, because the complement of $X_j$ has exactly one gap longer than $2$. Then $\mathrm{Box}_k(X)=\bigotimes_jA_j$, where $A_j$ is the smallest grid arc with $J_j\subseteq|A_j|$.
