# Buffered restriction of a matrix

For $v>0$ let $\iota_v$ be the based inclusion of the span of the [buffered cells](buffered-cells.md) $\mathcal B_i(v)$ (in $D_i$ or in $D_i^{N-*}$) and $p_v$ the based projection onto it; these spans carry the [control labels](control-labels.md) $\lambda$. For $0<v\le v'$ and a matrix $u$ between such modules, the *restriction* is $u^{[v,v']}=p_{v'}u\,\iota_v$, and $u^{[v]}=u^{[v,v]}$.
