# The circle complex

For $k\in\mathbb N$, the *circle complex* $S_k$ is the complex of [based vector spaces](../../../l-theory/localization/products/based-vector-spaces.md) with basis $[w]$ of $S_{k,0}$ and $e_w$ of $S_{k,1}$, both indexed by $w\in\mathbb Z/k=\{0,\dots,k-1\}$ and enumerated in this order, zero in other degrees, and differential $de_w=[w+1]-[w]$.
