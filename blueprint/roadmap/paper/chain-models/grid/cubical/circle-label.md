# Labels of the circle cells

Each [cell](circle-cell.md) of the [circle complex](circle-complex.md) $S_k$ is labelled by its center in $\mathbb R/4\mathbb Z$: $[w]$ has label $4w/k$ and $e_w$ has label $(4w+2)/k$. A dual basis vector has the label of its cell.
