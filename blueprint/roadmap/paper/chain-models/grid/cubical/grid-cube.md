# Grid cubes

A *cube* of the cubical torus grid is $c=(w,S)$ with $w\in(\mathbb Z/k)^n$ and $S\subseteq\{1,\dots,n\}$, written $c=b_1\otimes\dots\otimes b_n$ with $b_j=e_{w_j}$ if $j\in S$ and $b_j=[w_j]$ otherwise, cells of the [circle complex](circle-complex.md).
