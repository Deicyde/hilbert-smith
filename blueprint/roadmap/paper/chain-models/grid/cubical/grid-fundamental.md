# Fundamental chain of the grid complex

The *fundamental chain* of the [grid complex](cubical-torus-grid.md) is $z(k)=\bigotimes_j\sum_we_w\in C(k)_n$, the tensor product of the [fundamental chains](circle-fundamental.md) of the factors: by recursion on $n$, it lies in the summand $C(k)_n\boxtimes S_{k,1}$ of $(C(k)\otimes S_k)_{n+1}$.
