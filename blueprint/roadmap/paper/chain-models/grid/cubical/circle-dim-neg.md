# The circle complex vanishes in negative degrees

The [circle complex](circle-complex.md) has $S_{k,q}=0$ for $q<0$. So $S_k$ may be the second factor of a [tensor product of based complexes](../based-complex-tensor.md).

*Proof.* $S_k$ has cells only in degrees $0$ and $1$.
