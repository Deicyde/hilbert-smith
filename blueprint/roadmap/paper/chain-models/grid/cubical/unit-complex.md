# The unit complex

The *unit complex* $\mathbb 1$ is the complex of [based vector spaces](../../../l-theory/localization/products/based-vector-spaces.md) with $\mathbb 1_0=\mathbb Q$, one basis vector, and $\mathbb 1_r=0$ for $r\ne0$, with zero differential.
