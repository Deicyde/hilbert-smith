# Realization of a circle cell

The *realization* of the cell $[w]$ of the [circle complex](circle-complex.md) $S_k$ is the point $4w/k$ of $\mathbb R/4\mathbb Z$, and the realization of $e_w$ is the image of $[4w/k,4(w+1)/k]$ in $\mathbb R/4\mathbb Z$.
