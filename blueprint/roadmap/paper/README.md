# The Hilbert–Smith conjecture in one paper

This part proves two theorems along a single route.

**Theorem (p-adic exclusion).** Let $p$ be a prime and let $M$ be a
connected Hausdorff topological manifold, possibly with boundary, with no
countability assumption. Every continuous action of $\mathbb{Z}_p$ on $M$
has nontrivial kernel.

**Corollary (Hilbert–Smith).** A locally compact group that acts
continuously and faithfully on such a manifold is a Lie group.

From a faithful action of $\mathbb{Z}_p$ the proof builds one controlled
Poincaré complex and computes the tail of its signatures twice: it is
eventually $\pm 1$, and eventually divisible by $p$. The architecture is
that of the HS paper; the good point and the orbit metric come from the
OpenAI preprint.
