# Divisibility over a free $C_p$-space

In this chapter $X$ is a compact metric space with a free isometric action of $C_p$. For each $i$, $\chi_i:G_i\to C_p$ is a surjection from a finite $p$-group, and $G_i$ acts on $X$ through $\chi_i$. The goal is the [divisibility theorem](divisibility.md): for every class of $\mathbb L_4(\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X))$, the signature tail is eventually divisible by $p$.

The induction behind it does not depend on L-theory, so we isolate it first. A [Mayer-Vietoris functor](mayer-vietoris-functor.md) assigns groups to the members of a lattice. It has a boundary for each pair, and the sequence is exact in the middle. A [natural endomorphism](natural-endomorphism.md) commutes with the inclusions and the boundaries. The [abstract nilpotence lemma](abstract-nilpotence.md) says that if a natural endomorphism vanishes below each of $s$ pieces, then its $s$-th power vanishes on their join. The [support Mayer-Vietoris functor](support-mayer-vietoris.md) sends $S\mapsto\mathbb L_k(\mathcal A_S)$ on closed invariant subsets. Its boundary is the excision boundary of the relative support pair.

The [permutation tensor](../categories/tensor-permutation.md) preserves [supports](../categories/support-preservation.md), so [$\nu=p-\tau\otimes$](permutation-nu.md) is a natural endomorphism. Over a closed invariant set that has an equivariant sheet map to $C_p$, the sheets turn $\tau\otimes$ into $p$ copies of the identity. This is [local triviality](local-triviality.md). A [trivializing closed cover](trivializing-cover.md) depends on $X$ alone, so it is fixed before any index $i$ appears. [Any such cover of length $s$ gives $\nu^s=0$](nu-nilpotent.md). Evaluating signature characters at an element outside $\ker\chi_i$ then gives divisibility.

**Remark (not formalized).** Suppose $X$ is connected and nonempty. Then $X$ carries no continuous equivariant map $c:X\to C_p$. Its fibres would be pairwise disjoint and clopen, and equivariance permutes them transitively, so all $p$ of them would be nonempty. A cover with $s=1$ has $T_1=X$, so every trivializing cover of such an $X$ has $s\ge2$. The chapter does not use this. The local-data chapter does not show that its control space $T=Z^+/H_1$ is connected, since the test region $Z^+$ is only a sublevel set of the averaged chart map.
