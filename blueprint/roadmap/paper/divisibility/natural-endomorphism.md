# Natural endomorphism

A *natural endomorphism* $\nu$ of a Mayer-Vietoris functor $F$ is a family of endomorphisms $\nu_{S,k}$ of $F_k(S)$ with $\nu\,i_{S\le S'}=i_{S\le S'}\,\nu$ and $\nu\,\partial_{A,B}=\partial_{A,B}\,\nu$.
