# Nilpotence

Let $s\ge1$, and let $T_1,\dots,T_s$ be closed invariant subsets covering $X$, each with a continuous map $c_a:T_a\to C_p$ satisfying $c_a(gx)=g+c_a(x)$. Then $\nu^s=0$ on $\mathbb L_k(\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X))$ for all $k$.

*Proof sketch.*
1. For $S\subseteq T_a$ closed and invariant, $c_a|_S$ is a continuous equivariant sheet map. Local triviality gives $\nu_S=0$.
2. Abstract nilpotence gives $\nu^s=0$ on $F_k(T_1\cup\dots\cup T_s)=\mathbb L_k(\mathcal A_X)$.
3. $\mathcal A_X=\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X)$.
