# The action on the intrinsic interior

Let a group $G$ act on a topological space $M$, each element acting by a continuous map, and let $n\in\mathbb{N}$. Then each element maps $M^\circ_n$ onto itself, and the action restricts to an action of $G$ on $M^\circ_n$.
