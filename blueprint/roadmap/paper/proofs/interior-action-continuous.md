# The restricted action is continuous

Let a topological group $G$ act jointly continuously on a topological space $M$ and let $n\in\mathbb{N}$. Then the restricted action of $G$ on $M^\circ_n$ is jointly continuous.

*Proof sketch.* Joint continuity passes to the invariant subspace $M^\circ_n$.
