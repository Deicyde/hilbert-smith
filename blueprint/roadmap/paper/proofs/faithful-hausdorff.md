# Faithful actions give Hausdorff groups

Let $Q$ be a topological group acting jointly continuously and faithfully on a $T_1$ space $X$. Then $Q$ is Hausdorff.

*Proof sketch.* Let $g\in\overline{\{1\}}$. For each $x$, continuity of $h\mapsto hx$ puts $gx$ in $\overline{\{x\}}=\{x\}$. Faithfulness gives $g=1$. So $\{1\}$ is closed, and a topological group with closed identity is Hausdorff.
