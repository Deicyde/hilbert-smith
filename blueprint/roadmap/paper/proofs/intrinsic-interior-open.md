# The intrinsic interior is open

For every topological space $M$ and $n\in\mathbb{N}$, $M^\circ_n$ is open in $M$.

*Proof sketch.* If $U$ is an open set with a homeomorphism onto an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$, every point of $U$ is intrinsically interior, so $U\subseteq M^\circ_n$.
