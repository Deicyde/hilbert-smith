# The intrinsic interior is charted

For every topological space $M$ and $n\in\mathbb{N}$, the subspace $M^\circ_n$ carries an atlas of charts onto open subsets of $\mathbb{R}^n$: for each point choose an open set $U$ around it with a homeomorphism onto an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$; then $U\subseteq M^\circ_n$, so $U$ is open in $M^\circ_n$.
