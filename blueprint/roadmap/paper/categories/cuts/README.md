# Cuts

A closed cover $A\cup B$ of a closed invariant set, with an exterior inside $A$, gives the cut square and the cut boundary. The cut boundary can be iterated along a flag through two connecting functors, the identification $j_Z$ of the exterior quotient with a cut domain and the quotient functors by $\mathcal A_\varnothing$; support-preserving functors induce functors on the quotients that commute strictly with both (with $j_Z$ by a separate naturality lemma). The boundary is natural, and the cut formula computes it on closed unions.
