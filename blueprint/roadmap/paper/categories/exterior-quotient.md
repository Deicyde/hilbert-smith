# Exterior quotient

For closed invariant $Z\subset X$, $\mathcal B_{\mathbf G,Z}(X)=\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X)/\mathcal A_Z$ is the [Karoubi quotient](../l-theory/A3.md) by the [support filtration](support-filtration.md).
