# Permutation tensor preserves support

For closed invariant $S\subset X$, the [permutation tensor](tensor-permutation.md) carries $\mathcal A_S$ into $\mathcal A_S$.

*Proof sketch.* The labels are $\ell(c,b)=\ell(b)$.
