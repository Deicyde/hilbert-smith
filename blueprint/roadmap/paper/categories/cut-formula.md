# Cut formula

Let the cut data be as in the [cut boundary](cut-boundary.md). Let $(P\to D_A)$ and $(P\to D_B)$ be $k$-dimensional strict [Poincaré pairs](../l-theory/base/poincare-pair.md) in $\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathcal Q$. Assume $D_A$ is $A$-supported, $D_B$ is $B$-supported and $P$ is $Y$-supported, where a Karoubi object $(M,e)$ has the support of $M$. Assume the common boundary $P$ is oppositely oriented and oriented as the boundary of $D_A$, and let $V$ be their [union](../l-theory/base/gluing.md). Then $\partial_{A,B}\,\mathrm{cls}[V]=\eta_k\,\mathrm{cls}[\bar P]$, where $\bar P$ is $P$ transported along [separated support](quotient-separation-iso.md).

*Proof sketch.* The [union formula](../l-theory/ultimate/union-formula.md) for the cut square gives $\eta_k\,\mathrm{cls}[P]$ in $\mathbb L_{k-1}(\mathcal U_Y)$. Then transport it along the separated-support isomorphism.
