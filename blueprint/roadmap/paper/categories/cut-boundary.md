# Cut boundary

Let $A,B,Z$ be as in the [cut square](cut-square.md), and assume also $Z\cap B=\varnothing$, so that $Y\cap Z=\varnothing$. The cut boundary $\partial_{A,B}:\mathbb L_k(\mathcal B_{\mathbf G,Z}(X))\to\mathbb L_{k-1}(\mathcal A_Y)$ is the [excision boundary](../l-theory/ultimate/excision-boundary.md) of the cut square, followed by the map induced by the inverse in [separated support](quotient-separation-iso.md) $\mathcal U_Y\to\mathcal A_Y$.
