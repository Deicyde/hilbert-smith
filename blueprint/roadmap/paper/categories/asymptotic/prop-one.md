# Propagation of the identity

For a finite set $B$ with labels $\lambda:B\to X$, the identity matrix has $\operatorname{prop}(1)=0$, with the labels $\lambda$ on its source and its target.

*Proof sketch.* The nonzero entries of $1$ are its diagonal entries, which join each label to itself.
