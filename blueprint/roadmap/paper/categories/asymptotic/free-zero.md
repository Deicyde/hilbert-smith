# Objects of rank zero are zero

In the [free category](free-category.md) $\mathrm{Free}(\mathbb Q[G])$, the object $0$ is a zero object.

*Proof sketch.* Its basis $G\times\varnothing$ is empty, so every matrix to or from it is the empty matrix.
