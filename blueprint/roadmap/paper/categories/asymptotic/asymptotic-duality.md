# Duality on the asymptotic category

$\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X)$ is a [strict duality category](../../l-theory/base/categories/duality-category.md) under $M^*=M$ and $[u_i]^*=[u_i^{T}]$.

*Proof sketch.* Transposition keeps equivariance and propagation ([propagation of a transpose](prop-transpose.md)), reverses composition, is $\mathbb Q$-linear and is an involution on the nose.
