# Free category of a finite group

For a finite group $G$, $\mathrm{Free}(G)$ has objects $n\in\mathbb N$. Its morphisms $n\to m$ are the $G$-equivariant matrices in $\mathbb Q^{(G\times\{1..m\})\times(G\times\{1..n\})}$, composed by matrix product. It is a strict duality category under $n^*=n$ and $u^*=u^T$.
