# Propagation

Let $X$ be a metric space. For finite sets $B,B'$ with labels $\ell:B\to X$, $\ell':B'\to X$ and a rational matrix $u\in\mathbb Q^{B'\times B}$, put $\operatorname{prop}(u)=\max\{d(\ell'(b'),\ell(b)) : u_{b'b}\neq0\}$, which is $0$ when $u=0$.
