# Enumeration of a sub-basis

Let $M=(n_i,x_i)$ be an object of the [asymptotic category](../asymptotic-category.md) and $P$ a predicate on its indices $(i,k)$. At each level $i$, the enumeration $e_i:\{1,\dots,m_i\}\to\{1,\dots,n_i\}$ lists the indices $k$ with $P(i,k)$ in increasing order; $m_i$ is their number. It is a strictly increasing map.
