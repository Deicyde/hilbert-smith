# The quotient functor of the point category

Let $\mathbf G$ be a [group sequence](group-sequence.md). The quotient functor $q:\mathcal P_{\mathbf G}\to\mathcal V_{\mathbf G}$ from the [sequence category](point-sequence-category.md) to the [point category](point-quotient.md) is the quotient functor of the [quotient by a subcategory](../../l-theory/base/categories/filtration-quotient.md), for the [finite-support subcategory](eventually-zero-subcategory.md). It is a [duality functor](../../l-theory/base/categories/duality-functor.md) with $\xi=\mathrm{id}$, so it is strict.
