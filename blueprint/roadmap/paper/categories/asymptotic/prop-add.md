# Propagation of a sum

For labelled matrices $u,v$ of the same shape, $\operatorname{prop}(u+v)\le\max(\operatorname{prop}u,\operatorname{prop}v)$.

*Proof sketch.* A nonzero entry of $u+v$ is a nonzero entry of $u$ or of $v$.
