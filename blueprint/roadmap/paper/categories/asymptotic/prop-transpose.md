# Propagation of a transpose

For a labelled matrix $u\in\mathbb Q^{B'\times B}$, $\operatorname{prop}(u^{T})=\operatorname{prop}(u)$, where $u^T$ takes the labels of $B'$ on its source and those of $B$ on its target.

*Proof sketch.* The entries are the same, and the metric is symmetric.
