# Propagation of a scalar multiple

For $c\in\mathbb Q$ and a labelled matrix $u$, $\operatorname{prop}(cu)\le\operatorname{prop}(u)$.

*Proof sketch.* Every nonzero entry of $cu$ is a nonzero entry of $u$.
