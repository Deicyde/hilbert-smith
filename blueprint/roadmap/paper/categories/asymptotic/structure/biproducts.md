# The asymptotic category has finite biproducts

$\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X)$ has a zero object, the [empty sequence](empty-object-zero.md), and binary biproducts, the [concatenation](concatenation-bilimit.md); hence it has finite biproducts.
