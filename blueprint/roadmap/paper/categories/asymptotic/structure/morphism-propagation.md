# Morphisms have propagation tending to zero

In the [asymptotic category](../asymptotic-category.md), the matrices $u_i$ of a sequence $u:M\to N$ representing a morphism satisfy $\operatorname{prop}(u_i)\to0$, for the labels of $M$ and $N$.

*Proof sketch.* This is the defining condition of the sequences.
