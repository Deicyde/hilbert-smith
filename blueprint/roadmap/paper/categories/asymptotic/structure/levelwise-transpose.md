# The levelwise transpose

For a sequence $u=(u_i)$ of matrices $M\to N$ representing a morphism of the [asymptotic category](../asymptotic-category.md), the *levelwise transpose* is the sequence $(u_i^{T})$ from $N$ to $M$, with $u_i^T$ the dual of $u_i$ in the [free category](../free-category.md). It has the same propagation as $u$ at every level ([propagation of a transpose](../prop-transpose.md)), so its propagation tends to zero ([morphisms have propagation tending to zero](morphism-propagation.md)).
