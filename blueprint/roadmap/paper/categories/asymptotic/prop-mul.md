# Propagation of a product

For labelled finite sets $B,B',B''$ and matrices $u\in\mathbb Q^{B'\times B}$, $v\in\mathbb Q^{B''\times B'}$, $\operatorname{prop}(vu)\le\operatorname{prop}(v)+\operatorname{prop}(u)$.

*Proof sketch.* A nonzero entry $(vu)_{ca}$ needs some $b$ with $v_{cb}u_{ba}\ne0$. Then the triangle inequality through $\lambda'(b)$ gives the bound.
