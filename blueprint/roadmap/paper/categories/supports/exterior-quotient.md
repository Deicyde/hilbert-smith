# Exterior quotient

For $Z\subset X$, $\mathcal B_{\mathbf G,Z}(X)=\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X)/\mathcal A_Z$ is the [quotient](../../l-theory/base/categories/filtration-quotient.md) of $\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X)$ by the [support subcategory](support-subcategory.md) $\mathcal A_Z$, a full additive subcategory closed under duality.

**Remark (not formalized).** For closed invariant $Z$, $\mathcal A_Z$ is moreover a Karoubi filtration of $\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X)$ (the [support filtration](support-filtration.md) with $S=X$), so $\mathcal B_{\mathbf G,Z}(X)$ is a Karoubi quotient.
