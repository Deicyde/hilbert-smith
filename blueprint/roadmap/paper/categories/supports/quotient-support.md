# Supported objects of a support quotient

For $S,Z,Y\subset X$, $\mathcal U_Y$ is the predicate "$Y$-supported" on the objects of the [quotient](../../l-theory/base/categories/filtration-quotient.md) $\mathcal A_S/\mathcal A_Z$ of $\mathcal A_S$ by the [support subcategory inside it](relative-support.md) $\mathcal A_Z$.
