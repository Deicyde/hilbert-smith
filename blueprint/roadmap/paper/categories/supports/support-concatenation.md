# Concatenation of supported objects

The concatenation $(n_i+m_i,\;x_i\sqcup y_i)$ of two $S$-supported objects, the chosen biproduct of the [asymptotic category](../asymptotic/asymptotic-category.md), is $S$-supported ([support subcategory](support-subcategory.md)).

*Proof sketch.* Eventually the labels of both pieces lie in the $\varepsilon$-thickening of $S$.
