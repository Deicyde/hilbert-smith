# Support subcategory

For a closed invariant $S\subset X$, $\mathcal A_S$ is the full subcategory of $\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X)$ on the objects such that for every $\varepsilon>0$, eventually all $x_i(k)$ lie in the open $\varepsilon$-thickening of $S$. Then $\mathcal A_\varnothing$ consists of the objects with $n_i=0$ eventually, and $\mathcal A_X$ consists of all objects.
