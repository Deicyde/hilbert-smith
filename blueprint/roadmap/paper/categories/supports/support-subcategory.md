# Support subcategory

For any $S\subseteq X$ (in every use $S$ is closed and invariant; the definition needs neither), $\mathcal A_S$ is the full subcategory of $\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X)$ on the objects such that for every $\varepsilon>0$, eventually all $x_i(k)$ lie in the open $\varepsilon$-thickening of $S$. It is a full additive subcategory closed under duality ([additivity](support-dual-additive.md)).

**Remark (not formalized).** $\mathcal A_\varnothing$ consists of the objects that have no generators at level $i$ for all but finitely many $i$, and $\mathcal A_X$ consists of all objects.
