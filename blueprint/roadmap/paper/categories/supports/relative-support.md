# Support subcategory inside a support subcategory

For $S,S'\subset X$, $\mathcal A_{S'}\subset\mathcal A_S$ is the predicate "$S'$-supported" on the objects of the duality category $\mathcal A_S$, the full subcategory of the [support subcategory](support-subcategory.md) $\mathcal A_S$.
