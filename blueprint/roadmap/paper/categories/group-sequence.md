# Group sequence

A group sequence is a sequence $\mathbf G=(G_i,\chi_i)_{i\in\mathbb N}$ of finite groups $G_i$ with homomorphisms $\chi_i:G_i\to C_p$. A $C_p$-space is regarded as a $G_i$-space through $\chi_i$. The trivial sequence $\mathbf 1$ has trivial groups.
