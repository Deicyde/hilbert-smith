# Duality on the asymptotic category

$\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X)$ is a [strict duality category](../l-theory/A1.md) under $M^*=M$ and $[u_i]^*=[u_i^{T}]$.

*Proof sketch.* Transposition keeps equivariance and propagation ([propagation bounds](propagation-bounds.md)), reverses composition, is $\mathbb Q$-linear and is an involution on the nose.
