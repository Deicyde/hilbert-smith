# Forgetting the group

$U:\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X)\to\mathcal A_{\mathbf 1}(X)$ is the strict duality functor sending $(n_i,x_i)$ to the object with basis $B_i=G_i\times\{1..n_i\}$ (enumerated in a fixed order) and labels $\lambda_i$, and sending $[u]\mapsto[u]$.
