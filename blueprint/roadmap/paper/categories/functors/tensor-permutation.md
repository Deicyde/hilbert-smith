# Permutation tensor

$\tau\otimes-:\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X)\to\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X)$ is the strict duality functor sending the basis $B$ to $C_p\times B$ with diagonal action $g(c,b)=(\chi_i(g)c,gb)$ and labels $\ell(c,b)=\ell(b)$, and sending $u\mapsto\mathrm{id}_{\mathbb Q[C_p]}\otimes u$. When $\chi_i$ is onto with kernel $P_i$, this is tensoring with $\mathbb Q[G_i/P_i]$.
