# The relabelling isomorphism is unitary

In the setting of the [relabelling isomorphism](relabelling-isomorphism.md) $f:M\to M'$, $f^{*}=f^{-1}$.

*Proof sketch.* Both $f^*$ and $f^{-1}$ are given by identity matrices, since the identity matrices are self-transpose.
