# Pushforward of labels

For an equivariant continuous map $f:X\to X'$ of compact metric isometric $C_p$-spaces, $f_*:\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X)\to\mathcal A_{\mathbf G}(X')$ is the strict duality functor $(n_i,x_i)\mapsto(n_i,f\circ x_i)$, $[u]\mapsto[u]$.
