# Admissible sphere maps

A continuous $g:S^n\to S^n$ is *admissible* if $g(x)=\infty$ for every $x\in\mathbb R^n$ with $|x|\ge1$, and $g$ is homotopic to $\mathrm{id}_{S^n}$ relative to $\{\infty\}$. Its torus form is $g_T(x)=g(\tilde x)$, a continuous map $T^n\to S^n$.
