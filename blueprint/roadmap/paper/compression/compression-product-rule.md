# Product rule

Let $M_0,M_1,M_2\in\{D_i,D_i^{N-*}\}$. On each $M_j$ let $p_j,\jmath_j$ be the retained projection and inclusion: $\mathrm{pr},\mathrm{in}$ on chains and $\mathrm{in}^*,\mathrm{pr}^*$ on cochains. Let $Q_1=1-\jmath_1p_1$. For matrices $u:M_1\to M_2$ and $v:M_0\to M_1$,
$$(p_2u\jmath_1)(p_1v\jmath_0)-p_2uv\jmath_0=-p_2uQ_1v\jmath_0 .$$

*Proof sketch.* Substitute $\jmath_1p_1=1-Q_1$. On cochains, $\mathrm{pr}^*\mathrm{in}^*=(\mathrm{in}\,\mathrm{pr})^*=1-Q^*$.
