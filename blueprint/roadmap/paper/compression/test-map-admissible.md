# The test map is admissible

The test map $f$ is admissible, and its torus form is $f_T$.

*Proof sketch.* $f=\infty$ off $B(0,17/64)$ by [properties](../chain-models/completed-maps-properties.md) (4). [Homotopy](../local-data/chart/test-map-homotopy.md) gives the rest.
