# Compressed complex

For $i\ge i_0$, $C_i$ is the based module spanned by $R_i$, and $\mathrm{pr}_i:D_i\to C_i$ and $\mathrm{in}_i:C_i\to D_i$ are the based projection and inclusion. Put $Q_i=1-\mathrm{in}_i\mathrm{pr}_i$ and $\hat d=\mathrm{pr}_i\,d\,\mathrm{in}_i$. A retained cell $b$ has the label $\ell(b)=(\pi(\tilde x_b),f_T(x_b))\in T\times S^n$. For $i<i_0$, $C_i=0$. The sequence $(C_i)$ is an object of $\mathcal A_{\mathbf 1}(T\times S^n)$.
