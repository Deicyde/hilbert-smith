# Standing data

The standing data consist of:
- a prime $p$;
- integers $a_i\ge1$ for $i\in\mathbb N$, groups $G_i=C_{p^{a_i}}$ and surjections $\chi_i:G_i\to C_p$, with $q_i=|G_i|$;
- a nonempty compact metric space $T$ with a free isometric $C_p$-action;
- $n\ge0$, a point $y\in S^n$ (round metric) and $0<R<d(y,-y)$.

From these, $Z_0=\{z\in S^n: d(y,z)\ge R\}$ and $Z=T\times Z_0\subset T\times S^n$, with the maximum metric. Put $N=n+4$. $G_i$ acts on $T\times S^n$ through $\chi_i$ on the first factor.
