# Sheet shift

For a matrix $u:M\to M'$ at level $i$ and $g\in G_i$, $\operatorname{Ind}_g u=u\otimes R_{g^{-1}}$, where $R_{g^{-1}}(b\otimes s)=b\otimes sg^{-1}$. So $\operatorname{Ind}_1u=u\otimes1$.
