# The exterior

Let $n\ge1$ and $R>0$. The *exterior* is
$$Z_0=\{\infty\}\cup\{z\in\mathbb R^n:|z|\ge R\}\subseteq S^n,$$
in the [round sphere](round-sphere.md). For a compact metric space $T$ with an isometric $C_p$-action, possibly empty, we write $Z=T\times Z_0\subseteq T\times S^n$, where $T\times S^n$ has the maximum metric $\max(d_T,d_S)$ and the $C_p$-action on the first factor. The definition of $Z_0$ does not need $n\ge1$ or $R>0$.
