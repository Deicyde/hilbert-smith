# Duality-compatible homotopy idempotent

Let $(\mathcal C,\Phi)$ be a strict $N$-dimensional symmetric Poincaré complex in $\operatorname{Kar}\mathcal A$. A *compatible homotopy idempotent* on it is a triple $(E,\mathcal H,\mathcal V)$ of a chain map $E:\mathcal C\to\mathcal C$, a degree $+1$ map $\mathcal H$ on $\mathcal C$ and a degree $+1$ map $\mathcal V:\mathcal C^{N-*}\to\mathcal C$, with
$$E^2-E=d\mathcal H+\mathcal Hd,\qquad E\Phi-\Phi E^*=d\mathcal V+\mathcal Vd^{N-*}.$$
