# The round sphere

Let $n\ge1$. Write $S^n=\mathbb R^n\cup\{\infty\}$, with $|z|$ the Euclidean norm on $\mathbb R^n$. The *round metric* $d_S(z,w)$ is the angle between $\sigma^{-1}(z)$ and $\sigma^{-1}(w)$, where $\sigma$ is stereographic projection from $(0,\dots,0,1)$ of the unit sphere of $\mathbb R^{n+1}$ onto $S^n$. It is a metric inducing the topology of the one-point compactification, so $(S^n,d_S)$ is a compact metric space; $C_p$ acts on it trivially.
