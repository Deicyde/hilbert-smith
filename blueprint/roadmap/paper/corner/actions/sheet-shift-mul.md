# Sheet shifts multiply

$\operatorname{Ind}_g u\,\operatorname{Ind}_h v=\operatorname{Ind}_{g+h}(uv)$.

*Proof sketch.* $R_{-g}R_{-h}=R_{-(g+h)}$, since $(s-h)-g=s-(g+h)$.
