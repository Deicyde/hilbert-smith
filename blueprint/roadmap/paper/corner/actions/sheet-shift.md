# Sheet shift

For a matrix $u:M\to M'$ at level $i$ and $g\in G_i$, $\operatorname{Ind}_g u=u\otimes R_{-g}$, where $R_{-g}(b\otimes s)=b\otimes(s-g)$. So $\operatorname{Ind}_0u=u\otimes1$.
