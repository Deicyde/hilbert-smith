# The boundary of the cube face

Let $n\in\mathbb N$ and $s\in\mathbb R$. In the [round sphere](round-sphere.md), $Y_0=S^n$, and for $m\ge1$, $Y_m=\{x\in B_m:|x_k|=s\text{ for some }k\le n-m+1\}$, the boundary of the [cube face](cut-face.md) $B_m$ in the affine plane $\mathbb R^{n-m+1}\times\{s\}^{m-1}$.
