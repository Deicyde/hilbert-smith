# The induced complex

For $(C,\phi)$ as in [Graph-type homotopy action](../actions/graph-type-action.md), $(\operatorname{Ind}C,\Phi)$ with $\Phi=\operatorname{Ind}\phi$ is a strict $N$-dimensional symmetric Poincaré complex in $\mathcal B_{\mathbf G,Z}(T\times S^n)$, concentrated in $[0,N]$.

*Proof sketch.* $\operatorname{Ind}$ is a strict duality functor $\mathcal B_{\mathbf 1,Z}(T\times S^n)\to\mathcal B_{\mathbf G,Z}(T\times S^n)$ ([Induction is a strict duality functor](../actions/induction-duality-functor.md)). A duality functor carries strict $N$-dimensional symmetric Poincaré complexes concentrated in $[0,N]$ to such complexes, and for a strict functor the structure is $\operatorname{Ind}\phi$ ([Duality functors carry Poincaré complexes](../../l-theory/base/pairs/functor-poincare.md)).
