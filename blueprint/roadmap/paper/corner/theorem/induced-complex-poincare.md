# The induced complex

For $(C,\phi)$ as in [Graph-type homotopy action](../actions/graph-type-action.md), $(\operatorname{Ind}C,\Phi)$ with $\Phi=\operatorname{Ind}\phi$ is a strict $N$-dimensional symmetric Poincaré complex in $\mathcal B_{\mathbf G,Z}(T\times S^n)$, concentrated in $[0,N]$.

*Proof sketch.* A strict duality functor carries strict Poincaré complexes to strict Poincaré complexes ([Induced homomorphism](../../l-theory/base/groups/induced-map.md), step 2), and it keeps zero objects zero.
