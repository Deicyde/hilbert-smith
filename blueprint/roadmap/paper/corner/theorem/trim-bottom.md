# Trimming the bottom

In the setting of [Trimming the top](trim-top.md), with $C_k$ a zero object for $k<0$ instead, there are $D''$ with $D''_k$ a zero object for $k<0$ and $D''_k=D'_k$ for $k>0$, and $j,\pi$ with $\pi j=1$ and $j\pi\simeq1$.

*Proof sketch.*
1. For $k<0$, $-1=d_{k+1}h_k+h_{k-1}d_k$.
2. $p=-h_{-1}d_0$ is idempotent on $D'_0$, with $pd_1=0$ and $d_0(1-p)=0$.
3. $D''_0=\operatorname{im}(1-p)$, a zero object below $0$; $j,\pi$ as before.
4. $j\pi-1=dk+kd$ with $k_m=h_m$ for $m<0$ and $k_m=0$ for $m\ge0$.
