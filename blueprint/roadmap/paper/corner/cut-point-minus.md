# The point y₋

Let $n\in\mathbb N$ and $s\in\mathbb R$. The point $y_-=(-s,s,\dots,s)\in\mathbb R^n$ of the [round sphere](round-sphere.md) $S^n$ (the first coordinate is $-s$).
