# Lemma U (open statement)

For an additive category $B$, $U(B)$ is the proposition: for every $j\ge1$ and every object $P$ of $\mathrm{Kar}\,C_{\mathbb Z^j}(B)$, there are objects $F,F'$ of $C_{\mathbb Z^j}(B)$ with $P\oplus F\cong F'$. The paper assumes $U(\mathcal S)$ for the finite-support category $\mathcal S$ of [Finite-support sum](../base/groups/finite-support-sum.md). It is an open statement: no proof is given.
