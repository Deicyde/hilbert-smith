# The symmetric ray

$\Lambda_+\subset\Lambda$ is the subcomplex of [the symmetric line](../localization/line/symmetric-line.md) spanned by the $v_n$ and $e_{n+1/2}$ with $n\ge0$, an object of $H_+$ over $\mathbb Q$. Its structure $\delta\phi_0\in M(\Lambda_+)_1$ is $\delta\phi_0(e_{n+1/2}^*)=\tfrac12(v_n+v_{n+1})$ for $n\ge0$, $\delta\phi_0(v_n^*)=\tfrac12(e_{n-1/2}+e_{n+1/2})$ for $n\ge1$, and $\delta\phi_0(v_0^*)=\tfrac12e_{1/2}$.

## Sources

- New: the cellular chains of $[0,\infty)$, with the line's structure cut at $0$.
