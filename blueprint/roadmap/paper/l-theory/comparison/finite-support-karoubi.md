# Idempotent completion of the finite-support category

Let $\mathbf G$ be a group sequence. $\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathcal S_{\mathbf G}$, for the [finite-support subcategory](../../categories/asymptotic/eventually-zero-subcategory.md) $\mathcal S_{\mathbf G}$, is $\mathbb Q$-linearly equivalent to the category of sequences $(M_i)$ of finitely generated $\mathbb Q[G_i]$-modules with $M_i$ a zero object for all but finitely many $i$. Hence it is semisimple abelian with objects of finite length.

*Proof sketch.* An idempotent of $\mathcal S_{\mathbf G}$ lives in one finite block. Kar commutes with finite products. Then apply [the free case](free-group-ring-karoubi.md) blockwise.
