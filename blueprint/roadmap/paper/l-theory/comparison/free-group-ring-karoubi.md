# Idempotent completion of free $\mathbb Q[G]$-modules

For a finite group $G$, $\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathrm{Free}(\mathbb Q[G])$ is $\mathbb Q$-linearly equivalent to the category of finitely generated $\mathbb Q[G]$-modules. Every such module is semisimple of finite length.

*Proof sketch.* $(M,e)\mapsto\mathrm{im}\,e$ is fully faithful onto the finitely generated projectives. By Maschke, every finitely generated module is projective and semisimple.
