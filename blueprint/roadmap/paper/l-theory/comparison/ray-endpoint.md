# The endpoint of the ray

$\mathrm{pt}_0$ is the complex over $\mathbb Q$ with $\mathbb Qv_0$ in chain degree $0$ at position $0$ and zero objects elsewhere, with the $0$-dimensional structure $\theta\in M(\mathrm{pt}_0)_0$, $\theta(v_0^*)=\sigma_0v_0$, where $\sigma_0=-1$. The map $f:\mathrm{pt}_0\to\Lambda_+$ is the inclusion of $\mathbb Qv_0$ into [the symmetric ray](symmetric-ray.md).
