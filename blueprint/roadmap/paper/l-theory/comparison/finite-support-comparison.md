# Point comparison for the finite-support category

Assume $U(\mathcal S)$. Then $\mathrm{cls}:L^p_k(\mathcal S)\to\mathbb L_k(\mathcal S)$ is bijective for all $k$.

*Proof sketch.* $K_{-j}(\mathcal S)=0$ for every $j\ge1$ by the [stable criterion](../bounded/categories/lower-k-vanishing-criterion.md). Then apply [the decoration comparison](decoration-comparison.md).
