# Homotopy-isometry invariance

Let $(C,\phi)$ and $(C',\phi')$ be strict $k$-dimensional symmetric Poincaré complexes in $\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathcal A$, $h:C\to C'$ a chain homotopy equivalence, and $\chi\in M(C')_{k+1}$ with $\partial\chi=h_\%\phi-\phi'$. Then $[C,\phi]=[C',\phi']$ in $L^p_k(\mathcal A)$.

*Proof sketch.* [Homotopy-isometric complexes are cobordant](../pairs/cylinder-cobordism.md) with $\mathcal P$ all objects gives a cobordism; then use the definition of [Projective L-group](l-group.md).
