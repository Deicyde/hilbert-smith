# Sums of functors

For duality functors $(F,\xi),(G,\zeta):\mathcal B\to\mathcal B'$, let $F\oplus G$ be $M\mapsto FM\oplus GM$, with $\xi\oplus\zeta$ followed by the canonical isomorphism $(FM\oplus GM)^*\cong FM^*\oplus GM^*$. Then $F\oplus G$ is a duality functor and $(F\oplus G)_*=F_*+G_*:L_k(\mathcal B)\to L_k(\mathcal B')$.

*Proof sketch.* $(F\oplus G)C=FC\oplus GC$ with structure $F_\%\phi\oplus G_\%\phi$, then [Group structure on L](l-group-add.md).
