# The projective L-group

For a [duality category](../categories/duality-category.md) $\mathcal A$, the *projective L-group* is
$$L^p_k(\mathcal A):=L_k(\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathcal A),$$
the [L-group](l-group.md) of the [idempotent completion](../categories/karoubi-duality.md) of $\mathcal A$ with its induced duality.
