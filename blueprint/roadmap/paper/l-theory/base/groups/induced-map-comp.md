# Functoriality of induced maps

For duality functors $F:\mathcal A\to\mathcal B$ and $G:\mathcal B\to\mathcal C$, $(G\circ F)_*=G_*\circ F_*$.

*Proof sketch.* $(GF)C=G(FC)$, and the composite $\xi$ gives $(GF)_\%\phi=G_\%F_\%\phi$.
