# Negation in L^p

For every strict $k$-dimensional symmetric Poincaré complex $(C,\phi)$, $-[C,\phi]=[C,-\phi]$ in $L^p_k(\mathcal A)$.

*Proof sketch.* By definition of negation in [Group structure on L^p](l-group-add.md).
