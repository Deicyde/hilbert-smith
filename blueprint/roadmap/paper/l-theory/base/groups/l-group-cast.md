# Transport of L-groups along an equality of degrees

For a duality category $\mathcal B$ and integers $m=m'$, the *transport* $L_m(\mathcal B)\cong L_{m'}(\mathcal B)$ is the identity isomorphism of abelian groups along the equality of degrees.
