# Duals of identity chain maps

For a complex $C$ and $n\in\mathbb Z$, the [dual](dual-map.md) of the identity of $C$ is the identity: $1_C^{n-*}=1_{C^{n-*}}$.

*Proof sketch.* In degree $r$, $1^*=1$.
