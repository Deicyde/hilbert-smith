# Lifting homotopy equivalences to a full subcategory

Let $\mathcal P\subset\mathcal B$ be a full additive subcategory and $K,L$ complexes in $\mathcal P$. A chain homotopy equivalence between $K$ and $L$, taken in $\mathcal B$, lifts to a chain homotopy equivalence between $K$ and $L$ in $\mathcal P$ with the same maps and homotopies. More generally this holds for complexes $K,L$ and a chain homotopy equivalence $FK\simeq FL$, for any fully faithful additive functor $F$: the chain maps and homotopies lift componentwise, and the identities they satisfy hold because they hold after $F$.
