# Dual of an isomorphism of complexes

Let $u:C\cong D$ be an isomorphism of complexes in a duality category and $n\in\mathbb Z$. Its *dual* is the isomorphism $u^{n-*}:D^{n-*}\cong C^{n-*}$ given by the [dual](dual-map.md) of $u$, with inverse the dual of $u^{-1}$; the two composites are identities by [Duals of chain maps reverse composition](dual-map-comp.md) and [Duals of identity chain maps](dual-map-id.md).
