# Image of a complex under a duality functor

Let $(F,\xi):\mathcal B\to\mathcal B'$ be a [duality functor](../../categories/duality-functor.md) and $C$ a complex in $\mathcal B$, indexed by $\mathbb Z$. Its *image* $FC$ is the complex in $\mathcal B'$ with chain objects $(FC)_r=F(C_r)$ and differentials $F(d_r)$; these compose to zero because $F$ is additive.
