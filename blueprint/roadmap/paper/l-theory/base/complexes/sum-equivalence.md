# Sums of equivalences

For chain maps $f:C\to C'$ and $g:D\to D'$, $f\oplus g$ is a chain homotopy equivalence iff $f$ and $g$ both are.

*Proof sketch.* ($\Leftarrow$) Take sums of the inverses and the homotopies. ($\Rightarrow$) $\mathcal C(f\oplus g)=\mathcal C(f)\oplus\mathcal C(g)$, so $\mathcal C(f)$ is a retract of a contractible complex. If $s$ contracts $E$ and $pi=1$ for chain maps $i,p$, then $psi$ contracts the retract. Conclude with [Cone criterion](cone-criterion.md).
