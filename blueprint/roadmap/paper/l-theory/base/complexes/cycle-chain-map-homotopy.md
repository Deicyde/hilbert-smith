# Homologous cycles give homotopic maps

If $\phi,\phi'\in M(C)_n$ are cycles and $\phi-\phi'=\partial X$, then $\phi_\natural\simeq\phi'_\natural$.

*Proof sketch.* The homotopy is $H_r=X_{r+1}:C^*_{n-r}\to C_{r+1}$. The identity $(\partial X)_r=dX_{r+1}+(-1)^rX_rd^*$ is the homotopy equation, because the differential of $C^{n-*}$ is $(-1)^rd^*$.
