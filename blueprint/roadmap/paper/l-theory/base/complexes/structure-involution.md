# The involution T

$T:M(C)_n\to M(C)_n$, $(Tf)_r=(-1)^{r(n-r)}f_{n-r}^*$, is a chain map: $T\partial=\partial T$. In pair indices, $(Tf)_{r,s}=(-1)^{rs}(f_{s,r})^*$, read through $C_r^{**}=C_r$.
