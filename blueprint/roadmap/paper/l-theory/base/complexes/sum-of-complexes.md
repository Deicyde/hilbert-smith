# Sum of structured complexes

$(C,\phi)\oplus(C',\phi')=(C\oplus C',\phi\oplus\phi')$, where $(\phi\oplus\phi')_r$ is $\phi_r\oplus\phi'_r$ precomposed with the canonical isomorphism $(C_{n-r}\oplus C'_{n-r})^*\cong C^*_{n-r}\oplus C'^*_{n-r}$.
