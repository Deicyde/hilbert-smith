# The shift of a complex

For a complex $C$ in a preadditive category with binary direct sums and a zero object, the *shift* is the [mapping cone](cone.md) $\Sigma C=\mathcal C(C\to0)$ of the zero map to the zero complex: $(\Sigma C)_r=0\oplus C_{r-1}$, with the differential of $C$ on the second summand.
