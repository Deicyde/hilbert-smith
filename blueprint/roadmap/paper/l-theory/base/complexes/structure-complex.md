# Structure complex

For a complex $C$, $M(C)_n=\prod_{r\in\mathbb Z}\mathrm{Hom}(C_{n-r}^*,C_r)$, which is a finite product when $C$ is bounded, with $(\partial f)_r=d f_{r+1}+(-1)^r f_r d^*$. Then $\partial^2=0$, so $M(C)$ is a complex of $\mathbb Q$-vector spaces.
