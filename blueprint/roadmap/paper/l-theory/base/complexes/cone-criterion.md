# Cone criterion

A chain map $f$ of bounded complexes in an additive category is a chain homotopy equivalence iff $\mathcal C(f)$ is contractible.

*Proof sketch.* In the homotopy category, which is triangulated, $C\to D\to\mathcal C(f)$ is distinguished, so $f$ is invertible iff $\mathcal C(f)\cong0$, and $\mathcal C(f)\cong0$ iff $\mathcal C(f)$ is contractible.
