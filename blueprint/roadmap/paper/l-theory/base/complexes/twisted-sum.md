# Twisted sum

Let $X,Y$ be complexes and $\tau_r:Y_r\to X_{r-1}$ maps with $d\tau+\tau d=0$. Then $X\oplus_\tau Y$ has terms $X_r\oplus Y_r$ and differential $\begin{pmatrix}d&\tau\\0&d\end{pmatrix}$, which squares to zero because $d\tau+\tau d=0$.
