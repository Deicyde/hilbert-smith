# Triangular map

A chain map $F:X\oplus_\tau Y\to X'\oplus_{\tau'}Y'$ of twisted sums is *triangular* if its component $X\to Y'$ is zero, so that $F_r=\begin{pmatrix}a_r&b_r\\0&c_r\end{pmatrix}$. Its *diagonal components* are $a:X\to X'$ and $c:Y\to Y'$; both are chain maps.

*Proof sketch.* Compare the diagonal entries of $dF$ and $Fd$: they are $da=ad$ and $dc=cd$. The off-diagonal entry gives $db+\tau'c=a\tau+bd$, which is not needed.
