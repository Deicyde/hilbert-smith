# Dual complex

Complexes are $\mathbb Z$-graded chain complexes in a duality category $\mathcal B$. For a complex $C$ and $n\in\mathbb Z$, $C^{n-*}$ has $(C^{n-*})_r=C_{n-r}^*$ and $d_r=(-1)^r d^*$.
