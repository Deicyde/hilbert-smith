# Bounded complexes have finitely many nonzero chain objects

Let $C$ be a complex, indexed by $\mathbb Z$, in a preadditive category, whose chain objects $C_r$ are zero objects for $r<0$ and for $r>k$. Then only finitely many chain objects of $C$ are not zero objects.

*Proof sketch.* They all lie in degrees $0\le r\le k$.
