# Triangular equivalences

A [triangular](triangular-map.md) chain map $F:X\oplus_\tau Y\to X'\oplus_{\tau'}Y'$ whose diagonal components are chain homotopy equivalences is a chain homotopy equivalence.

*Proof sketch.* After the summands are reordered, $\mathcal C(F)$ is a twisted sum of $\mathcal C(a)$ and $\mathcal C(c)$. These are contractible by [Cone criterion](cone-criterion.md), so $\mathcal C(F)$ is contractible by [Twisted sums of contractible complexes](split-extension-contractible.md). Apply the cone criterion again.
