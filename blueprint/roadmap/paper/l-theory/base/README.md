# Strict symmetric L-theory

Complexes live in the idempotent completion of a small $\mathbb Q$-linear additive category with a strict duality. A structure on a complex is a cycle of the structure complex fixed by the involution $T$; over $\mathbb Q$ this carries all of symmetric L-theory. The sections build the categories, the chain-level algebra, Poincaré complexes and pairs with their gluing and cobordism, and the projective L-groups with their functoriality.
