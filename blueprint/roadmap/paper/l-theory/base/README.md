# Strict symmetric L-theory

Complexes live in an arbitrary small $\mathbb Q$-linear additive category $\mathcal B$ with a strict duality. A structure on a complex is a cycle of the structure complex fixed by the involution $T$; over $\mathbb Q$ this carries all of symmetric L-theory. The sections build the categories, the chain-level algebra, Poincaré complexes and pairs with their gluing and cobordism, and the L-groups $L_k(\mathcal B)$ with their functoriality. The projective L-groups are $L^p_k(\mathcal A)=L_k(\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathcal A)$; other choices of $\mathcal B$, such as a subcategory of decorated objects, are used in the localization chapter.
