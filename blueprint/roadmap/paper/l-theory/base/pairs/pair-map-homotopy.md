# Pair maps of homologous data

In the setting of [Pair map](pair-map.md), for $X\in M(D)_{n+1}$ the pair maps of $(\delta\phi,\phi)$ and $(\delta\phi-\partial X,\phi)$ are homotopic.

*Proof sketch.* They differ by the inclusion $D\to\mathcal C(f)$ composed with $(\partial X)_\natural$. That map is null-homotopic by [Homologous cycles give homotopic maps](../complexes/cycle-chain-map-homotopy.md) applied to $\partial X$ and $0$.
