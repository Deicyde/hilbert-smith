# Zero Poincaré complex

In a duality category $\mathcal B$, the zero complex with the zero structure, $(0,0)$, is a strict $k$-dimensional symmetric Poincaré complex for every $k$.

*Proof sketch.* (Proof obligations.) All chain objects of $0$ and of $0^{k-*}$ are zero objects, since the dual of a zero object is one, so $0_\natural$ is an isomorphism.
