# The completed quotient is full

Every morphism $(M,[e])\to(N,[e'])$ of $\mathrm{Kar}(\mathcal A/\mathcal U)$ is $(\mathrm{Kar}\,q)(f)$ for some $f:(M,e)\to(N,e')$ in $\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathcal A$.

*Proof sketch.* The morphism is a class $[g]$ with $[e'][g][e]=[g]$. Take $f=e'ge$.
