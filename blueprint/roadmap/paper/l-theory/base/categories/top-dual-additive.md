# All objects form a dual-closed additive subcategory

Let $\mathcal B$ be a [duality category](duality-category.md). All objects of $\mathcal B$ form a [full additive subcategory closed under duality](dual-additive-subcategory.md).

*Proof sketch.* It contains a zero object of $\mathcal B$, a sum of any two objects (the finite direct sums of $\mathcal B$ give binary ones), and the dual of each object.
