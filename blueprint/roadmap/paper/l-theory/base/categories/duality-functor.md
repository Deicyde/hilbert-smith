# Duality functor

A *duality functor* $(F,\xi):\mathcal A\to\mathcal B$ between duality categories is a $\mathbb Q$-linear functor $F$ with a natural isomorphism $\xi_M:F(M^*)\to F(M)^*$ such that $(\xi_M)^*=\xi_{M^*}$. It is *strict* if $\xi=\mathrm{id}$. Composition: $\xi^{GF}_M=\zeta_{FM}\circ G(\xi_M)$.
