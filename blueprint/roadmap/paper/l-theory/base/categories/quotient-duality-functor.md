# The quotient duality functor

Let $\mathcal U\subset\mathcal A$ be a [full additive subcategory with $\mathcal U^*\subset\mathcal U$](dual-additive-subcategory.md). The quotient functor $q:\mathcal A\to\mathcal A/\mathcal U$ to the [quotient by $\mathcal U$](filtration-quotient.md), $M\mapsto M$, $f\mapsto[f]$, is additive and $\mathbb Q$-linear and commutes with the duality on the nose, since $[f]^*=[f^*]$. With the identity as duality isomorphism, it is a [duality functor](duality-functor.md); it is [strict](duality-functors/quotient-functor-strict.md).
