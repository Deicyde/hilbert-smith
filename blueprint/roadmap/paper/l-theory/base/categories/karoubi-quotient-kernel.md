# Kernel of the completed quotient

For a full additive $\mathcal U\subset\mathcal A$ with $\mathcal U^*\subset\mathcal U$, a morphism $f$ of $\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathcal A$ satisfies $(\mathrm{Kar}\,q)(f)=0$ iff $f$ factors through an object of $\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathcal U$.

*Proof sketch.* $(\mathrm{Kar}\,q)(f)=[f]$, and $[f]=0$ iff $f\in I_{\mathcal U}$. Then apply [Maps factoring through Kar U](karoubi-of-filtration.md).
