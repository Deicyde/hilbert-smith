# A duality category has binary direct sums

A [duality category](duality-category.md) has a direct sum of any two objects, given by its finite direct sums. Mapping cones and sums of complexes use them.

*Proof sketch.* The direct sum of the family indexed by a two-element set is a binary direct sum.
