# Small Q-linear category with strict duality

A *duality category* is a small $\mathbb Q$-linear additive category $\mathcal A$ with a $\mathbb Q$-linear contravariant functor $*:\mathcal A\to\mathcal A$ such that $M^{**}=M$ and $f^{**}=f$ on the nose.
