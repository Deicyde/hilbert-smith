# Unitary natural isomorphism

A natural isomorphism $\eta:F\cong G$ of duality functors $(F,\xi),(G,\zeta):\mathcal A\to\mathcal B$ is *unitary* if $\eta_M^*\circ\zeta_M\circ\eta_{M^*}=\xi_M$ for every $M$.
