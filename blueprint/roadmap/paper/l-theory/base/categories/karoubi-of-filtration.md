# Maps factoring through Kar U

For a full additive $\mathcal U\subset\mathcal A$ with $\mathcal U^*\subset\mathcal U$, a morphism $f:(M,e)\to(N,e')$ of $\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathcal A$ factors through an object of $\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathcal U$ iff $f\in I_{\mathcal U}(M,N)$.

*Proof sketch.* ($\Rightarrow$) If $f=ba$ through $(W,w)$ and $w=cd$ through $V\in\mathcal U$, then $f=bwa=(bc)(da)$. ($\Leftarrow$) If $f=ba$ through $V$, then $f=e'fe=(e'b)(ae)$ factors through $(V,1)\in\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathcal U$.
