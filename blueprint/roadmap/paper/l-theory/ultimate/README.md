# Ultimate lower L-theory

The lower L-groups are $L^{\langle -j\rangle}_k(\mathcal A)=L^p_{k+j}(C_{\mathbb Z^j}\mathcal A)$, and $\mathbb L_k(\mathcal A)$ is their colimit along the transitions. On $\mathbb L$ the localization sequence needs no decoration, and excision and Mayer–Vietoris middle exactness follow.
