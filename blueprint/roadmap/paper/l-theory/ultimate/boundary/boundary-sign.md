# Boundary sign

For $k\in\mathbb Z$ and $j\ge0$, define $\sigma_{k,j}=\pm1$ by $\sigma_{k,0}=\eta_k$ and $\sigma_{k,j+1}=\eta_{k+j+1}\,\sigma_{k,j}$, where $\eta$ is the [line sign](../../localization/line/line-sign.md). Thus $\sigma_{k,j}=\prod_{i=0}^{j}\eta_{k+i}$.

## Sources

- New: chosen so that the ultimate boundary is well defined and the [ultimate pair formula](pair-formula.md) has sign $+1$.
