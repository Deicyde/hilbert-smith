# The ultimate boundary at each stage

For $x\in L^{\langle -j\rangle}_k(\mathcal A/\mathcal U)$, $\partial(\iota_jx)=\iota_{j+1}\big(\rho_j\,\partial^{(j)}x\big)$, where $\rho_j$ reads the [stage boundary](../../localization/transitions/level-boundary.md) $\partial^{(j)}x$ in $L^{\langle -j-1\rangle}_{k-1}(\mathcal U)$ as in [Ultimate boundary](ultimate-boundary.md).

*Proof sketch.* This is the defining property of a homomorphism out of a colimit given by compatible maps at each stage.
