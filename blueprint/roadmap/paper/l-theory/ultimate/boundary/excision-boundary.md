# Excision boundary

For an excisive pair $(\mathcal U_A,\mathcal U_B)$ in $\mathcal A$, with $r$ as in its definition,
$$\partial_{A,B}:=\partial\circ r_*\circ(q_B)_*:\mathbb L_k(\mathcal A)\to\mathbb L_k(\mathcal A/\mathcal U_B)\to\mathbb L_k(\mathcal U_A/\mathcal U_Y)\to\mathbb L_{k-1}(\mathcal U_Y),$$
where $\partial$ is the [ultimate boundary](ultimate-boundary.md) of $\mathcal U_Y\subset\mathcal U_A$ and the other maps are [induced maps](../groups/induced-map.md).
