# Intersection of full subcategories

For full subcategories $\mathcal U_A,\mathcal U_B\subset\mathcal A$, the *intersection* $\mathcal U_A\cap\mathcal U_B$ is the full subcategory of $\mathcal U_A$ whose objects are the objects of $\mathcal U_A$ that lie in $\mathcal U_B$.
