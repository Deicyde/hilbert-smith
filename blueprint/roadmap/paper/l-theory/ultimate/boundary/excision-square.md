# Excision square

For an excisive pair $(\mathcal U_A,\mathcal U_B)$ in $\mathcal A$, $(q_B)_*\circ(i_A)_*=e_*\circ(q_Y)_*:\mathbb L_k(\mathcal U_A)\to\mathbb L_k(\mathcal A/\mathcal U_B)$.

*Proof sketch.* $q_B\circ i_A=e\circ q_Y$ as duality functors ([Excisive pair of filtrations](excisive-pair.md)). Apply [Functoriality on ultimate L](../groups/induced-map-comp.md) to both sides.
