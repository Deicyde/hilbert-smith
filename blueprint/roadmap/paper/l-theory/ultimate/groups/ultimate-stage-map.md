# Stage maps into the ultimate L-group

For $j\ge0$, $\iota_j:L^{\langle -j\rangle}_k(\mathcal A)\to\mathbb L_k(\mathcal A)$ is the colimit map from stage $j$ of the [sequence of lower L-groups](lower-l-sequence.md) into its colimit, the [ultimate L-group](ultimate-l-group.md).
