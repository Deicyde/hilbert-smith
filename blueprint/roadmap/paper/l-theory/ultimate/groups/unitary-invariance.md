# Unitary invariance on ultimate L

If $\eta:F\cong G$ is a unitary natural isomorphism of duality functors $\mathcal A\to\mathcal B$, then $F_*=G_*$ on $\mathbb L_k(\mathcal A)$.

*Proof sketch.* At level $j$, [Bounded extension of a unitary isomorphism](bounded-unitary-extension.md) gives a unitary natural isomorphism $C_{\mathbb Z^j}\eta:C_{\mathbb Z^j}F\cong C_{\mathbb Z^j}G$. [Unitary invariance](../../base/groups/unitary-invariance.md) gives $(C_{\mathbb Z^j}F)_*=(C_{\mathbb Z^j}G)_*$, so $F_*\iota_j=G_*\iota_j$ by [The induced map at each level](induced-map-level.md). Every element of the colimit comes from a finite level ([Every element comes from a finite level](level-exhaustive.md)).
