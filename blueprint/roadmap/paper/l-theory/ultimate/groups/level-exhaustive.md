# Every element comes from a finite stage

Every element of $\mathbb L_k(\mathcal A)$ is $\iota_j x$ for some $j\ge0$ and some $x\in L^{\langle -j\rangle}_k(\mathcal A)$.

*Proof sketch.* Every element of a colimit of a sequence of groups comes from a finite stage.
