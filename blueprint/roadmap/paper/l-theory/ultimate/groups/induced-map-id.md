# Identity on ultimate L

$(\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal A})_*=\mathrm{id}$ on $\mathbb L_k(\mathcal A)$.

*Proof sketch.* $C_{\mathbb Z^j}(\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal A})$ is the identity functor of $C_{\mathbb Z^j}\mathcal A$ by [Bounded extension is functorial](../../bounded/categories/bounded-functor-comp.md). At stage $j$, [Functoriality on L^p](../../base/groups/lp-functoriality.md), item 1, and [The induced map at each stage](induced-map-level.md) give $(\mathrm{id})_*\iota_j=\iota_j$. Every element of the colimit comes from a finite stage ([Every element comes from a finite stage](level-exhaustive.md)).
