# Induced map on ultimate L

Let $F:\mathcal A\to\mathcal A'$ be a duality functor, and let $C_{\mathbb Z^j}F:C_{\mathbb Z^j}\mathcal A\to C_{\mathbb Z^j}\mathcal A'$ be its $j$-fold bounded extension ($C_{\mathbb Z^0}F=F$). $F_*:\mathbb L_k(\mathcal A)\to\mathbb L_k(\mathcal A')$ is the homomorphism of colimits induced by the levelwise homomorphisms $(C_{\mathbb Z^j}F)_*:L^{\langle -j\rangle}_k(\mathcal A)\to L^{\langle -j\rangle}_k(\mathcal A')$, which commute with $t$ by [Naturality of the transition](../../localization/line/transition-naturality.md).
