# Algebraic L-theory

This chapter builds the L-theory the paper uses, from strict symmetric Poincaré complexes over $\mathbb Q$. The base defines the projective L-groups $L^p_k$ of a small additive category with strict duality by cobordism. Bounded categories $C_{\mathbb Z^j}$ shift degrees and give the lower L-groups. For an involution-invariant Karoubi filtration, the localization sections construct the boundary from the decorated L-groups of the quotient to the L-groups of the filtration and prove exactness where the paper needs it, and product with the symmetric line gives the transition maps between levels. The ultimate L-groups $\mathbb L_k$ are the colimit over the levels; on them the localization sequence holds without decorations, with excision and a Mayer–Vietoris middle exactness. The comparison section shows that $L^p_k$ and $\mathbb L_k$ agree when lower K-theory vanishes, and applies this to the point categories.
