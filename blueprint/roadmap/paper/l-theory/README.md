# Algebraic L-theory

This chapter builds the L-theory the paper uses, from strict symmetric Poincaré complexes over $\mathbb Q$. The base defines, by cobordism, the L-groups $L_k(\mathcal B)$ of any small additive category $\mathcal B$ with strict duality, and the projective L-groups $L^p_k(\mathcal A)=L_k(\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathcal A)$. Bounded categories $C_{\mathbb Z^j}$ shift degrees and give the lower L-groups. For an involution-invariant Karoubi filtration, the localization sections construct the boundary from the decorated L-groups of the quotient to the L-groups of the filtration and prove exactness where the paper needs it, and product with the symmetric line gives the transition maps between levels. The ultimate L-groups $\mathbb L_k$ are the colimit over the levels; on them the localization sequence holds without decorations, with excision and a Mayer–Vietoris middle exactness. The comparison section shows that $L^p_k$ and $\mathbb L_k$ agree when lower K-theory vanishes, and applies this, through the open Lemma U, to the categories chapter's finite-support category $\mathcal S_{\mathbf G}$ and free categories $\mathrm{Free}(\mathbb Q[G_i])$.
