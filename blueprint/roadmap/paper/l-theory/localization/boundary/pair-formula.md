# Pair formula

Let $\mathcal U\subset\mathcal A$ be an involution-invariant Karoubi filtration. For every strict $k$-dimensional [$\mathcal U$-pair](u-pair.md) $V$ over $(P,\pi)$, $\partial[qV]=[P,\pi]$ in $L^p_{k-1}(\mathcal U)$.

*Proof sketch.* By definition of the [localization boundary](localization-boundary.md), $\partial[qV]=[P_x,\pi_x]$ for the chosen $\mathcal U$-pair $V_x$ with $[qV_x]=[qV]$. [Independence of the lift](boundary-independence.md) gives $[P_x,\pi_x]=[P,\pi]$.
