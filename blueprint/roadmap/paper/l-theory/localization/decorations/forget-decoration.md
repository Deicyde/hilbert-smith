# Forgetting the decoration

Let $\mathcal U\subset\mathcal A$ be as in [Decorated objects](decorated-object.md). The homomorphism
$$\mathrm{fgt}:L^{\mathrm{im}}_k(\mathcal A/\mathcal U)\to L^p_k(\mathcal A/\mathcal U),\qquad [C,\phi]\mapsto[C,\phi],$$
is the [induced homomorphism](../../base/groups/induced-map.md) of the inclusion $\mathrm{Dec}(\mathcal A/\mathcal U)\subset\mathrm{Kar}(\mathcal A/\mathcal U)$, a strict duality functor.
