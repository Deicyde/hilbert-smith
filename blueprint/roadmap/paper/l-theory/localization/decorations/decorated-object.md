# Decorated objects

Let $\mathcal U\subset\mathcal A$ be a full additive subcategory with $\mathcal U^*\subset\mathcal U$. The full subcategory $\mathrm{Dec}(\mathcal A/\mathcal U)\subset\mathrm{Kar}(\mathcal A/\mathcal U)$ of [decorated objects](decorated-predicate.md), with the duality of $\mathrm{Kar}(\mathcal A/\mathcal U)$, is a [duality category](../../base/categories/duality-category.md), since the decorated objects are [closed under sums and duals](decorated-closure.md) ([Duality category of a dual-closed subcategory](../../base/categories/dual-subcategory.md)).
