# Forgetting after the quotient map

$\mathrm{fgt}\circ q^{\mathrm{im}}_*=q_*:L^p_k(\mathcal A)\to L^p_k(\mathcal A/\mathcal U)$, where $q_*$ is the [induced homomorphism](../../base/groups/induced-map.md) of $q:\mathcal A\to\mathcal A/\mathcal U$.

*Proof sketch.* The corestriction $\mathrm{Kar}\,\mathcal A\to\mathrm{Dec}(\mathcal A/\mathcal U)$ followed by the inclusion into $\mathrm{Kar}(\mathcal A/\mathcal U)$ is $\mathrm{Kar}\,q$, as strict duality functors. Apply [Functoriality of induced maps](../../base/groups/induced-map-comp.md).
