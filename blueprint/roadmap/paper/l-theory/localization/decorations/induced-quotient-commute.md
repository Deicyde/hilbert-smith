# Induced quotient functors commute with the quotients

In the setting of [Functor induced on quotients](induced-quotient-functor.md), $\bar F\circ q=q'\circ F$ and $\mathrm{Kar}\,\bar F\circ\mathrm{Kar}\,q=\mathrm{Kar}\,q'\circ\mathrm{Kar}\,F$ as duality functors.

*Proof sketch.* Both composites agree on objects and on morphisms by definition, with duality data $[\xi_M]$ on both sides; on the completion both send $(M,e)$ to $(FM,[Fe])$ with duality data $[\xi_M\circ F(e^*)]$ ([Completion of a duality functor](../../base/categories/karoubi-functor.md)).
