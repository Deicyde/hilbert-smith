# Box product on iterated bounded categories

For a duality category $\mathcal A$ and $j\ge0$, the [box product](box-dual.md) duality pairing for $\mathcal B=C_{\mathbb Z^j}\mathcal A$ is a duality pairing
$$\operatorname{Kar}C_{\mathbb Z^j}\mathcal A\times C_\mathbb Z(\mathbb Q)\to\operatorname{Kar}C_{\mathbb Z^{j+1}}\mathcal A,$$
since $C_{\mathbb Z^{j+1}}\mathcal A=C_\mathbb Z(C_{\mathbb Z^j}\mathcal A)$ by the definition of [iterated bounded categories](../../bounded/categories/iterated-bounded-category.md).
