# Supports of tensor products

Let $S\subset\mathbb Z$. If every chain object $E_q$ of a complex $E$ in $C_\mathbb Z(\mathbb Q)$ holds zero objects at all positions outside $S$, then for every complex $C$ in $\operatorname{Kar}\mathcal B$ every chain object of the [tensor product](complex-product.md) $C\otimes E$ holds zero objects at all positions outside $S$.

*Proof sketch.* In position $n$, $C_p\boxtimes E_q$ holds $M^{\oplus d}$ with $d$ the dimension of $E_q$ at $n$, which is $0$ for $n\notin S$. A finite sum of zero objects is a zero object.
