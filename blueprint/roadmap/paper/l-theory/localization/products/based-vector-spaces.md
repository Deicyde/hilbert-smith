# Based vector spaces

$\mathbb Q$ also denotes the duality category whose objects are the based vector spaces $\mathbb Q^d$ ($d\ge0$), whose morphisms $\mathbb Q^d\to\mathbb Q^{d'}$ are the $d'\times d$ rational matrices, with composition the matrix product, the biproduct $\mathbb Q^d\oplus\mathbb Q^{d'}=\mathbb Q^{d+d'}$, and the duality $(\mathbb Q^d)^*=\mathbb Q^d$, $g^*=g^{\mathsf T}$ (transpose). Both identities $M^{**}=M$ and $g^{**}=g$ hold on the nose. The standard basis vector $e_i$ of $\mathbb Q^d$ has dual basis vector $e_i^*$, the same vector of $(\mathbb Q^d)^*$.
