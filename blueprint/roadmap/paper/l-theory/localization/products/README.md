# Products with the line

The symmetric line is a Poincaré complex over $C_{\mathbb Z}(\mathbb Q)$. Box products with bounded vector spaces give tensor products of complexes and of structures, with a Leibniz rule, compatibility with $T$ and pushforwards, and a duality map. Products with the line carry complexes and pairs up one bounded level, with no sign on the boundary.
