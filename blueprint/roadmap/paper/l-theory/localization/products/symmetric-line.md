# The symmetric line

Let $\mathbb Q$ be the duality category of [based vector spaces](based-vector-spaces.md). The *symmetric line* is the complex $\Lambda$ in $C_\mathbb Z(\mathbb Q)$ with $\Lambda_0$ equal to $\mathbb Qv_n$ in position $n$, $\Lambda_1$ equal to $\mathbb Qe_{n+1/2}$ in position $n$, every other $\Lambda_r$ a zero object, and $d e_{n+1/2}=v_{n+1}-v_n$ (propagation $1$).
