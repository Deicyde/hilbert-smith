# Summand projections of a tensor product

Let $(\boxtimes,\kappa):\mathcal B\times\mathcal E\to\mathcal B''$ be a [duality pairing](../duality-pairing.md), and $C$ a complex in $\mathcal B$ and $E$ a complex in $\mathcal E$, each with all but finitely many chain objects zero objects. For $p,q,r\in\mathbb Z$ the map $\pi_{p,q}:(C\otimes E)_r\to C_p\boxtimes E_q$ out of the [tensor product](../complex-product.md) $C\otimes E$ is the map out of the direct sum that is the identity on the summand $C_p\boxtimes E_q$ and $0$ on the other summands, when $p+q=r$; and $0$ otherwise.
