# Components of box products of maps

Let $\mathcal B$ be a $\mathbb Q$-linear additive category with finite biproducts, $f:N\to N'$ a map of $\operatorname{Kar}\mathcal B$ with $N=(M,e)$, $N'=(M',e')$, and $g:V\to V'$ a map of $C_\mathbb Z(\mathbb Q)$. For $m,n\in\mathbb Z$ the component $(f\boxtimes g)_{mn}:M^{\oplus d_n}\to M'^{\oplus d'_m}$ between the positions of the [objects of the box product](box-object.md) is the block matrix whose entry from summand $i$ to summand $i'$ is $(g_{mn})_{i'i}\,f$.
