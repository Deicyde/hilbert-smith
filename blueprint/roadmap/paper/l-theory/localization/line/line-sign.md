# The line sign

For $k\in\mathbb Z$, $\eta_k:=1$.

$\eta_k$ is the sign relating the boundary of the product of a strict $k$-dimensional pair with the line to the product of its boundary with the line. [Product of a pair with the line](product-with-line.md) computes that the boundary of $(f\otimes1,\delta\phi\otimes\lambda,\phi\otimes\lambda)$ is $(C\otimes\Lambda,\phi\otimes\lambda)$, so the sign is $+1$ for every $k$ in the conventions of [Structure complex](../../base/complexes/structure-complex.md), [Dual complex](../../base/complexes/dual-complex.md), [Mapping cone](../../base/complexes/cone.md) and [Pair map](../../base/pairs/pair-map.md), with the line as the second factor of [the external product](external-product.md). The index is the dimension of the pair.
