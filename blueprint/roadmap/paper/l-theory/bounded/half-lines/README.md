# Half-lines

The positive and negative half-lines of $C_{\mathbb Z}(\mathcal D)$ are Karoubi filtrations whose intersection is equivalent to $\mathcal D$. Swindles kill their $K_0$ and $L^p$. Truncation and support collapse give the functors that compare the levels.
