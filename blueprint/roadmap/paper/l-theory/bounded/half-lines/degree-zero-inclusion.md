# Degree-zero inclusion

Fix a zero object $0$ of $D$. $\iota_0:D\to H_b$ sends $X$ to the object with $X$ in degree $0$ and $0$ in every other degree, and sends $f$ to the matrix whose only nonzero entry is $f$, in position $(0,0)$. With $\xi_X:\iota_0(X^*)\to(\iota_0X)^*$ the identity in degree $0$ and the unique isomorphism $0\to0^*$ in every other degree, $(\iota_0,\xi)$ is a duality functor of propagation $0$.

*Proof sketch.* $\iota_0$ is $\mathbb Q$-linear and functorial entrywise. $\xi$ is natural, and $\xi_X^*=\xi_{X^*}$ holds in degree $0$ because both sides are identities, and in every other degree because both sides are maps between zero objects.
