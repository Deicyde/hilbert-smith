# The intersection of the half-lines is $D$

$\iota_{0*}:L^p_k(D)\to L^p_k(H_b)$ is an isomorphism with inverse $\sigma_*$.

*Proof sketch.* Apply [Equivalence invariance](../../base/groups/equivalence-invariance.md) to $\iota_0$ and $\sigma$, with the [collapse unit](support-collapse-unit.md) and [collapse counit](support-collapse-counit.md), which are unitary by [Collapse inverts the degree-zero inclusion](support-collapse-equivalence.md).
