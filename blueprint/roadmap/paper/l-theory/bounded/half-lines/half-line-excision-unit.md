# Excision unit

For $M\in H_+$ let $\eta_M:M\to\tau eM=M_{\ge0}$ be the class mod $H_b$ of the projection, which is the identity in degrees $n\ge0$ and the zero map to the zero object in degrees $n<0$. Then $\eta:\mathrm{id}_{H_+/H_b}\cong\tau\circ e$ is a natural isomorphism, for the [inclusion of the half-line quotient](half-line-quotient-inclusion.md) $e$ and the [truncation](half-line-truncation.md) $\tau$.

*Proof sketch.* (1) Inverse: the class of the inclusion $M_{\ge0}\to M$. Inclusion followed by projection is $1$. Projection followed by inclusion is $1-\pi_{<0}$, where $\pi_{<0}$ projects onto the degrees $n<0$; for $M\in H_+$ these degrees form an object of $H_b$, so $\pi_{<0}$ is $0$ mod $H_b$. (2) Naturality: for $f$ of propagation $r$, the two composites differ by the entries of $f$ between degrees $n<0$ and $m\ge0$, which pass through the degrees $[-r,-1]$, an object of $H_b$.
