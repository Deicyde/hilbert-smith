# Half-line excision

The [excision unit](half-line-excision-unit.md) $\eta:\mathrm{id}_{H_+/H_b}\cong\tau e$ and the [excision counit](half-line-excision-counit.md) $\varepsilon:e\tau\cong\mathrm{id}_{C_\mathbb Z(D)/H_-}$ are unitary, for the [inclusion of the half-line quotient](half-line-quotient-inclusion.md) $e$ and the [truncation](half-line-truncation.md) $\tau$ with their duality data.

*Proof sketch.* The dual of the projection $M\to M_{\ge0}$ is the inclusion in degrees $n\ge0$. (1) $\eta$: $\eta_M^*\xi^{\tau e}_M\eta_{M^*}$ is $1-\pi_{<0}$ on $M^*$, which is $1$ mod $H_b$ for $M\in H_+$. (2) $\varepsilon$: $\varepsilon_M^*\varepsilon_{M^*}$ equals $\xi^{e\tau}_M$ mod $H_-$. In degrees $n<0$ every entry involved is a map between zero objects or passes through $M_{<0}$.
