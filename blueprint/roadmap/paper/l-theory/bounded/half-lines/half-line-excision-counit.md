# Excision counit

For $M\in C_\mathbb Z(D)$ let $\varepsilon_M:e\tau M=M_{\ge0}\to M$ be the class mod $H_-$ of the inclusion, which is the identity in degrees $n\ge0$ and the zero map from the zero object in degrees $n<0$. Then $\varepsilon:e\circ\tau\cong\mathrm{id}_{C_\mathbb Z(D)/H_-}$ is a natural isomorphism, for the [inclusion of the half-line quotient](half-line-quotient-inclusion.md) $e$ and the [truncation](half-line-truncation.md) $\tau$.

*Proof sketch.* (1) Inverse: the class of the projection $M\to M_{\ge0}$. Inclusion followed by projection is $1$. Projection followed by inclusion is $1-\pi_{<0}$, and the degrees $n<0$ of any $M$ form an object of $H_-$, so $\pi_{<0}$ is $0$ mod $H_-$. (2) Naturality: for $f$ of propagation $r$, the two composites differ by entries of $f$ that pass through the degrees $n<0$ of $M$ or of the target, objects of $H_-$.
