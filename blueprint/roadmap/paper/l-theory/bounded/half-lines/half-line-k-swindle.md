# $K_0$ of a half-line vanishes

$K_0(\mathrm{Kar}\,H_\varepsilon)=0$.

*Proof sketch.* Let $P=(M,e)$. Then $G=\bigoplus_{k\ge0}\Sigma^{\varepsilon k}P$ is an object of $\mathrm{Kar}\,H_\varepsilon$: each degree is a finite sum, and the idempotent keeps the propagation of $e$. We have $G\cong P\oplus\Sigma^\varepsilon G$ and $\Sigma^\varepsilon G\cong G$, so $P\oplus G\cong G$ and $[P]=0$.
