# Half-line subcategories

For $\varepsilon=\pm1$, let $H_\varepsilon\subset C_\mathbb Z(D)$ be the full subcategory of objects $M$ for which some $R\in\mathbb N$ makes $M_n$ a zero object whenever $\varepsilon n<-R$. Let $H_b=H_+\cap H_-$ be the objects of bounded support.
