# Inclusion of the half-line quotient

Let $e:H_+/H_b\to C_\mathbb Z(D)/H_-$ be the identity on objects and send the class of $f$ mod $H_b$ to the class of $f$ mod $H_-$. Then $e$ is a well-defined strict duality functor.

*Proof sketch.* $H_b\subset H_-$, so a map that factors through an object of $H_b$ factors through an object of $H_-$, and $e$ is well defined. It is $\mathbb Q$-linear and functorial because both quotient maps are. Both dualities are induced from that of $C_\mathbb Z(D)$, so $e$ commutes with them on the nose.
