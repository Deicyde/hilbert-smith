# The bounded category $C_\mathbb Z(A)$

Let $A$ be a small $\mathbb Q$-linear additive category. An object of $C_\mathbb Z(A)$ is a family $M=(M_n)_{n\in\mathbb Z}$ of objects of $A$. A morphism $f:M\to N$ is a family $f_{mn}:M_n\to N_m$ for which some $r\ge0$ (the propagation) gives $f_{mn}=0$ whenever $|m-n|>r$. Composition is $(gf)_{ln}=\sum_m g_{lm}f_{mn}$, which is a finite sum. Biproducts are taken degreewise.
