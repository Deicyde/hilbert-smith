# Iterated bounded categories

Put $C_{\mathbb Z^0}(A)=A$ and $C_{\mathbb Z^{j+1}}(A)=C_\mathbb Z(C_{\mathbb Z^j}(A))$, with the duality of [The induced duality](bounded-duality.md) at each step.
