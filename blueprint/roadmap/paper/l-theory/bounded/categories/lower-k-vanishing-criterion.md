# Stable criterion for vanishing lower $K$

For $j\ge1$: $K_{-j}(B)=0$ iff for every object $P$ of $\mathrm{Kar}\,C_{\mathbb Z^j}(B)$ there are objects $F,F'$ of $C_{\mathbb Z^j}(B)$ with $P\oplus F\cong F'$.

*Proof sketch.* (⇒) [Equality in $K_0$](k-zero-stable-equality.md) applied to $[P]=[0]$. (⇐) $[P]=[F']-[F]$, and both terms are $0$ by [the bounded swindle](bounded-swindle.md), applied in $C_\mathbb Z(C_{\mathbb Z^{j-1}}B)$.
