# The bounded subcategory

For a full subcategory $U\subset A$, the *bounded subcategory* $C_\mathbb Z(U)\subset C_\mathbb Z(A)$ of the [bounded category](bounded-category.md) is the full subcategory of the objects $M$ with every $M_n$ in $U$.
