# The induced duality on $C_\mathbb Z(A)$

Let $A$ have strict duality $*$. Put $(M^*)_n=(M_n)^*$ and $(f^*)_{nm}=(f_{mn})^*$. Then $f^*$ has the same propagation as $f$, and $M^{**}=M$, $f^{**}=f$ hold on the nose. So $C_\mathbb Z(A)$ is a $\mathbb Q$-linear additive category with strict duality.
