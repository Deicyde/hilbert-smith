# Isomorphism classes of objects

Let $\mathcal C$ be a preadditive category with finite direct sums. Its *isomorphism classes of objects* form a commutative monoid: write $[X]$ for the class of $X$, so that $[X]=[Y]$ if and only if $X\cong Y$; the sum is $[X]+[Y]=[X\oplus Y]$, well defined because direct sums of isomorphisms are isomorphisms, and the zero is the class of a zero object. Associativity, commutativity and the unit laws hold because $\oplus$ is associative, commutative and unital up to isomorphism.
