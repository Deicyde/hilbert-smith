# $K_0$ of an idempotent completion

$K_0(\mathrm{Kar}\,A)$ is the Grothendieck group of the commutative monoid of isomorphism classes of objects of $\mathrm{Kar}\,A$ under $\oplus$. Write $[P]$ for the class of $P$.
