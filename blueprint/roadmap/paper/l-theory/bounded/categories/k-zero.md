# $K_0$ of an idempotent completion

$K_0(\mathrm{Kar}\,A)$ is the Grothendieck group of the commutative monoid of [isomorphism classes of objects](classes/iso-classes.md) of $\mathrm{Kar}\,A$ under $\oplus$.
