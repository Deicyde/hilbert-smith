# Bounded extension of a duality functor

A duality-preserving additive functor $F:A\to A'$ induces the duality-preserving functor $C_\mathbb Z(F):C_\mathbb Z(A)\to C_\mathbb Z(A')$, defined by $(M_n)\mapsto(FM_n)$ and $(f_{mn})\mapsto(Ff_{mn})$. Propagation is preserved, and $C_\mathbb Z(GF)=C_\mathbb Z(G)C_\mathbb Z(F)$.
