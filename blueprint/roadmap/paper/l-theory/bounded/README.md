# Bounded categories and lower K-theory

For an additive category $\mathcal A$ with duality, $C_{\mathbb Z}(\mathcal A)$ has $\mathbb Z$-graded objects and morphisms of bounded propagation. Iterating gives $C_{\mathbb Z^j}(\mathcal A)$ and the lower K-groups. Half-lines are flasque, so their $K_0$ and $L^p$ vanish.
