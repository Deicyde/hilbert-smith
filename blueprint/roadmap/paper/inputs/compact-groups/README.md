# $\mathbb{Z}_p$ in compact groups

The group step of the main theorems reaches a compact Hausdorff group with no nontrivial element of finite order, and needs a copy of $\mathbb{Z}_p$ inside it. That is the input of this appendix.

- [$\mathbb Z_p$ inside a compact torsion-free group](zp-in-torsion-free.md)

The closure of a cyclic subgroup reduces it to the commutative case, which follows from Pontryagin duality for compact commutative groups: extend a nontrivial character to a homomorphism of the dual into the $p$-power roots of unity, and realise its $p$-adic powers as points of the group.

- [$\mathbb Z_p$ inside a compact torsion-free abelian group](zp-in-compact-abelian.md)

Duality enters in three places. Characters separate points; a character of the dual of a discrete group is an evaluation; and together these realise every homomorphism on the dual of a compact group as evaluation at a point. Finally, continuity into a compact commutative group can be tested on characters.
