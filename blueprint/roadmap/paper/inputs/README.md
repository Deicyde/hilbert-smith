# Appendices: the cited inputs

Four classical results are cited rather than proved: Newman's theorem on periodic maps of manifolds in two forms (fixing an open set, and small orbits), the embedding of $\mathbb{Z}_p$ in a compact torsion-free group, and the Gleason–Yamabe theorem. Each appendix states its input exactly as the main text uses it, as an open statement: a theorem that the Lean states now and proves later, with its planned route in a remark. Of these four, $p$-adic exclusion rests only on Newman's theorem on fixing an open set (it also rests on four open statements inside the chapters); Hilbert–Smith rests on all four. Two further open statements, that characters separate points and Pontryagin duality for discrete groups, appear only on the sketched route to the embedding of $\mathbb{Z}_p$, and no main theorem rests on them. Where a route to the input is sketched, its articles are marked as not ready: they fix statements and glue only, and their decomposition comes later.
