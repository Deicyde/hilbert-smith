# Appendices: the cited inputs

Three results are cited rather than proved: Newman's theorem on periodic maps of manifolds, the embedding of $\mathbb{Z}_p$ in a compact torsion-free group, and the Gleason–Yamabe theorem. Each appendix states its input exactly as the main text uses it, as an open statement: a theorem that the Lean states now and proves later, with its planned route in a remark. The main theorems rest on these open statements until they are proved. Where a route to the input is sketched, its articles are marked as not ready: they fix statements and glue only, and their decomposition comes later.
