# Fixed sphere lattice

Let $d \geq 3$ be odd with orientation class $u_d$. There is a
positive power of $2$, $L_d = 2^{e_d}$, such that
$E_d(S^d) \otimes \Q = \Q\bar u_d$ and the integral image satisfies
$\im(E_d(S^d) \to E_d(S^d) \otimes \Q) \subseteq
L_d^{-1}\Z\bar u_d$. This denominator is fixed by the sphere
dimension and comparison, independent of every later action, test
map, character partition, prime, and exponent.

*Proof sketch.* Bounded comparison makes the comparison a rational
isomorphism, identifying $\bar u_d$ with the nonzero rational
orientation class. Since $L_d$ annihilates the comparison cokernel
in degree $d$, every integral class times $L_d$ lifts to the
semialgebraic side, where its rational image is an integral
multiple of the orientation class.
