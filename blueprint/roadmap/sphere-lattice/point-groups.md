# Point groups

For every integer $r$, the comparison $F_r(\pt) \to E_r(\pt)$ is an
isomorphism, and both equal $\Z$ for $r \equiv 0 \pmod 4$ and $0$
otherwise. In degree zero the isomorphism is signature. On $S^0$
both theories are the direct sum of two point theories, and the map
to the point is addition.

*Proof sketch.* Both point categories are exactly the bounded
derived category of finite-dimensional real vector spaces. A degree
$r$ form splits into cohomology pairings; off-diagonal terms are
hyperbolic pairs, odd $r$ leaves nothing, $r = 2k$ with $k$ odd is
alternating hence hyperbolic, and $r = 4m$ is a real symmetric form
whose class is its signature. Signature respects neutral relations
because a lagrangian triangle exhibits the image as self-perpendicular
in middle degree.
