# Signature on a finite relation

Every finite collection of objects of $\Pcat(S^d)$ has
finite-dimensional locally constant cohomology on a common dense
open subset of $S^d$. On a sufficiently small oriented ball there,
a degree-$d$ form has an integral signature on its degree-zero
cohomology, and for any finite Witt relation one ball serves all
objects and lagrangian diagrams simultaneously, so signatures
respect the relation. Consequently $u_{F,d}$ is nontorsion and
$F_d(S^d) \otimes \Q = \Q\bar u_{F,d}$ with integral image exactly
$\Z\bar u_{F,d}$.

*Proof sketch.* Hardt triviality plus cell refinement gives a dense
open set where each generator has finite locally constant
cohomology; the property passes through cones since extensions of
finite local systems split on small balls. On such a ball the
complex is a finite constant complex, duality becomes ordinary
derived linear duality, and a lagrangian triangle gives an ordinary
lagrangian, hence signature zero. Clearing denominators against the
ball signature shows every rational coefficient is integral.
