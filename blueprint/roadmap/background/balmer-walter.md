# Balmer--Walter localization

For a saturated full triangulated duality-stable subcategory with
essentially small categories, bijective multiplication by $2$ on
morphism groups, and the enriched octahedral axiom, there is a long
exact sequence of triangular Witt groups for every shifted duality,
with the boundary given by compatible symmetric cones. A symmetric
form in the quotient is isometric to the image of a symmetric
morphism whose cone lies in the kernel category.

Cited to produce the Witt localization sequence from the support
characterization; the paper verifies the hypotheses and the
naturality of the boundary map itself.
