# Classical reduction

A locally compact group acting faithfully and continuously on a
connected manifold, if not a Lie group, contains a topologically
embedded copy of $\Zp$ for some prime $p$. Restriction to the
embedded subgroup retains joint continuity and faithfulness.

Cited for the final step from $p$-adic exclusion to the full
Hilbert--Smith conjecture. The underlying structure theory is
Gleason, Montgomery--Zippin, and Yamabe; the action-specific form
is Pardon's with Lee's treatment of the reduction.
