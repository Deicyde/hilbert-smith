# Serre odd-sphere maps

For an odd sphere $S^d$: the higher homotopy groups $\pi_j(S^d)$,
$j > d$, are finite; and if $qx = 0$ in a homotopy group, then
postcomposition with a degree-$2q$ map kills $x$, in every
homotopy degree.

Cited for the mapping-telescope computation identifying the
telescope with $K(\Q,d)$ in the odd-sphere nullhomotopy argument.
