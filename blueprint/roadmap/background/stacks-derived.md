# Stacks derived facts

Bounded-below proper base change for closed separated maps with
compact fibers; countable sheaf sums are exact and termwise sums
compute coproducts in the derived category; the inclusions of
bounded and bounded-below derived categories into the ambient
derived category are fully faithful.

Cited for the orbit pullback vanishing, the derived character sum,
and coproduct detection of morphisms between bounded objects.
