# Cech comparison

On compact Hausdorff spaces, the sheaf--Cech comparison is an
isomorphism commuting with pullback; for finite covers, Cech
cohomology is the direct limit of nerve cohomologies, and the map
from a nerve is pullback by the nerve map, independent of the
subordinate partition.

Cited for rational orbit-cohomology detection and for reducing the
odd-sphere nullhomotopy to a finite complex.
