# Closed subgroups of $\Zp$

Every nonzero closed subgroup of $\Zp$ is open, of the form
$p^j\Zp$: an element of smallest finite $p$-adic valuation has
integer multiples dense in that subgroup. Consequently a quotient
$\Zp/H$ by a closed subgroup is a point, a finite quotient, or
$\Zp$ itself, and a nonzero kernel in $\Zp$ makes the action factor
through a finite quotient.

Standard fact; the paper sketches the proof where it is used.
