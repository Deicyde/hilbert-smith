# Hardt triviality

A continuous semialgebraic map from a compact polyhedron admits a
finite semialgebraic partition of the target over whose pieces the
map is trivial; fibers are compact semialgebraic sets with the
finite cohomology of a finite triangulation.

Cited to find a common dense open set where finitely many
semialgebraic generators have finite locally constant cohomology,
for the generic signature evaluation.
