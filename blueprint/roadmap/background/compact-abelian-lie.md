# Compact abelian Lie groups

A compact abelian Lie group has identity component a torus of
finite index, and the exponential map of a torus is surjective.

Cited for the almost-periodic flow consequence: the compact closure
of an almost-periodic homeomorphism, once known to be a compact
abelian Lie group, puts some positive iterate on a one-parameter
subgroup.
