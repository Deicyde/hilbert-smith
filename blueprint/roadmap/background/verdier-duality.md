# Verdier duality formalism

For locally compact spaces and maps of finite $c$-soft dimension:
$Rf_!$ has right adjoint $f^!$; composition identifies
$f^!\omega_Y$ with $\omega_X$; the counit gives the trace
$Rf_!\omega_X \to \omega_Y$; the internal duality isomorphism
$Rf_*\D_X A \xrightarrow{\sim} \D_Y Rf_!A$ holds, adjoint to
evaluation followed by the trace and compatible with evaluation
maps; for an open inclusion $j$, $j^! = j^{-1}$, so duality
commutes with open restriction. None of this asserts reflexivity
of every bounded complex.

Cited throughout the sheaf-category chapters for duality,
pushforward, and product computations.
