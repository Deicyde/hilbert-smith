# Sheaf cohomology bounds

Finite $c$-soft dimension and its consequences: $R\Gamma_c(\R^N;-)$
has no cohomology above $N$; soft resolutions and their acyclicity
on compact Hausdorff spaces, so sheaf cohomology there commutes
with direct sums and sums of soft sheaves are soft.

Cited for the finite-dimensionality hypotheses on allowed maps and
for the derived character-sum computation.
