# Main theorems

The goal of the book: a locally compact group acting faithfully and
continuously on a connected manifold must be a Lie group. The paper
reduces this to excluding faithful actions of the $p$-adic integers,
proves that exclusion by contradiction on an invariant chart subset,
and records a dynamical consequence for almost-periodic
homeomorphisms.
