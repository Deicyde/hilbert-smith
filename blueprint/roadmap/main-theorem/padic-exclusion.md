# $p$-adic exclusion

Let $n \geq 1$ and $p$ a prime. Every jointly continuous action of
$\Zp$ on a connected Hausdorff second-countable topological
$n$-manifold without boundary has nonzero kernel. Consequently the
action factors through a finite quotient of $\Zp$.

*Proof sketch.* If the action were faithful, the invariant chart
subset would carry a faithful orientation-preserving action of an
open subgroup isomorphic to $\Zp$, contradicting the chart
contradiction. The kernel is closed as an intersection of point
stabilizers, and every nonzero closed subgroup of $\Zp$ is open, so
the action factors through a finite quotient. No compactness, global
orientability, or triangulability of the manifold is used.
