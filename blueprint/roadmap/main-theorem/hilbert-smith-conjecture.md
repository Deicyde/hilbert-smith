# Hilbert--Smith conjecture

Let $n \geq 1$. A locally compact second-countable Hausdorff group
acting faithfully and jointly continuously on a connected Hausdorff
second-countable topological $n$-manifold without boundary is a Lie
group.

*Proof sketch.* The classical Hilbert--Smith reduction says a locally
compact group acting faithfully and continuously on a connected
manifold, if not a Lie group, contains a topologically embedded copy
of $\Zp$ for some prime $p$. Restricting the action to that subgroup
keeps joint continuity and faithfulness, contradicting $p$-adic
exclusion. Hence the group is a Lie group.
