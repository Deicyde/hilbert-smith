# Almost-periodic flow

Let $M$ be a manifold as in the Hilbert--Smith conjecture and $f$ an
almost-periodic homeomorphism of $M$: the cyclic subgroup generated
by $f$ has compact closure $K$ in $\Homeo(M)$. Then some positive
iterate of $f$ is the time-one map of a continuous real flow: there
are an integer $m \geq 1$ and a continuous homomorphism
$\Phi : \R \to \Homeo(M)$ with $\Phi(1) = f^m$.

*Proof sketch.* The compact abelian group $K$ acts faithfully and
jointly continuously on $M$. Evaluation on a countable dense subset
of $M$ embeds $K$ into a countable product of copies of $M$, so $K$
is second countable. By the Hilbert--Smith conjecture it is a compact
abelian Lie group; its identity component is a torus of finite index,
so $f^m$ lies in it for some $m \geq 1$, and the surjective
exponential map of the torus places $f^m$ on a one-parameter
subgroup.
