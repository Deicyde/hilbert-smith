# The collision

*How do the divisibility half and the normalization half collide?*

Both proofs end with a few lines of arithmetic.
In HS_proof it is the proof of the HS paper's Theorem 1.1, which sets HS Proposition 13.1 against HS Theorem 4.3 for one class over $T$.
In OpenAI's proof it is the end of the proof of the preprint's Theorem 6.8, which sets the lattice of preprint Theorem 4.7 against $m$ equal parts with a known sum, followed by the proof of preprint Theorem 1.2, which reaches Theorem 6.8 through preprint Proposition 5.1.

## HS_proof

The proof of Theorem 1.1 first reduces to a boundaryless manifold of positive dimension: a connected $0$-manifold is a point; restricting to the interior keeps the action effective, because homeomorphisms preserve the interior and an element fixing the dense interior fixes everything; and the interior is connected, so the boundaryless case applies ([HS paper, lines 1352-1359](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1352-L1359)).
If the action were effective, Proposition 13.1 would turn it into data over one fixed compact free $C_p$-space $T$, namely the groups $G_i$ and a class $\alpha \in L_4(\mathcal{A}_{\mathbf{G}}(T))$, with tail signature $1$ ([HS paper, lines 1328-1335](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1328-L1335)).
Theorem 4.3 speaks about every class over the same $T$ and the same $G_i$, so the same signatures are eventually divisible by $p$ ([HS paper, lines 367-369](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L367-L369)).
No integer sequence is both eventually $1$ and eventually divisible by $p$ ([HS paper, lines 1361-1365](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1361-L1365)).

"Eventually" is built into the invariant.
The tail signature takes values in $\mathbb{Z}^{\mathbb{N}}/\mathbb{Z}^{(\mathbb{N})}$, integer sequences modulo finitely supported ones ([HS paper, lines 309-314](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L309-L314)).
Why HS_proof needs a whole sequence of finite quotients, and so compares sequences only eventually ([HS paper, lines 134-137](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L134-L137)), is explained in [The group that carries the invariant](the-invariant.md).

The order of choices matters here too, but not for integrality.
$T$, the detector and the buffers are fixed first, and only then the subgroups $K_i$ ([HS paper, lines 829-846](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L829-L846)); the cover of $T/C_p$ is fixed independently of $i$ ([lines 350-351](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L350-L351)); the cuts are fixed before the index varies ([lines 1164-1166](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1164-L1166)).
These are control data: they keep the asymptotic control uniform in $i$ and the exponent $s$ in $\nu^s = 0$ fixed.
The signatures are integers whatever is chosen ([HS paper, lines 255-257](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L255-L257)), and the divisor is $p$ for every $i$ ([lines 367-380](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L367-L380)).

## OpenAI's proof

Theorem 6.8 says that an orientation-preserving continuous $\mathbb{Z}_p$-action on a connected open subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$ has nonzero kernel ([preprint, Theorem 6.8](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L445-L449)).
Suppose it is faithful; choose an odd $d > n$ with $d \geq 3$ and form the test data of Section 5, possibly after passing to an open subgroup ([preprint, §6, lines 451-455](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L451-L455)).

The order of choices drives the arithmetic.
First fix the lattice: Theorem 4.7 gives a power of $2$, $L_d$, such that $E_d(S^d) \otimes \mathbb{Q} = \mathbb{Q}\overline{u}_d$ and the image of $E_d(S^d)$ lies in $L_d^{-1}\mathbb{Z}\overline{u}_d$; it depends only on the sphere comparison ([preprint, §6, lines 457-464](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L457-L464)).
Only then choose $k$ with $m = p^k > 4L_d$, and after that the region $V$, the multiplier $\beta$ and the partition ([preprint, §6, lines 464-467](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L464-L467)).
The introduction states the rule outright: $L_d$ is fixed before $k$ ([preprint, §1, lines 176-181](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex#L176-L181)).

Then count.
Equal parts make the rational images $\overline{z}_i(a)$ all equal ([preprint, §6, lines 469-484](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L469-L484)), their sum is $4\overline{u}_d$ ([lines 486-492](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L486-L492)), and so each is $(4/m)\overline{u}_d$ ([line 493](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L493)).
Each $z_i(a)$ is integral, so $\overline{z}_i(a) \in L_d^{-1}\mathbb{Z}\overline{u}_d$, which forces $4L_d/m \in \mathbb{Z}$; but $0 < 4L_d/m < 1$ ([preprint, §6, lines 496-499](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L496-L499)).
Reading off a coefficient uses $\overline{u}_d \neq 0$, which Propositions 4.5 and 4.6 and the proof of Theorem 4.7 supply ([preprint, §4, lines 388-391](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L388-L391)).
The argument works for every prime, $p = 2$ included ([§6, lines 498-499](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L498-L499)).

The proof of Theorem 1.2 reaches this situation from a faithful action on a manifold of any positive dimension: Proposition 5.1 gives a faithful orientation-preserving action of an open subgroup, itself isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}_p$, on a connected invariant open subset of one chart, which Theorem 6.8 rules out ([preprint, §6, lines 502-507](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L502-L507)).
So the kernel is nonzero; it is closed, and a nonzero closed subgroup of $\mathbb{Z}_p$ is open, so the action factors through a finite quotient ([§6, lines 507-512](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L507-L512)).

## What is shared

- Both fix some data before the group order grows: OpenAI's proof fixes the integrality bound $L_d$ before $k$ ([preprint, §6, lines 457-467](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L457-L467)); HS_proof fixes $T$, the detector, the buffers, the cover and the cut flags before the $K_i$ ([HS paper, lines 829-846](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L829-L846), [lines 350-351](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L350-L351), [lines 1164-1166](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1164-L1166)). What the advance choice is for differs; see the first item below.
- The same shape of contradiction: a class of fixed nonzero size cannot be divisible by an amount that the $p$-adic symmetry forces. HS_proof sets signature $1$ against $p$ ([HS paper, lines 1361-1364](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1361-L1364)); OpenAI sets the total $4\overline{u}_d$ against $m$ ([preprint, §6, lines 493-498](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L493-L498)).

## What differs

- Which number grows. HS_proof sets a fixed divisor $p$ against a signature exactly $1$; its advance choices are control data, needed for uniform control along $i$ and for nilpotence, $p$ divides the signatures for every $i$ ([HS paper, lines 367-380](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L367-L380)), and its integers, ordinary signatures, need no advance choice ([lines 255-257](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L255-L257)). OpenAI sets a growing divisor $m = p^k$ against a fixed numerator $4L_d$; $m > 4L_d$ is available only because $L_d$ was fixed before $k$ ([preprint, §6, lines 464-465](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L464-L465)).
- One class along a sequence, or $m$ classes at one level. HS_proof collides one equivariant class over a free $C_p$-space $T$, read as a tail along the quotients $G_i$ ([HS paper, lines 1328-1335](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1328-L1335)). OpenAI collides $m$ classes in $E_d(S^d)$ at a single $k$ ([preprint, §6, lines 396-402](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L396-L402)).
- Integer or lattice. HS_proof reads an exact integer, so the collision is $1 \in p\mathbb{Z}$. OpenAI reads a rational coefficient with denominator dividing $L_d$, so the collision is $4L_d/m \in \mathbb{Z}$ with $0 < 4L_d/m < 1$ ([preprint, §6, lines 496-498](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L496-L498)).
- What surrounds the collision. HS_proof handles boundary and dimension $0$ inside the proof of HS Theorem 1.1 ([HS paper, lines 1352-1359](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1352-L1359)). OpenAI's Theorem 6.8 needs an orientation-preserving action on an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$, $n \geq 1$, which preprint Proposition 5.1 supplies for preprint Theorem 1.2 ([preprint, §6, lines 502-507](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L502-L507)).
