# Two routes: the HS_proof and OpenAI proofs side by side

This chapter reads two proofs of the Hilbert–Smith conjecture against
each other. HS_proof is A. Dabrowski's AI agents, *Integral tail
signatures and the Hilbert–Smith conjecture* (October 8, 2026; "the HS
paper"). OpenAI's proof is OpenAI, *The Hilbert–Smith conjecture in
every finite dimension* (September 23, 2026; "the preprint"), the proof
the rest of this book follows. Each page takes one question that both
proofs must answer, gives each proof's answer in its own terms, and says
what is shared, what differs, and where to read further. The table below
gives the whole comparison, one sentence per cell; the pages give the
arguments.

Both proofs aim at the same target and start the same way. Each reduces
Hilbert–Smith to $p$-adic exclusion, the statement that $\mathbb{Z}_p$
cannot act effectively on a connected manifold, and argues by
contradiction from an effective action (HS paper,
[lines 85-101](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L85-L101);
preprint,
[Theorems 1.1 and 1.2](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex#L9-L35)).
Each then works in one coordinate chart, with an open subgroup
$p^j\mathbb{Z}_p$ small enough, by joint continuity and compactness, to
keep a ball inside the chart; gets an invariant test map of degree one
by Haar averaging over a small open subgroup (HS_proof averages the
chart coordinates over $p^j\mathbb{Z}_p$ itself and then pinches the
average onto a sphere; OpenAI's proof pinches first and averages the
sphere-valued map over a smaller open subgroup); and finds a
region where a finite cyclic quotient of the group acts freely, with a
coordinate there that transforms by a character of the quotient (HS
paper,
[lines 782-830](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L782-L830);
preprint,
[Proposition 5.1](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/05-local-tests.tex#L14-L73),
[Lemma 5.3](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/05-local-tests.tex#L135-L183)
and
[Lemma 5.6](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/05-local-tests.tex#L386-L434)).

From there they part. HS_proof reads its test in ultimate lower
$L$-theory of controlled categories of modules over the rational group
rings $\mathbb{Q}[G_i]$ of a sequence of finite quotients (HS paper,
[lines 140-167](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L140-L167)).
OpenAI's proof reads it in shifted Witt groups of real sheaf complexes
generated by proper direct images of constant sheaves on compact
polyhedra, with Verdier duality; these sheaves need not be constructible
(preprint,
[lines 154-205](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex#L154-L205)).
The collisions differ too. HS_proof builds one equivariant class whose
signature tail is eventually divisible by $p$ and constantly $1$, so
$1 \in p\mathbb{Z}$ (HS paper,
[lines 1361-1365](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1361-L1365)).
OpenAI's proof builds $m = p^k$ integral classes with equal rational
images summing to $4\bar{u}_d$, four times the rational orientation
class of the sphere $S^d$, in a lattice whose denominator $L_d$ was
fixed before $k$, so $m$ would divide $4L_d$, which fails once
$m > 4L_d$ (preprint,
[Theorem 6.8](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L445-L500)).

## Statements and second countability

The first and third rows differ in hypotheses more than in substance.
HS_proof assumes no countability, allows boundary and covers dimension 0
(HS paper,
[lines 85-99](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L85-L99)
and
[1352-1359](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1352-L1359)).
OpenAI assumes the group and the manifold second countable, no boundary
and $n \geq 1$, and adds that the action factors through a finite
quotient, which holds because the kernel is closed and nonzero closed
subgroups of $\mathbb{Z}_p$ are open (preprint,
[Theorems 1.1 and 1.2](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex#L9-L35),
[lines 507-512](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L507-L512)).
On connected, Hausdorff, second-countable manifolds of positive
dimension without boundary, the two papers' statements say the same
thing:

- [The two statements agree](same-statement.md)

Second countability of the manifold costs neither proof anything. The HS
paper removes it by its invariant exhaustion. The preprint keeps it in
its statements, but after Proposition 5.1 everything happens in an open
subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$ or $\mathbb{R}^d$, where it holds automatically
(preprint,
[lines 52-66](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/05-local-tests.tex#L52-L66)
and
[line 118](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/05-local-tests.tex#L118)).
For the group, the HS paper passes to compact subgroups, which act
through metrizable quotients (HS paper,
[lines 1368-1381](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1368-L1381));
the preprint assumes the hypothesis and hands the group to the cited
reduction
