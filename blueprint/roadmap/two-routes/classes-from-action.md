# Turning the action into classes

*How does the group action on the manifold become an operator on an object of the invariant's category, and how are classes cut out of it?*

Both proofs end this stage with classes in their invariant built from a projector that the action defines.
In HS_proof the stage is the geometric bulk of the paper: chain models and compression in Sections 7, 9 and 10, then the graph corner of Section 11 with the algebra of Section 5.2; its output is one equivariant class $\mathfrak{d}$ over $T \times S^n$ whose scalar image is the manifold germ $\mathfrak{m}_f$.
In OpenAI's proof it is the first half of Section 6; its output is a family of classes $z_i(t) \in E_d(X)$, one for each part of a partition of the characters, for every admissible test $t$.

## HS_proof

To get a class from $G_i$, HS_proof needs $G_i$ to act, compatibly with duality, on one object of its category, so that averaging over the group gives a projector whose image is the class.
It has only representatives $h_g$ that act on the chart and compose up to elements of $K_i$ ([HS paper, lines 891-898](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L891-L898)).
So it passes to chain complexes of fine triangulations of $S^n \times \mathbb{CP}^2$, where each representative gives a chain map that is small in the $f$-coordinate and multiplies correctly up to small homotopies; it compresses away everything far from where $f$ is nonconstant, so that every defect lands in the exterior the quotient ignores; and it spreads each complex over free sheets of $\mathbb{Q}[G_i]$, labelled through the detector, so that the group acts on free modules and the average becomes a homotopy idempotent compatible with duality.
Splitting it gives the equivariant class $\mathfrak{d}$; forgetting the group and $T$ turns it into $\mathfrak{m}_f$, the class of the triangulated $S^n \times \mathbb{CP}^2$ labelled through $f$ ([HS paper, lines 1035-1037](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1035-L1037), [lines 1139-1146](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1139-L1146)).

The models are triangulations $\Sigma_i$ of $S^n \times \mathbb{CP}^2$ of mesh $\eta_i \to 0$, where the sphere is the compactified coordinate space of the chart ([HS paper, lines 850-858](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L850-L858); the paper writes $Y_i$).
Their rational chain complexes $D_i$ carry strict symmetric cap-product dualities whose inverse homotopies have propagation of order $\eta_i$ ([HS paper, Lemma 9.1](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L860-L878)), built with the acyclic-carrier method of Section 7 ([HS paper, lines 577-603](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L577-L603), [lines 731-753](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L731-L753)).

Each $h \in H$ is completed by a cutoff to a self-map $\widehat h$ of the sphere, equal to $h$ near the free point and to the identity far away; these maps are equicontinuous, have degree one, and preserve the control map, $f\widehat h = f$ ([HS paper, lines 880-891](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L880-L891)).
Representatives $h_g$ of $G_i$ give chain approximations $a_g$ of graph type $g$: their propagation measured through $f$ tends to $0$, and on each compact set $f^{-1}(\overline{B}(y,v))$ they move the detector value by $\chi(g)$ ([HS paper, lines 900-913](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L900-L913)).
The $a_g$ are simplicial approximations on fixed complexes, and the representatives compose only up to $K_i$, so the relations $a_ga_h = a_{gh}$ and $a_g\phi = \phi a_{g^{-1}}^*$ hold only up to controlled homotopies, uniformly in $i$, $g$ and $h$: Lemma 9.2 for products, whose straight-line tracks absorb the $K_i$ error ([HS paper, Lemma 9.2](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L916-L946)), and two further homotopies for the duality ([lines 948-975](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L948-L975)).
Uniformity in $g$ matters because the orders of the $G_i$ grow without bound ([HS paper, lines 841-842](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L841-L842), [lines 1121-1123](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1121-L1123)).
Degree one of the completed maps makes each $a_g$ fix the fundamental cycle, which the duality homotopies use ([HS paper, lines 909-913](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L909-L913), [lines 956-961](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L956-L961)); [One chart and a small subgroup](one-chart.md) explains why small displacement gives degree one.

Compression keeps the simplices that meet $f^{-1}(\overline{B(y,t)})$: the far simplices span a subcomplex $F_i$, the compressed complex is $C_i = D_i/F_i$, and the compressed maps are $A_g = \pi a_g \sigma$, with $\pi$ the projection and $\sigma$ its based section ([HS paper, lines 984-991](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L984-L991), [lines 1028-1034](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1028-L1034)).
Each defect of the compressed relations is a word that passes through a far simplex, and the band lemma factors such words through objects supported in the fixed exterior ([HS paper, Lemma 10.1](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L998-L1026)).
Proposition 10.2 makes $C_i$ Poincaré in $\mathcal{B}_{\mathbf{1},Z}(T \times S^n)$, with the homotopy relations valid modulo the exterior, and its class after forgetting $T$ is $\mathfrak{m}_f$ ([HS paper, lines 1039-1045](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1039-L1045)).

The group then acts on free sheets.
On $\mathcal{C}_i = C_i \otimes \mathbb{Q}[G_i]$, sheet $s$ of a retained simplex is labelled by $\chi(s)$ times its detector value, together with its value under $f$ ([HS paper, lines 1104-1110](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1104-L1110)); right multiplication $R_g$ on the sheets then cancels the detector rotation of a map of graph type $g$ ([HS paper, lines 1019-1022](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1019-L1022)).
With $\Phi = \widehat\phi \otimes 1$ the duality copied sheetwise, the average $E = \lvert G_i \rvert^{-1}\sum_g A_g \otimes R_{g^{-1}}$ satisfies $E^2 \simeq E$ and $E\Phi \simeq \Phi E^*$ in the controlled exterior category ([HS paper, lines 1113-1127](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1113-L1127)): averaging over a group that acts up to homotopy gives an idempotent up to homotopy.
The algebra behind this, in Section 5.2, assumes only an action in the homotopy category, with no higher coherence ([HS paper, lines 416-436](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L416-L436)).

Lemma 5.1 splits $E$ in the bounded homotopy category of the idempotent completion, which is idempotent complete by Balmer–Schlichting, and the corner $D$ inherits a strict symmetric duality and an L-class ([HS paper, lines 438-461](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L438-L461)); this gives $\mathfrak{d} \in L_{n+4}(\mathcal{B}_{\mathbf{G},Z}(T \times S^n))$ ([HS paper, lines 1128-1137](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1128-L1137)).
Let $\mathcal{U}$ forget the group and $T$.
Lemma 5.2 shows that $\mathcal{U}$ carries the corner to a complex homotopy-isometric to the original one, because the averaged duality $\sum_b A_b\phi A_b^*$ is homotopic to $\lvert G_i \rvert\phi$ ([HS paper, lines 487-511](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L487-L511)); hence $\mathcal{U}_*\mathfrak{d} = \mathfrak{m}_f$ ([HS paper, lines 1139-1146](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1139-L1146)).

## OpenAI's proof

$G = \mathbb{Z}_p$ acts on $W^+$, so it acts on the orbit sheaf $\mathcal{H} = q_*\mathbb{C}_{W^+}$ on $Y = W^+/G$; with $\gamma$ the action of $1 \in G$ (the preprint's $T$), put $\mathcal{H}_\lambda = \ker(\gamma - M_\lambda)$ for $\lambda$ in the group $\Lambda$ of $p$-power roots of unity ([preprint, §6, lines 25-35](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L25-L35)).
Lemma 6.1 gives $Rq_*\mathbb{C}_{W^+} \simeq \mathcal{H} \simeq \bigoplus_\lambda \mathcal{H}_\lambda$, compatibly with $Rt_*$ for every continuous $t$ into a compact Hausdorff space ([preprint, Lemma 6.1](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L37-L99)).

For a compact test $t : Y \to X$ whose base value $b_t = t(y_\infty)$ has contractible neighbourhoods, the test object is $A_t = Rf_{t*}\mathbb{C}_{S^d}$ with $f_t = tqc$, and $\alpha_t$ pushes forward the orientation pairing of $S^d$ with coefficient form $B(z,w) = \operatorname{Re}(z\bar w)$ ([preprint, §6, lines 101-120](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L101-L120)).
Here $\mathbb{C}$ is the real plane with its complex scalars ([preprint, §6, lines 15-16](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L15-L16)): the sheaves and pairings stay real, while multiplication by a unit complex number preserves $B$ ([preprint, §6, line 163](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L163)), so complex characters can act by real isometries.
Away from $b_t$, $A_t$ is $Rt_*\mathcal{H}$, and its form is the direct image of the orientation pairing of an oriented open subset of $W$; no duality on $Y$ is used ([preprint, §6, lines 122-134](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L122-L134)).

Lemma 6.2 says that character parts with disjoint index sets are orthogonal, so the projectors of a finite partition are self-adjoint ([preprint, Lemma 6.2](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L150-L203)).
The orientation-preserving action acts by isometries, so $\gamma^\dagger = \gamma^{-1}$, and unit scalars preserve $B$, so $M_\lambda^\dagger = M_{\lambda^{-1}}$; then $\gamma - M_\lambda$ kills the $\lambda$-part while $\gamma^{-1} - M_{\lambda^{-1}}$ is invertible on the other parts, which forces the cross pairing to vanish ([preprint, §6, lines 160-192](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L160-L192)).

The projectors live only over $X \setminus \{b_t\}$ and need not extend to summands at $b_t$ ([preprint, §1, lines 266-272](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex#L266-L272)).
In $\mathcal{Q}_U(X) = \mathcal{T}(X)/\mathcal{T}(U)$, for a contractible open $U \ni b_t$, the germ theorem turns them into endomorphisms $e_i$ of $A_t$ with $e_ie_j = \delta_{ij}e_i$, $\sum_i e_i = 1$ and $e_i^\dagger = e_i$ ([preprint, §6, lines 210-223](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L210-L223)).
Definition 6.3 lifts $[A_t,\alpha_t] + [A_t,\alpha_t \circ (2e_i - 1)]$ through the integral isomorphism $\ell_U$ to a class $z_i(t) \in E_d(X)$ ([preprint, Definition 6.3](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L225-L236)).
Since $2e_i - 1$ is a self-adjoint involution, the twisted form is a form; if $e_i$ did split orthogonally, the sum would double the selected part and cancel the rest, but no image object is needed, and the class does not depend on $U$ ([preprint, §6, lines 238-250](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L238-L250)).

One matrix isometry gives $\sum_i z_i(t) = 2[A_t,\alpha_t]$ ([preprint, Proposition 6.4](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L252-L291)).
The classes are covariant, $g_*z_i(t) = z_i(gt)$ ([preprint, Proposition 6.5](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L295-L343)).
Homotopic tests into $S^d$ have equal classes ([preprint, Proposition 6.6](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L345-L392)), but this needs its own argument, because $z_i(t)$ is built in a quotient that discards a neighbourhood of $b_t = t(y_\infty)$, and a homotopy may move $b_t$ ([§6, lines 351-352](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L351-L352)).
The proof packs two nearby stages $t_0, t_1$ into one test $(t_0, t_1) : Y \to C_d$, where $C_d = \{(x_0, x_1) \in S^d \times S^d : \langle x_0, x_1 \rangle \geq 0\}$ is a compact $2d$-manifold with boundary ([§6, lines 354-367](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L354-L367)); its two projections to $S^d$ are homotopic along normalized segments, so covariance along each and homotopy invariance of $E_d$ (Theorem 2.9) give $z_i(t_0) = z_i(t_1)$, and a fine subdivision of the homotopy chains such steps ([§6, lines 369-391](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L369-L391)).

## Side by side

|  | HS_proof | OpenAI's proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idea | a homotopy action of each $G_i$ on chain models, averaged over induced sheets into a duality-compatible homotopy idempotent, split after idempotent completion | character projectors of the orbit sheaf, pushed into $X$ and read as germs in a quotient, then the test form twisted by $2e_i - 1$ |
| Inputs | acyclic carriers and simplicial approximation (Hatcher, Theorem 2C.1); idempotent completeness (Balmer–Schlichting, Theorem 2.8); invariance under idempotent completion (Ranicki, *Lower K- and L-theory*, Definition 17.7); Lemmas 5.1, 5.2, 9.2; Proposition 10.2 | Lemma 5.4; proper base change; Theorems 2.9 and 3.3; Proposition 4.9; Lemmas 6.1 and 6.2 |

## What is shared

- Both turn the action into a projector compatible with duality: HS_proof's averaged idempotent with $E\Phi \simeq \Phi E^*$ ([HS paper, lines 426-434](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L426-L434)), OpenAI's self-adjoint $e_i$ ([preprint, §6, lines 216-222](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L216-L222)).
- Neither needs an image object in the category it starts in: HS_proof splits only after idempotent completion and up to homotopy ([HS paper, lines 438-461](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L438-L461)); OpenAI never splits and twists by the involution $2e_i - 1$ ([preprint, §6, lines 238-244](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L238-L244)).
- Both need the group to act by isometries of the duality, so both need orientation preservation; [One chart and a small subgroup](one-chart.md) compares how each gets it.
- Both work in a quotient that discards a neighbourhood of the image of infinity; [The group that carries the invariant](the-invariant.md) compares HS_proof's exterior with OpenAI's $U$.

## What differs

- HS_proof has only a homotopy action on a combinatorial model and must build it, in Sections 7, 9 and 10 ([HS paper, lines 577-1100](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L577-L1100)). OpenAI's group acts strictly on the orbit sheaf and the decomposition survives direct image ([preprint, §6, lines 25-52](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L25-L52)), so its work sits in the sheaf theory of Sections 2 and 3.
- HS_proof needs the whole sequence $G_i = H/K_i$, for the reason given in [The group that carries the invariant](the-invariant.md). OpenAI uses all of $G$ at once, exactly, and one level $m = p^k$ only to choose the partition ([preprint, §6, lines 396-402](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L396-L402)).
- HS_proof spreads the group over free sheets labelled through the detector; OpenAI diagonalizes it by complex characters, which is why it needs the real coefficient plane with $B(z,w) = \operatorname{Re}(z\bar w)$ ([preprint, §6, lines 163-171](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L163-L171)).
- HS_proof cuts one corner, whose underlying scalar class is the manifold germ ([HS paper, lines 1135-1146](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1135-L1146)); OpenAI cuts $m$ classes, one per part of a partition of the characters, summing to $2[A_t,\alpha_t]$ ([preprint, §6, lines 252-259](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L252-L259)).
- OpenAI's classes are covariant for every admissible test (Proposition 6.5) and homotopy invariant for tests into $S^d$ (Proposition 6.6); HS_proof needs one class for one fixed control map $f$, and changes $f$ only in the boundary evaluation ([HS paper, lines 1300-1304](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1300-L1304)).
