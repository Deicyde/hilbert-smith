# The group that carries the invariant

*In what group does the obstruction live, and which properties of that group does each proof use?*

Both proofs measure the action by a group of symmetric forms modulo neutral ones, but they build it from different objects.
HS_proof uses lower L-theory of controlled categories of modules over the group rings of finite quotients of the acting group; the paper sets it up in Sections 2, 5.1 and 6 and uses it in every later section.
OpenAI's proof uses the Witt group $E_d(X)$ of a category of real sheaves on a test space $X$, set up in Sections 2 and 3 and evaluated on the sphere in Section 4.
The group action sits inside HS_proof's category and outside OpenAI's.

## HS_proof

The control space $X$ needs only an isometric action of $C_p$ on a compact metric space; each finite cyclic group $G_i = C_{p^{a_i}}$ acts on $X$ through its quotient by an index-$p$ subgroup $P_i$, which is $C_p$ ([HS paper, lines 154-156](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L154-L156)).
An object of the asymptotic category $\mathcal{A}_{\mathbf{G}}(X)$ is a sequence, indexed by $i$, of finite free based $\mathbb{Q}[G_i]$-modules whose basis elements carry equivariant labels in $X$.
A morphism is a sequence of equivariant rational matrices whose propagation (the farthest that any nonzero entry reaches from one label to another) tends to $0$, taken modulo eventual equality, and duality transposes matrices ([HS paper, lines 157-167](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L157-L167)).
The control space sees only the quotient $C_p$; the modules see all of $G_i$.

Why a whole sequence of groups?
The $G_i = H/K_i$ are quotients of a small subgroup $H \cong \mathbb{Z}_p$ by $K_i = p^{\ell_i}\mathbb{Z}_p$ with $\ell_i \to \infty$, chosen after all the geometric data ([HS paper, lines 839-846](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L839-L846)).
Chosen representatives $h_g$ multiply only up to elements of $K_i$, so they act on the chart only approximately, with an error bounded by $K_i$-displacements, which tend to $0$ uniformly ([HS paper, lines 845-846](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L845-L846), [lines 891-898](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L891-L898)).
No single $i$ gives an action of $G_i$.
A category whose morphisms are sequences with propagation tending to $0$, compared only eventually ([HS paper, lines 134-137](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L134-L137)), turns relations whose error shrinks with $i$ into exact equations.

Supports come with the objects.
For a closed invariant $S \subset X$, $\mathcal{A}_S$ consists of the objects concentrated near $S$, with all labels uniformly closer to $S$ as $i$ grows, and a closed invariant exterior $Z$ gives the quotient $\mathcal{B}_{\mathbf{G},Z}(X) = \mathcal{A}_{\mathbf{G}}(X)/\mathcal{A}_Z$ ([HS paper, lines 177-184](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L177-L184)).
The support calculus makes each $\mathcal{A}_S$ a Karoubi filtration and gives exact excision for closed covers ([HS paper, Lemma 2.2](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L189-L203)).

The group is ultimate lower quadratic L-theory of these categories, cited from Carlsson–Pedersen and Ranicki.
The paper needs only a few properties of it, each for a job: functoriality and additivity to push and add classes, Karoubi localization with natural connecting maps for the boundary maps of Sections 6 and 12, decoration maps to pass from projective to ultimate groups, and invariance under idempotent completion so that the corner split in Section 5.2 still has a class ([HS paper, lines 140-150](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L140-L150), [lines 476-479](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L476-L479)).
Section 5.1 explains why it may cite quadratic L-theory and still compute with symmetric complexes: over $\mathbb{Q}$ the symmetrization $1 + \mathrm{t}$, with $\mathrm{t}$ the transposition involution (the paper's $T$), is invertible, with inverse given by halving, so a symmetric structure $\phi$ has the quadratic lift $\phi/2$, and a homotopy between symmetric cycles halves the same way ([HS paper, lines 384-414](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L384-L414)).
The references supply the quadratic theory; the chain models produce symmetric structures.

HS_proof uses localization boundaries repeatedly.
A closed invariant cover $X = A \cup B$ gives the ordinary boundary $\partial_{A,B} : L_k(\mathcal{A}(X)) \to L_{k-1}(\mathcal{A}_{A \cap B}(X))$, display (6.1) ([HS paper, lines 513-520](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L513-L520)).
Proposition 6.1 is the exterior boundary: the same map defined on $\mathcal{A}(X)/\mathcal{A}_Z(X)$ when $Z \subset A$ is positively separated from $B$ ([HS paper, lines 522-552](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L522-L552)).
Lemma 6.2 is the union formula that computes these boundaries: for a Poincaré complex glued from pairs over $A$ and $B$ along an interface $P$ over $A \cap B$, the boundary is $[P]$ ([HS paper, lines 554-562](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L554-L562)).
Section 12 applies the boundaries $n$ times to descend from $T \times S^n$ to $T$ ([HS paper, lines 1156-1163](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1156-L1163)).
There is no general homotopy invariance; the homotopy statement used is Lemma 12.3: the chain complexes of one triangulation, labelled by the two ends of a fixed homotopy, are cobordant, also in an exterior quotient ([HS paper, lines 1259-1287](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1259-L1287)).

## OpenAI's proof

For a test space $X$ (a finite polyhedron, a space homeomorphic to one, an open subset of such, or a finite product of these), $\mathcal{T}(X)$ is the closure under summands of the triangulated subcategory of $D^b(\mathrm{Sh}_{\mathbb{R}}(X))$ generated by the direct images $Rf_*\mathbb{R}_B$ along arbitrary continuous maps $f$ from compact polyhedra ([preprint, Definition 2.1](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/02-categories.tex#L10-L57)).
Because $f$ is only continuous, images need not be constructible and stalks can be infinite dimensional ([preprint, §1, lines 154-155](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex#L154-L155), [lines 198-201](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex#L198-L201)).

Verdier duality restricts to $\mathcal{T}(X)$, with compact supports ([preprint, Proposition 2.3](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/02-categories.tex#L182-L193)), and direct image along a continuous map is an exact functor compatible with duality and composition ([preprint, Proposition 2.5](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/02-categories.tex#L300-L312)).
$E_r(X) = W_r(\mathcal{T}(X))$ is Balmer's triangular Witt group for the shifted duality $[-r]\mathcal{D}_X$: a form of degree $r$ is a symmetric pairing $A \otimes A \to \omega_X[-r]$ with invertible adjoint, and on an oriented $d$-manifold the multiplication pairing of the constant sheaf is a form of degree $d$ ([preprint, §2, lines 378-403](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/02-categories.tex#L378-L403)).
The groups are symmetric Witt groups throughout; no quadratic structure appears.
Homotopic maps of test spaces induce equal maps on $E_r$, proved with a neutral form on the two ends of a cylinder ([preprint, Theorem 2.9](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/02-categories.tex#L701-L739)).

Section 3 supplies the localization theory.
Morphisms in the Verdier quotient $\mathcal{T}(X)/\mathcal{T}(U)$ are germs of sheaf morphisms near $X \setminus U$ ([preprint, Theorem 3.3](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/03-localization.tex#L165-L177)), Balmer–Walter gives a natural long exact Witt sequence for $U \subset X$ ([preprint, Theorem 3.4](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/03-localization.tex#L227-L243)), and an open cover gives only a dense inclusion of quotients ([preprint, Proposition 3.5](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/03-localization.tex#L301-L313)), whose Witt map has kernel and cokernel killed by $2$ ([preprint, Corollary 3.7](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/03-localization.tex#L462-L472)).
Section 3 serves both later stages: the germ theorem defines the character projectors ([preprint, §6, lines 216-217](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L216-L217)), the localization sequence gives the lifting isomorphism of Proposition 4.9 and its naturality ([preprint, §4, lines 518-527](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L518-L527), [§6, lines 310-315](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L310-L315)), and the localization rows together with the density bound give the lattice ([preprint, Theorem 4.3](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L136-L185)).

The category contains no group.
$G$ acts on $W^+$ and therefore on the orbit sheaf $q_*\mathbb{C}_{W^+}$ on $Y = W^+/G$, before anything is pushed into $X$ ([preprint, §6, lines 25-35](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L25-L35)); what reaches $\mathcal{T}(X)$ is one object $A_t$ with endomorphisms ([preprint, §6, lines 216-223](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L216-L223)).
Duality is used on the manifold source and on test spaces, never on the orbit space ([preprint, §6, lines 131-134](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L131-L134)).
The classes live in $\mathcal{Q}_U(X) = \mathcal{T}(X)/\mathcal{T}(U)$ for a contractible open neighbourhood $U$ of $b_t = t(y_\infty)$ ([preprint, §6, lines 101-107](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L101-L107), [lines 210-215](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L210-L215)), which can be as small as one likes, since nothing depends on it ([preprint, §6, lines 245-250](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L245-L250)).
For odd $d$ the quotient functor induces an integral isomorphism $E_d(X) \cong W_d(\mathcal{Q}_U(X))$, because $E_d(U) = 0$ and $E_{d-1}(U) \to E_{d-1}(X)$ is split injective ([preprint, Proposition 4.9](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L487-L529)): the localization sequence is used where its boundary vanishes.

## Side by side

|  | HS_proof | OpenAI's proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idea | L-theory of sequences of labelled free $\mathbb{Q}[G_i]$-modules with propagation tending to $0$; exterior quotients | Witt groups of real sheaf complexes generated by direct images of polyhedra; quotients by $\mathcal{T}(U)$ |
| Inputs | ultimate lower quadratic L-theory and Karoubi localization (Carlsson–Pedersen Theorem 4.2, Ranicki §17); rational halving (Section 5.1); support calculus (Lemma 2.2) | Verdier formalism (Schapira); triangular Witt groups (Balmer); Witt localization (Balmer–Walter Theorem 2.1); Theorems 2.9, 3.3, 3.4; Proposition 4.9 |

## What is shared

- Both groups are built from nonsingular symmetric forms modulo neutral ones in a category with duality whose objects have supports in a control space, and both have localization sequences for a decomposition of that space ([HS paper, lines 513-529](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L513-L529); [preprint, Theorem 3.4](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/03-localization.tex#L227-L243)).
- Both are covariant in the control space: HS_proof pushes labels along uniformly continuous equivariant maps ([HS paper, lines 174-176](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L174-L176)), OpenAI takes direct images ([preprint, Proposition 2.5](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/02-categories.tex#L300-L312)).
- Both discard a neighbourhood of the image of the point at infinity of the chart. HS_proof's exterior is $Z = T \times Z_0$ with $Z_0 = \{z : d(y,z) \ge R\}$, the complement of a ball around $y$: a closed neighbourhood of $\infty$ that need not be small ([HS paper, lines 831-835](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L831-L835)). OpenAI's $U$ is a contractible neighbourhood of $b_t = t(y_\infty)$, as small as one likes ([preprint, §6, lines 101-107](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L101-L107)).

## What differs

- HS_proof's objects are based modules over rational group rings, indexed by $i$ and controlled by propagation ([HS paper, lines 157-167](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L157-L167)); OpenAI's are real sheaf complexes on one space, controlled by support and Verdier duality ([preprint, Proposition 2.3](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/02-categories.tex#L182-L193)).
- HS_proof builds the finite groups $G_i$ into the category; OpenAI's category has no group in it, and $G$ acts on the orbit sheaf before the direct image ([preprint, §6, lines 25-35](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L25-L35)).
- HS_proof needs a sequence of groups, for the reason above; OpenAI's $G$ acts exactly, by homeomorphisms of $W^+$, and one finite level $m = p^k$ enters later through a partition of the characters ([preprint, §6, lines 396-402](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L396-L402)).
- HS_proof cites quadratic L-theory and computes with symmetric complexes, as above; OpenAI uses symmetric Witt groups throughout.
- HS_proof applies boundary maps $n$ times, with the exterior version and the union formula; OpenAI uses localization where it is an isomorphism ([preprint, Proposition 4.9](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L487-L529)) and inside the diagram chases of the lattice ([preprint, Theorem 4.3](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L125-L185)).
- HS_proof's excision is an equivalence of quotient categories ([HS paper, lines 193-198](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L193-L198)); OpenAI's open-cover comparison is only dense and costs a factor of $2$ on Witt groups ([preprint, Corollary 3.7](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/03-localization.tex#L462-L472)).
- OpenAI proves homotopy invariance for every homotopy of test spaces ([preprint, Theorem 2.9](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/02-categories.tex#L701-L714)); HS_proof proves a cobordism for the labels at the two ends of one fixed homotopy ([HS paper, Lemma 12.3](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1259-L1267)).
