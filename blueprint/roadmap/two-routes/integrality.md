# Reading an integer, fixed in advance

*How does each proof read a number off the invariant, and why is that number an integer, or of bounded denominator, whatever the action?*

Both contradictions compare a number produced by the action with an arithmetic constraint, so each proof needs a way to read numbers off its group that the action cannot spoil.
HS_proof reads a sequence of integer signatures, one for each finite quotient $G_i$, defined in Section 3 of the HS paper and applied once, at the end, to the class it builds over $T$.
OpenAI's proof reads one rational coefficient on the sphere, whose denominator is bounded by a power of $2$ fixed before the action enters; Section 4 of the preprint proves the bound, with the density estimate of its Section 3.
Only OpenAI's constraint has to be fixed in advance.

## HS_proof

For a finite group $G$, invariant rational forms and forms over $\mathbb{Q}[G]$ correspond through the unnormalized trace, and the coefficient of the identity recovers the invariant form, so forgetting equivariance preserves duality ([HS paper, lines 232-238](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L232-L238)).
By a half-unit comparison and a theorem of Ranicki, $L_4^p(\mathbb{Q}[G])$ is computed by Witt classes of nondegenerate $G$-invariant symmetric forms over $\mathbb{Q}$ ([HS paper, lines 238-240](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L238-L240)).
Such a form has a signature representation $[V^+_{\mathbb{C}}] - [V^-_{\mathbb{C}}]$ in the complex representation ring, built from positive and negative subspaces for an invariant inner product; it does not depend on that choice and vanishes on metabolic forms ([HS paper, lines 241-255](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L241-L255)).
Its character at the identity is the ordinary signature, an integer ([HS paper, lines 255-257](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L255-L257)); its characters at other elements carry the divisibility ([Why the symmetry forces divisibility](symmetry-forces.md)).

The asymptotic category does not have one form per class, only a sequence, so the paper extracts coordinates.
Forgetting labels maps $\mathcal{A}_{\mathbf{G}}(X)$ to $\mathcal{V}_{\mathbf{G}} = \mathcal{P}_{\mathbf{G}}/\mathcal{S}$, where $\mathcal{P}_{\mathbf{G}} = \prod_i \mathrm{Free}(\mathbb{Q}[G_i])$ (the paper's $\mathcal{P}$) and $\mathcal{S}$ is its finite-support subcategory ([HS paper, lines 271-277](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L271-L277)).
Proposition 3.2 lifts a class of $L_4(\mathcal{V}_{\mathbf{G}})$ to $L_4(\mathcal{P}_{\mathbf{G}})$ and reads its coordinates in $\prod_i L_4^p(\mathbb{Q}[G_i])$ modulo the direct sum ([HS paper, lines 279-288](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L279-L288)).
The lift needs the decorations under control: the rings $\mathbb{Q}[G_i]$ are semisimple, finite data lie in one finite block, so $\mathcal{S}$ has no negative K-theory, and the Rothenberg comparison identifies its L-groups with the direct sum ([HS paper, lines 290-298](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L290-L298)).
So any class lifts, and two lifts agree in all but finitely many coordinates ([HS paper, lines 300-306](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L300-L306)).
Applying the ordinary signature gives the tail signature $\sigma_{\mathrm{tail}} : L_4(\mathcal{A}_{\mathbf{G}}(X)) \to \mathbb{Z}^{\mathbb{N}}/\mathbb{Z}^{(\mathbb{N})}$, integer sequences modulo finitely supported ones ([HS paper, lines 309-315](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L309-L315)).

Signatures are honest integers: only the chain coefficients are rational, never the $L$-groups or the signature targets ([HS paper, lines 129-130](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L129-L130)).
The answer is a tail because morphisms are compared only eventually ([HS paper, lines 134-135](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L134-L135)), so a class fixes its coordinates only for large $i$.
The tail is read only on classes over $T$, after the exterior is quotiented away ([HS paper, line 315](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L315)): on $\Delta_T\mathfrak{d}$ in $L_4(\mathcal{A}_{\mathbf{G}}(T))$, the class made from the action and cut down by boundary maps (see [Why the class is not zero](normalization.md)), whose signatures the identity-coefficient trace matches with those of its scalar image ([HS paper, lines 1346-1349](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1346-L1349)).

Nothing needs fixing in advance for integrality.
HS_proof does fix data before the index $i$ varies: $T$, the detector and the buffers before the $K_i$ ([HS paper, lines 839-846](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L839-L846)), a cover of $T/C_p$ independent of $i$ ([HS paper, lines 350-351](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L350-L351)), and every cut and orientation of the boundary maps ([HS paper, lines 1164-1166](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L1164-L1166)).
That is control data: it keeps estimates uniform in $i$ and gives $(p\,\mathrm{id} - \tau \otimes -)^s = 0$ for one fixed $s$ (see [Why the symmetry forces divisibility](symmetry-forces.md)), while the divisor stays $p$ whatever $s$ is ([HS paper, lines 367-380](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L367-L380)).

## OpenAI's proof

At a point both test categories are the bounded derived category of finite-dimensional real vector spaces ([preprint, §2, lines 747-752](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/02-categories.tex#L747-L752)), and $E_r(\mathrm{pt})$ is $\mathbb{Z}$ for $r \equiv 0 \bmod 4$ and $0$ otherwise, by signature in degree $0$ ([preprint, Lemma 4.1](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L20-L63)).
Over a sphere, objects of $\mathcal{T}(S^d)$ come from arbitrary continuous maps and can have infinite-dimensional stalks ([preprint, §1, lines 198-201](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex#L198-L201)), so the preprint reads signatures in a smaller category and transfers the integrality ([preprint, §1, lines 207-210](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex#L207-L210)).
The smaller category is $\mathcal{P}(X)$, generated by semialgebraic maps, with Witt group $F_r(X)$ ([preprint, §2, lines 42-47](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/02-categories.tex#L42-L47), [§4, lines 4-10](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L4-L10)).
There Hardt triviality makes cohomology finite and locally constant on a dense open set, and on a small oriented ball inside it a degree-$d$ form has an integer signature on $H^0$ that respects any given finite Witt relation ([preprint, Proposition 4.6](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L262-L368)).
So the orientation class $u_{F,d}$ is nontorsion and the image of $F_d(S^d)$ in its rationalization is exactly $\mathbb{Z}\bar{u}_{F,d}$.

The transfer to arbitrary continuous maps costs powers of $2$.
Theorem 4.3 bounds kernel and cokernel of $F_r(S^j) \to E_r(S^j)$ by $2^{e_j}$, uniformly in $r$, by induction over the cover of $S^j$ by two pole complements: contractible caps, localization rows, five-term chases, and one square whose horizontal maps are dense inclusions with kernel and cokernel killed by $2$ ([preprint, Theorem 4.3](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L125-L185)).
One may take $e_j = 3(9^j - 1)/4$; a comparison after inverting $2$ would not bound denominators ([preprint, Remark 4.4](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L187-L199)).
Since $d$ is odd, $E_d(\mathrm{pt}) = 0$ and $E_d(S^d) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ is a line ([preprint, Proposition 4.5](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L203-L251)).

The fixed sphere lattice follows: $E_d(S^d) \otimes \mathbb{Q} = \mathbb{Q}\bar{u}_d$, and every integral class has rational image in $L_d^{-1}\mathbb{Z}\bar{u}_d$ with $L_d = 2^{e_d}$, because $L_d x$ comes from the semialgebraic group ([preprint, Theorem 4.7](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L370-L398)).
$L_d$ depends on nothing that comes later: action, test map, partition, prime, exponent ([preprint, §4, lines 383-385](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L383-L385)).
The order of choices is the point: $L_d$ is fixed before $k$ ([preprint, §1, line 181](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex#L181)), and the final proof chooses $p^k > 4L_d$ only afterwards ([preprint, §6, lines 457-467](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/06-character-classes.tex#L457-L467)).
"Integral" means an element of the Witt group before tensoring with $\mathbb{Q}$, and a rational isomorphism alone would lose the denominators ([preprint, §1, lines 160-161](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex#L160-L161), [lines 183-189](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex#L183-L189)).

## What is shared

- Both get their integers from ordinary signatures of finite-dimensional symmetric forms and never rationalize the groups ([HS paper, lines 129-130](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L129-L130), [lines 255-257](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L255-L257); [preprint, §1, lines 183-189](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex#L183-L189)).
- In both, signature vanishes on forms with a Lagrangian, so it is defined on the Witt group ([HS paper, lines 251-255](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L251-L255); [preprint, §4, lines 55-60](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L55-L60)).
- Both read signatures in a category where they are evident and transfer them to the category they use. HS_proof lifts a class along the quotient $\mathcal{P}_{\mathbf{G}} \to \mathcal{V}_{\mathbf{G}}$ and reads coordinates upstairs, losing only finitely many ([HS paper, lines 300-304](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L300-L304)); OpenAI maps the semialgebraic groups into the continuous ones and bounds kernel and cokernel by a power of $2$ ([preprint, §4, lines 391-397](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/04-lattice.tex#L391-L397)).

## What differs

- HS_proof gets exact integers in every coordinate, as a sequence modulo finitely supported ones; OpenAI gets one rational line with a bounded power-of-two denominator.
- HS_proof's integrality needs no advance choice; what it fixes before $i$ varies is control data. OpenAI must fix $L_d$ before $k$, because the contradiction compares $4/p^k$ with $L_d^{-1}\mathbb{Z}$ ([preprint, §1, lines 176-181](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex#L176-L181)).
- The price is paid in different places. HS_proof's excision is exact ([HS paper, lines 193-198](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L193-L198)); it pays with vanishing negative K-theory, in one short paragraph of Proposition 3.2 ([HS paper, lines 290-298](https://github.com/adbrw/HS_proof/blob/5dee3407a627f35a2cdeb0a34ac9e0f1ee98f862/HSproof.tex#L290-L298)). OpenAI pays with the open-cover density bound of Section 3 ([preprint, §3, lines 295-472](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/build/sections/03-localization.tex#L295-L472)) and the chases of Section 4; the rest of Section 3, the germ theorem and the localization sequence, also serves the character classes.
- HS_proof reads its integers in $L_4$ over $T$ after $n$ boundary maps, inside a representation ring whose other characters matter; OpenAI reads its number in degree $d$ on $S^d$, as the coefficient of $\bar{u}_d$, with no boundary maps.
