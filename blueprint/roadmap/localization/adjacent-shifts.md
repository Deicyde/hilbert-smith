# Adjacent shifts in dense subcategories

Let $\mathcal A \subset \mathcal C$ be a full replete triangulated
subcategory, dense in the sense that each object of $\mathcal C$ is a
summand of an object of $\mathcal A$. Then $y \oplus y[1] \in
\mathcal A$ and $y \oplus y[-1] \in \mathcal A$ for every $y \in
\mathcal C$. No closure of $\mathcal A$ under arbitrary summands is
required, and duality-stability of $\mathcal A$ plays no role here;
the argument uses only fullness and triangles.

Cited as the first step of the dense-doubling argument: the doubled
form and its lagrangian land in $\mathcal A$ through these
memberships.

*Proof sketch.* Write $c \simeq y \oplus z$ with $c \in \mathcal A$.
The endomorphism $0_y \oplus \mathrm{id}_z$ of $c$ lies in
$\mathcal A$ by fullness; its cone is $y \oplus y[1]$ by the
split-triangle identity, hence in $\mathcal A$. Applying this to
$y[-1]$ gives $y \oplus y[-1] \in \mathcal A$.
