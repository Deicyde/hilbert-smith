# Localization, germs, and dense subcategories

Quotient morphisms are identified with germs near the complement of
an open set, giving the Witt localization sequence. Comparing the
quotients from an open cover then yields an integral density bound:
kernel and cokernel annihilated by $2$. This bound controls the
denominators in the sphere calculation.

Throughout, $\Bcat$ is $\T$ or $\Pcat$ and $\Bzero$ its subcategory
before summands. $\Bcat(U)$ is identified with its image under
extension by zero when $U \subset X$ is open.
