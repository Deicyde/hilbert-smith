# Sum of the character classes

For a compact test $t$ and any finite partition of $\Lambda$, the
character classes satisfy the integral identity
$\sum_i z_i(t) = 2[A_t,\alpha_t]$ in $E_d(X)$.

*Proof sketch.* In the quotient, the explicit self-adjoint
involution $S = \sum_j E_jP_j$ on $A^{\oplus m}$ is an isometry
from the sum of the twisted forms to
$(A,\alpha) \perp (A,-\alpha)^{\perp(m-1)}$, whose class is
$(2-m)[A,\alpha]$; adding the $m$ untwisted terms gives
$2[A,\alpha]$. Injectivity of $\ell_U$ lifts the identity
integrally. No quotient idempotents are split.
