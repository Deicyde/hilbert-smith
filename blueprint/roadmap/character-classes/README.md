# Character classes and the contradiction

Finite partitions of the character set $\Lambda$ of $p$-power roots
of unity give orthogonal projectors on the test object away from
the point at infinity. Their germs in the quotient by a
contractible neighborhood define integral character classes,
natural under maps and homotopies of tests. Over finite quotient
sheets, a locally constant multiplier cyclically permutes the
character parts, equating their rational images; the sum formula
then forces each image to $(4/p^k)\bar u_d$, contradicting the
fixed lattice.

All sheaves and pairings are over $\R$; $\C$ denotes the real
coefficient plane with its complex scalar operations.
