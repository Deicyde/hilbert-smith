# Products with the interval

For $A \in \T(X)$ and $B \in \mathcal C_I$ (the full thick
subcategory of $\T(I)$ generated by the interval constant sheaf and
the two endpoint skyscrapers), the external product $B \boxtimes A$
belongs to $\T(I \times X)$; the same holds for $\Pcat$. For all
integers $s, r$, the natural external-duality map
$\mu_{s,r} : \D_{I,s}B \boxtimes \D_{X,r}A \to
\D_{I\times X,s+r}(B \boxtimes A)$ is an isomorphism, adjoint to the
product of evaluations. It commutes with proper direct image,
duality, and traces; in particular the product of symmetric pairings
is symmetric for the sum of the degrees.

*Proof sketch.* On generators the external-image formula gives a
compact polyhedral source; the interval triangle extends membership
to $J$, then exactness and summands to all objects. The map
$\mu_{s,r}$ is defined via the product trace and tensor--Hom
adjunction; compatibility with proper pushforward is checked by
reducing both routes to the tensor of traces. Invertibility is
verified on $H, E_0, E_1$ over $I \times \Delta$ by the simplex
duality calculation, then propagated by proper duality (even for
arbitrary continuous $f$), triangles, and summands. Symmetry follows
from naturality of the Koszul symmetry.
