# Simplex generators

In either test-category definition, closed simplices suffice in
place of all compact finite polyhedra. Every object of $\T(X)$,
respectively $\Pcat(X)$, is a direct summand of an object of
$\Tzero(X)$, respectively $\Pzero(X)$. In particular these summand
closures are triangulated and thick.

*Proof sketch.* Skeleta filtration plus the open-complement triangle
builds $\underline\R_B$ from constants on closed simplices; pushing
along $f$ proves the first assertion (all maps stay semialgebraic in
the $\Pcat$ case). Summands of a full triangulated subcategory form
a triangulated subcategory: for $u : A \to B$ between summands, a
cone on the block map $u \oplus 0$ exhibits $\Cone(u)$ as a summand.
