# Product shift sign

For $B \in \mathcal C_I$, $A \in \T(X)$ (or the semialgebraic
category), and $s, r \in \Z$, suspending the first product input
satisfies
$\gamma_{s+r}(B \boxtimes A)\,\widetilde\mu_{s,r} =
(-1)^r\mu_{s,r}^{B,A}[-1]\,\lambda$
with the paper's shift comparisons. The scalar $(-1)^r$ is
independent of $A$, its cohomological degrees, and the point of
$I$.

*Proof sketch.* Direct exponent comparison from the product
evaluation formula: the first-factor Hom comparison contributes
$(-1)^{l-s}$, the total comparison $(-1)^{l+n-s-r}$, and
suspending changes the braiding exponent by $-n$; all individual
degrees cancel leaving $r \bmod 2$.
